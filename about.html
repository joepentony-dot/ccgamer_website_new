About Cheeky Commodore Gamer

A neon love letter to the Commodore 64 and Amiga. I curate games, produce longplays, write quizzes and craft emulator-friendly guides so you can jump straight into the retro goodness.

Who’s the Cheeky one?

A joystick wobbler who grew up with CRT glow and SID bleeps. I still chase high scores, still love a chunky manual, and still think Workbench windows look gorgeous.

What you’ll find

How I build pages

Everything follows the Omega layout: responsive grids, neon C64 mode, pastel Amiga mode, zero inline CSS and clear accessibility labels so the site works on any device.

Hardware memories

Why the site exists

I wanted a home where games, videos, quizzes and emulator tips feel unified. Whether you browse on a phone or a hulking CRT, the goal is fast navigation and gorgeous retro polish.
