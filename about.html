About Cheeky Commodore Gamer

This page documents the story, motivation and long-term vision behind the Cheeky Commodore Gamer project.

Content will be expanded in later Omega stages, including personal history, featured images, community acknowledgements and curated milestones.

Project Philosophy

Cheeky Commodore Gamer exists to preserve, celebrate and explore the Commodore 64 and Amiga era through structured data, curated collections and long-form retrospectives.

What Comes Next

Future stages will introduce legacy content from earlier platforms, refined layouts, and additional visual elements — all integrated into the Omega system without regression.
