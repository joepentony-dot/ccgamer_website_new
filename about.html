About Cheeky Commodore Gamer

Welcome to the retro gaming world of Cheeky Commodore Gamer, where the golden era of home computing is celebrated through a lifelong love of the Commodore 64, the Amiga, and classic arcade games. My name’s Joe, and I’ve been joystick-deep in retro gaming since I was barely out of nappies — thanks largely to my brother Dave, who chose a Commodore 64 over the more affordable ZX Spectrum.

He saved every penny from his paper round to afford the C64 and the 1530 Datasette, loading games from cassette tapes and waiting patiently as those familiar tones filled the room. That experience shaped everything that came after.

I live in Lancashire with my wife, our children, and our Labrador, Jonathan — yes, Jonathan. Calling him in a busy park is always entertaining, and ringing the vet is even better.

The channel began during evenings when my wife worked unusual shifts. Rather than watching television, I wanted to spend that time on something I truly care about: the games themselves. Not just specs or technical details, but the feeling — the memories, the music, and the magic that stayed with us long after the screen faded to black.

Why Subscribe?

On the channel, I dive into Commodore 64 classics, Amiga gems, and the occasional arcade favourite. Expect honest takes on legendary titles, overlooked treasures, and stories from years of livestream mishaps and retro discoveries.

Whether you’re a seasoned C64 veteran, a devoted Amiga fan, or just curious about where modern gaming began, there’s something here for everyone.
