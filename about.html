Origin signal

Paper-round pennies to raster bars

My brother Dave chose a Commodore 64 over the cheaper ZX Spectrum, funding the C64 and 1530 Datasette with a paper round and endless patience. Those cassette tones and loading bars wrote the soundtrack to our living room, and they set the course for everything that followed.

The obsession survived every hardware upgrade and floppy swap. I still chase that feeling of flipping a switch, hearing the drive click, and watching sprites come alive one line at a time.

Home base

Lancashire, laughter, Labrador

I share the pixels with my wife, our kids, and Jonathan the Labrador — yes, Jonathan. Calling his name across a park always turns heads, and explaining it to the vet is a recurring gag.

Broadcast loop

Why the channel exists

When my wife’s late shifts left me with quiet evenings, I switched on the camera instead of the television. I wanted to share the stories hiding behind cartridge art and cracked intro tunes — not just specs, but the memories, the stumbles on livestreams, and the goosebumps from a perfect SID arpeggio.

Now the channel dives into Commodore 64 legends, Amiga deep cuts, and the arcade favourites that swallowed our pocket money. Expect honest takes, backstage anecdotes, and an open invitation to relive the magic together.
