About Cheeky Commodore Gamer

Cheeky Commodore Gamer is a long-running passion project dedicated to the Commodore 64 and Commodore Amiga — two machines that defined a generation of home computing and gaming.

The project began not as a technical archive, but as a way to relive the games themselves: the loading screens, the music, the moments of discovery, and the memories tied to them.

Growing up with the Commodore 64 and later the Amiga, gaming was never about frame rates or specs — it was about atmosphere, imagination, and persistence. Waiting for tapes to load, learning quirks of individual games, and sharing discoveries with friends were all part of the experience.

Cheeky Commodore Gamer exists to preserve that feeling. The YouTube channel and this site work together as a growing archive — each game given a proper place with artwork, information, videos, and trivia.

While many retro projects focus on hardware or programming, the emphasis here remains firmly on the games: what made them special, why they’re remembered, and why they still deserve attention today.

This site continues to evolve alongside the channel, with new games, collections, quizzes and features added over time. Everything is built to be explored at your own pace — no rush, no noise, just classic games and their stories.
