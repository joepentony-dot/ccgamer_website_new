Omega briefing

Four pillars of the channel

Each chapter aligns with the Omega design language: cinematic, story-driven, and always guided by C64/Amiga nostalgia.

Channel history

From tape-deck loading screens to modern capture rigs, the channel documents a lifelong love affair with Commodore hardware and the community that kept it alive.

Website mission

Build a living museum of reviews, memories, and curated collections so every visitor can rediscover a favourite or uncover a hidden gem.

Retro philosophy

Joy-first storytelling: celebrate the music, artwork, and quirks that made C64 and Amiga legends, not just the technical specs.

Future roadmap

Expand interviews, deepen the video archive, and launch new Omega-era features that keep the community involved in every update.
