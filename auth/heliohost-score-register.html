Crossfire High Score Registration

A separate HelioHost member account will be required to submit Sierra On-Line Crossfire scores. This is not your existing Cheeky Commodore Gamer website login.

Registration and verification are being prepared for release. Until HelioHost enables verified email delivery, registration requests will not be accepted. Any submitted score will still require human approval before appearing on a public leaderboard.

Use a new password of at least 14 characters. Never enter your Plesk password, administrator credentials, authenticator secret or recovery codes here.

The HelioHost service must enable verified registration before accounts can be created.

Email verification

If the service has sent you a one-use verification code, copy it into the field below. Never put the verification code in a URL, a public chat or a support message.

Codes are valid once and expire. Verification does not sign you in automatically.
