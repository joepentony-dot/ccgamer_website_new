Copyright and Takedown

Cheeky Commodore Gamer documents retro-gaming history while respecting the rights of creators, publishers, photographers, musicians and other rights holders.

Last updated: 6 August 2026Review requests welcomed

Original Cheeky Commodore Gamer material

Unless stated otherwise, original articles, reviews, commentary, video work, photographs, branding, page designs, graphics and custom code created for Cheeky Commodore Gamer are protected by copyright and other intellectual-property rights.

You may share links to public pages and quote brief extracts with attribution where permitted by law. Republishing substantial articles, copying the site design, re-uploading videos or commercially exploiting original CCG material requires written permission.

Third-party games, artwork and trademarks

Game titles, screenshots, box art, magazine imagery, logos, music, manuals, trademarks and other third-party materials remain the property of their respective owners. Their appearance on the site does not transfer ownership to Cheeky Commodore Gamer.

Such material may be used in a limited and contextual way for review, criticism, reporting, identification, historical discussion, preservation commentary or other purposes permitted by applicable law. No endorsement or official affiliation should be inferred unless expressly stated.

Download archive position

The site aims to provide or link only to material believed to be lawfully redistributable, authorised, released by its creator, public domain, freeware, abandonware with an applicable permission, or otherwise suitable for legitimate preservation access. Rights and licensing records for older software can be incomplete or disputed.

Availability is not intended to challenge a rights holder’s ownership or active commercial exploitation. A credible rights-holder notice will be reviewed and access may be restricted while the position is checked.

Requesting review or removal

Email info@cheekycommodoregamer.co.uk with the subject Copyright review request and include:

  1. your name, organisation and contact details;
  2. the exact website URL or download concerned;
  3. a clear description of the protected work;
  4. the basis on which you own or are authorised to act for the rights holder;
  5. the outcome requested, such as attribution correction, link change or removal; and
  6. any evidence that will help verify the claim.

Notices are reviewed in good faith. Additional information may be requested to prevent mistaken, fraudulent or unauthorised removals.

Corrections and permissions

Creators and rights holders are also encouraged to send corrections, licensing information, approved assets or written permission that can improve the historical record. The site may retain a private record of permissions and correspondence for accountability.

This process is intended to resolve concerns quickly and does not limit any rights or remedies available under applicable law.
