The essentials

What is THEA1200?

THEA1200 is a modern recreation inspired by the Commodore Amiga 1200 rather than an original 1990s machine. Retro Games says the system emulates the A1200 and its AGA graphics architecture, while also supporting A500-era OCS and ECS modes. It is designed to connect to a modern display through HDMI and includes USB connectivity.

The full-size working keyboard is a major difference from THEA500 Mini. Workbench is included, giving the machine a desktop environment as well as the game carousel.

Built-in library

Which games are included with THEA1200?

Retro Games says THEA1200 contains 25 classic Amiga games. Confirmed examples include Defender of the Crown and Defender of the Crown II, The Settlers II, the Turrican trilogy, Beneath a Steel Sky, Lure of the Temptress and Ruff 'n' Tumble.

That mix gives the machine a broader spread than a simple A1200 showcase, with games from several parts of the Amiga era.
