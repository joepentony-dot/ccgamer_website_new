About this video

This Retro Special goes through Zzap!64’s first full calendar year and revisits every Commodore 64 game that received either a Gold Medal or Sizzler award in 1986. From Uridium, Mercenary, Leaderboard and Ghosts ’n Goblins to World Games, The Sentinel, Thrust, Kikstart II, International Karate, Sanxion, Spindizzy, Cauldron II, Alter Ego, Ultima IV, Green Beret, Ballblazer, Koronis Rift, The Eidolon, Revs and more, it looks at which awards still feel fully deserved today and which choices remain open for debate.
