About this feature

Explore fifty Commodore 64 SID music selections through Zzap!64's reviews, presented chronologically from 1985 to 1991. Hear one-minute sections featuring Rob Hubbard, Martin Galway, Ben Daglish, Fred Gray, Tim Follin, Jeroen Tel, Chris Hülsbeck and many other composers. These are selections, not a ranking: the magazine's sound percentages assessed effects and music together. The original SID audio in the Sanxion and Last Ninja 2 segments was replaced following copyright claims; the chapter guide clearly marks both substitutions.

Commodore 64 SID music through Zzap!64, 1985–1991

From Rob Hubbard’s Monty on the Run and Commando to Martin Galway’s Wizball, Ben Daglish’s The Last Ninja and Jeroen Tel’s Cybernoid II, this chronological documentary revisits fifty tunes associated with Zzap!64’s original game reviews.

The fifty selections span 1985 to 1991. Each has a short spoken introduction and approximately one minute of music. They are not ranked by score: Zzap!64’s sound ratings assessed sound effects and music together, rather than assigning separate music marks.

Copyright note: the original SID music in the Sanxion and Last Ninja 2 segments could not be used following copyright claims, so substitute audio is heard instead. The original compositions remain credited for historical context; Last Ninja 3 is not one of the replaced tracks.
