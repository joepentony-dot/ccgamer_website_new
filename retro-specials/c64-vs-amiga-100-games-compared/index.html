About this feature

One hundred games that appeared on both the Commodore 64 and Amiga, shown in 30-second comparisons. Watch 7.5 seconds of C64 gameplay, then 7.5 seconds of Amiga gameplay, compare the two versions side by side and predict which received the higher separate reader average on Lemon64 or Lemon Amiga. The results appear during the final three seconds. Give yourself one point for each correct C64, Amiga or draw prediction and compare your own score out of 100. Machine standings update after every ten games. These are not direct head-to-head review scores: each community rated its version independently, and reader averages can change over time.

C64 vs Amiga: 100 classic games, 100 comparisons

This showcase revisits Commodore 64 and Commodore Amiga editions of familiar games, from Crazy Cars and Spy vs Spy through R-Type, Bubble Bobble and Turrican II to Pirates!. See how each title appeared on two generations of Commodore hardware, then find out which edition received the higher community rating.

The higher-rated version earns one machine point, while equal scores count as a draw. Lemon community scores are snapshots recorded during production and may change as new votes arrive. They do not settle which computer or conversion is objectively better.
