About this feature

This Retro Special revisits all 55 games that received a Zzap!64 award during 1988, including Commodore 64 Sizzlers, Gold Medals and Silver Medals alongside the magazine’s expanding Amiga and 16-bit coverage. Featuring games such as Tetris, Platoon, Hunter’s Moon, Hawkeye, Armalyte, Last Ninja 2, Starglider, Rocket Ranger, Fish and Bombuzal, it follows the awards month by month and looks back at a major year of change for Zzap!64.
