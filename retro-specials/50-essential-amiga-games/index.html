About this video

This countdown explores 50 essential Amiga games across multiple genres, from arcade action and strategy to adventure and simulation. Featuring classics such as Lotus Turbo Challenge 2, Cannon Fodder, Syndicate, Another World and The Secret of Monkey Island, this video highlights why the Amiga remains one of the most beloved gaming platforms of all time.
