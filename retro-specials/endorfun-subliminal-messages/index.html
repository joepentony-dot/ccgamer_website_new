About this feature

This Retro Special revisits Endorfun, the 1995 PC and Macintosh puzzle game developed by Onesong Partners and published by Time Warner Interactive. Behind its colourful cube-matching puzzles and world-music soundtrack were around 100 positive affirmations, some audible and others mixed much further into the audio, prompting contemporary debate about subliminal messaging in computer software. The feature explores Endorfun’s gameplay, the press controversy, Timothy Leary’s connection, Microsoft’s consideration of a Windows 95 sample, television coverage including Bad Influence!, and the Japanese game Cu-On-Pa, while asking whether there was a worthwhile puzzle game underneath the publicity.
