Friday the 13th (1986) Commodore 64 – Can You Solve the Forgotten Horror Sound Quiz?

Domark's Friday the 13th for the C64 had more than Jason Voorhees lurking on the cassette. Hidden among the original release's extras was a grisly sound-effects competition. Listen to ten mystery sounds and see how many you can identify.

The Friday the 13th Commodore 64 cassette competition

Released by Domark in 1986, Friday the 13th for the Commodore 64 included a promotional audio challenge using ten grisly sound effects supplied by BBC Enterprises. Players had to match the noises to descriptions including “Jason has a meaty meal” and “Madam La Guillotine makes her mark”. Five Eureka colour monitors were offered as prizes.

The original competition is long over, but the sounds make a rather entertaining C64 horror quiz today. Play the video, note your answers and see whether you'd have stood a chance of winning one of those monitors back in 1986.
