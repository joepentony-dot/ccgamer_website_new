The SID Chip – Commodore 64 Sound Interface Device & 8-Bit Music Revolution

A history feature on the Commodore 64 SID chip and its lasting role in 8-bit game music.

This Retro Special explains why the SID chip became one of the defining sounds of home computer gaming. It explores the chip’s musical identity and why SID-driven tracks still matter to retro audiences today.
