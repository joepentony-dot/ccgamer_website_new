About this feature

This Retro Special goes back through Zzap!64’s first year and revisits every Commodore 64 game that received either a Gold Medal or Sizzler award between May and December 1985. From Elite, Dropzone and Paradroid to Way of the Exploding Fist, Frankie Goes to Hollywood, Little Computer People and Electrosound, it looks at which awards still feel justified today and which choices feel very tied to 1985.
