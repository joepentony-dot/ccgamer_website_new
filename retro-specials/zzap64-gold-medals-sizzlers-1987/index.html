About this feature

A month-by-month look at every Commodore 64 game that won a Zzap!64 Gold Medal, Sizzler or Silver Medal in 1987. With Gold Medals becoming rarer, Sizzlers doing much of the work, and two unusual Silver Medal entries, 1987 is one of Zzap!64’s more interesting C64 award years. Featuring The Bard’s Tale, Gauntlet, Wizball, Head Over Heels, The Last Ninja, California Games, Bubble Bobble, IK+, Maniac Mansion, SEUCK, Nebulus and more.
