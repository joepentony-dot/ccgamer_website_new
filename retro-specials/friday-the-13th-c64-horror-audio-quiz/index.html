Friday the 13th on the Commodore 64: the forgotten 1986 sound effects competition

Domark’s Friday the 13th: The Computer Game arrived on the Commodore 64 in 1986, bringing Jason Voorhees and Camp Crystal Lake to home computers. But the original cassette offered more than the game: it also included a horror sound effects competition using recordings supplied by BBC Enterprises.

Players were asked to identify ten gruesome noises and match each one to a description from the competition entry form. The first five correct entries stood to win a Eureka colour monitor. The packaging was memorable too, with two novelty blood capsules included in the original release.

This Cheeky Commodore Gamer video revisits the original C64 cassette audio as a standalone ten-sound quiz. Listen closely and see whether you can work out clues such as “Jason has a meaty meal” and “Madam La Guillotine makes her mark”. You will need your ears, a little imagination and a reasonably strong stomach.

The original competition is long closed, but you can still take the challenge. Watch the Friday the 13th C64 quiz on YouTube, then leave your score in the video comments. Did you recognise all ten sound effects, and do you remember the original cassette release?

What was the Friday the 13th C64 horror audio quiz?

It was a ten-sound cassette competition bundled with the original Domark editions of Friday the 13th. Players matched audio effects to printed captions for a chance to win one of five Eureka colour monitors. This video lets C64 fans revisit those sounds without revealing the answers in advance.
