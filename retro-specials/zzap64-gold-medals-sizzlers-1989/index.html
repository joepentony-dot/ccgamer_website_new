About this feature

This Retro Special revisits all 70 games recognised by Zzap!64 during 1989, following 79 separate awards month by month across the Commodore 64 and Amiga. With 62 Sizzlers, 9 Silver Medals and 8 Gold Medals, the video looks back at major releases, budget reissues, games where the C64 outscored the Amiga and several ratings still open for debate. It also follows a year of changing staff, changing presentation and increasingly prominent Amiga coverage in the magazine.
