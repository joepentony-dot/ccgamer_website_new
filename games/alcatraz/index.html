Alcatraz

Alcatraz is an Amiga action-adventure game released in 1992. The story centres on a drug baron who has seized control of Alcatraz prison, with the player cast as a U.S. Navy SEAL sent in to stop him. The game blends arcade combat with exploration elements and is remembered for its gritty premise...
