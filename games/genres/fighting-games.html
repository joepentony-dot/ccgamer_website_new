Fighting Games

Fighting games on the Commodore 64 and Amiga cover everything from one-on-one arena battles to side-scrolling brawlers and wrestling spectaculars. They are built around timing, reaction and learning how to use each move, combo or throw to its full advantage.

This section highlights games where close combat is the main event. Whether it is martial arts, fantasy duels, wrestling rings or street battles, the focus is on head-to-head clashes and satisfying hits.

Fighting Beat-'em-ups Versus

What makes a Fighting game?

Games listed under this Fighting category usually revolve around direct physical combat between a small number of characters. Matches may take place on a single screen or across scrolling arenas, but your main goal is to wear down an opponent's energy, score knockouts or outlast them on points.

Expect distinctive fighters with different strengths and weaknesses, special moves, grapples and attacks that reward practice and mastery rather than simple button bashing.
