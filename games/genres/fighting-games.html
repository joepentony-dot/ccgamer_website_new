Fighting Games

Fighting games span martial arts duels, weapon-based combat, wrestling simulators, brawlers and arena battles. The Commodore 64 and Amiga delivered everything from simple one-button fighters to ambitious arcade conversions.

This genre is all about timing, reflex, style and competition — whether battling AI or a friend.
