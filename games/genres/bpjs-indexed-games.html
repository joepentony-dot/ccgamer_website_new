BPjS-Indexed Games

This section highlights games that found themselves on the BPjS index in Germany, usually for violence or controversial themes. On the C64 and Amiga these titles often built a certain reputation precisely because they were restricted.

Presented here from a historical perspective, these games are part of gaming culture and censorship history, and show how attitudes to content have changed over the years.
