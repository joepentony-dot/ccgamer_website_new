BPjS-Indexed Games

This page documents games that were historically listed by the BPjS in Germany (Bundesprüfstelle für jugendgefährdende Schriften) during the classic home computer era. It exists as an archival reference for collectors, historians and retro fans who are interested in how certain titles were handled at the time.

The focus is on factual, neutral information. No downloads are provided from this page, and the presence of a game here should be read as documentation of history rather than a statement about its content.

Historical Reference BPjS Commodore Era

Understanding the BPjS context

The BPjS list affected how some games were advertised, sold and discussed in Germany. For retro enthusiasts it has become part of the wider story of how computer games were perceived in the eighties and early nineties.

This overview is designed to help you identify which C64 and Amiga titles appeared on those lists and to give you a starting point for further research using independent sources.
