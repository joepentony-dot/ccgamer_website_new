BPjS-Indexed Games

Historically restricted titles from Germany’s Bundesprüfstelle, preserved here for context.

These games were placed on the BPjS index in Germany, often due to violence or themes considered controversial at the time. While restricted upon release, they remain important pieces of gaming culture and censorship history.

This section aims to document them respectfully and accurately as part of retro history.
