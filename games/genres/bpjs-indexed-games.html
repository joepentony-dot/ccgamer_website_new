BPjS Indexed Games

The BPjS (Bundesprüfstelle für jugendgefährdende Schriften) was a German authority that monitored and indexed media considered unsuitable for young audiences. A number of Commodore 64 and Amiga titles were placed on these lists for reasons ranging from violence to dark themes.

This section archives those historically restricted games for educational and preservation purposes.
