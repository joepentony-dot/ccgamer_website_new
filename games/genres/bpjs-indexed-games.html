BPJS Indexed Games

These games were indexed, restricted or banned in Germany by the BPjS for being potentially harmful to young people. Today they serve as a snapshot of how games, violence and censorship were viewed in the 80s and 90s.

This collection is presented for preservation and historical interest, highlighting some of the most controversial titles in the C64 and Amiga libraries.
