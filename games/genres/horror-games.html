Horror Games

Horror games on the Commodore 64 and Amiga lean into spooky settings, monsters, dark storylines and unsettling atmosphere. Even with limited graphics, developers used clever sound design and imagination to deliver genuine chills on a home computer.

This section gathers titles featuring haunted houses, vampires, zombies, cursed artefacts and other horror themes, whether they play as adventures, action games or hybrids.

Horror Monsters Atmospheric

Horror on classic micros

Many Horror games rely on mood and suggestion rather than explicit visuals. Flickering lights, eerie music and tense exploration all help create a sense of dread, even when you are viewing everything through chunky sprites and bold colours.

Games in this category are a great pick if you want something a little darker than the usual platformers and arcade conversions, especially around Halloween or late-night sessions.
