Strategy Games

Strategy games focus on planning, resource management and long-term decisions rather than quick reflexes. On the Commodore 64 and Amiga that includes war simulations, economic management titles, tactical squad games and conversions of classic board and tabletop systems.

This section is ideal for players who enjoy thinking several turns ahead, weighing risk versus reward and gradually building up an advantage over time.

Strategy Tactics Simulation

Plan, adapt and out-think the opposition

Strategy titles might play out in real time or in clear turns, but they almost always reward careful analysis of the situation on screen. Positioning units, managing resources, negotiating territories and choosing when to attack or defend are all central to success.

If you are in the mood for slower, more thoughtful sessions rather than pure twitch action, this category collects the C64 and Amiga games that put strategy first.
