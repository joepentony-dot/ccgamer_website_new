Miscellaneous Games

Not every Commodore 64 or Amiga title fits neatly into a single box. The Miscellaneous category is where you will find hybrids, oddball experiments, multi-genre curiosities and games that simply refuse to behave like anything else in the collection.

This is a great place to browse when you want something different, whether that means unusual controls, quirky themes or mechanics that did not really catch on anywhere else.

Miscellaneous Hybrid Unusual

Games that bend the rules

Some games blend several genres at once, while others are tied to very specific hobbies or interests that do not appear frequently enough to justify a dedicated category. Rather than hide them away, they are grouped here so you can dig through the more experimental side of the library.

If you enjoy discovering hidden gems, strange crossovers and titles that feel like prototypes for ideas that would be refined later, this section is well worth exploring.
