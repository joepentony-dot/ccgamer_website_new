Action Adventure Games

Action Adventure games blend fast-paced combat with exploration, light puzzle-solving and a strong sense of atmosphere. On the Commodore 64 and Amiga this meant epic quests, cinematic intros, moody soundtracks and huge maps to uncover one screen at a time.

This page focuses on titles where you are not just shooting or jumping, but also travelling through story locations, collecting items, unlocking new areas and battling memorable enemies along the way.

Action Adventure Commodore 64 Amiga

What counts as Action Adventure?

Games listed here usually mix real-time action with a clear narrative or world structure. That can include dungeon crawls, multi-screen quests, cinematic movie tie-ins and games that reward you for exploring every corner of the map rather than just chasing a score.

If a title has a defined storyline, a character on a journey and a world worth exploring, it is very likely to have landed in this Action Adventure category.
