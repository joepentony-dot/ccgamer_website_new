Racing Games

Racing games put you behind the wheel or handlebars and ask you to chase lap times, positions and checkpoints. On the Commodore 64 and Amiga this covers everything from simple overhead racers to pseudo-3D road burners and serious attempts at motorsport simulation.

Cars, bikes, futuristic craft and even more unusual vehicles all make an appearance, giving you a wide range of ways to put your reflexes and racing lines to the test.

Racing Driving Motorsport

From arcade sprints to simulations

Some Racing games focus on pure arcade thrills, with forgiving handling and dramatic crashes, while others ask you to manage gears, braking points and careful overtakes. Time limits, championship modes and multiplayer options all add extra replay value.

Whether you prefer casual laps around a track or mastering every corner of a full season, this category collects the C64 and Amiga racing experiences in one place.
