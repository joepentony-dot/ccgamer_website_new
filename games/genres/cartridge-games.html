Cartridge Games

Cartridge games were the premium end of the Commodore 64 experience. Plug in, power on and you were at the title screen in seconds, with no tape loading and no disk swapping to slow down your play sessions.

This section highlights single-game carts, compilation cartridges and special hardware releases that made the most of the format, whether that meant faster loading, built-in cheats or extra options.

Cartridge C64 Quick Load

Why cartridge games matter

With tight memory limits and instant replays, cartridge titles often focused on punchy arcade-style designs, polished front ends and fast restarts. They were ideal for score chasing and short sessions, and many have become prized collector's items.

This gallery lets you browse those releases together, making it easier to spot which carts you remember and which ones you might want to try in emulation or on modern hardware.
