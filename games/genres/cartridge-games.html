Cartridge Games

Cartridge games remove the waiting and drop you straight into the action. On the C64 and Amiga, cartridges meant instant loading, built-in menus and sometimes enhanced features over the tape or disk versions.

This section gathers fast-start arcade conversions, special editions and other plug-in releases that gave your 8-bit or 16-bit system a console feel.
