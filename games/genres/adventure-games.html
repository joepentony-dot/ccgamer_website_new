Adventure Games

Adventure games were where home computers really stretched their storytelling muscles. On the C64 and Amiga that meant text adventures, icon-driven graphic adventures and point-and-click epics that could easily steal whole weekends as you tried to solve one more puzzle.

If you enjoy talking to every character, examining every item and keeping a notepad by your side, this is the section to get lost in.

Pure Adventure experiences

Titles listed under Adventure tend to put story and puzzle-solving ahead of reflex-based action. Combat might appear, but the core of the experience usually lives in exploration, conversation, inventory management and figuring out the logic of the game world.

Expect parser-based adventures, menu-driven systems and later mouse-controlled games that defined computer storytelling for a generation.
