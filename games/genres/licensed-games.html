Licensed Games

Licensed games were a huge part of the Commodore 64 and Amiga library. Publishers secured rights to films, TV shows, comic-book heroes, sports stars and arcade brands, then tried to squeeze the essence of those properties into 8-bit and 16-bit form.

This page collects games that carry an official licence, from big blockbuster movie tie-ins through to more niche television and cartoon adaptations.

Licensed Movie Tie-ins TV & Comics

Film, TV and brand tie-ins

Licensed games could be anything from quick marketing cash-ins to genuinely creative reworks of on-screen stories. Some follow the source material closely, recreating key scenes, while others use the characters and logo as a starting point for entirely new adventures.

Browsing this section is a great way to rediscover the era when every cinema trip or cartoon favourite seemed to have a matching C64 or Amiga release on the shelves.
