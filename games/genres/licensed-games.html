Licensed Games

Licensed games bring films, TV shows, comics and famous faces onto your home computer. On the C64 and Amiga that meant everything from Hollywood blockbusters to British game show tie-ins.

This section gathers the best and most interesting licences, whether they nailed the source material or went in a wonderfully odd direction of their own.
