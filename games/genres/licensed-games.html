Licensed Games

Licensed games are built on blockbuster movies, animated shows, comic book heroes and pop-culture icons. On the Commodore 64 and Amiga, these were some of the most highly-anticipated releases.

This genre showcases everything from superhero adventures and action films to quirky entertainment tie-ins.
