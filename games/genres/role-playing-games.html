RPG Games

Role-playing games on the Commodore 64 and Amiga invite you to build characters, explore worlds and make decisions that shape your journey. That can mean first-person dungeon crawls, overhead party adventures or hybrid action RPGs with real-time combat layered over character stats.

This section shines a light on games where levelling up, inventory management and long-term quests are central to the experience.

RPG Dungeon Crawler Fantasy

Stats, stories and progression

RPG games typically allow you to improve your character or party over time, gaining new abilities, spells and equipment as you defeat enemies and complete objectives. Choices about where to go, which quests to tackle and how to build your party are a big part of the appeal.

If you enjoy slow-burn adventures that can last for many sessions, this category brings together the C64 and Amiga titles that offer exactly that kind of long-form progression.
