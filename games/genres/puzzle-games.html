Puzzle Games

Puzzle games challenge your brain more than your reflexes. On the Commodore 64 and Amiga that means sliding block puzzles, matching games, falling shapes, logic conundrums and score-based thinking challenges that keep you coming back for one more go.

This section is ideal for players who like to plan several moves ahead, search for patterns and experiment with different strategies until everything finally clicks into place.

Puzzle Logic Brain-Teasers

Think before you move

Many Puzzle games start simply and then quietly ramp up the difficulty as new rules, block types or level layouts are introduced. A single mistake can cost you the round, but clever planning can lead to huge chains and very satisfying clear screens.

Whether you enjoy relaxed problem-solving or intense high-speed puzzlers, this category pulls together a wide range of C64 and Amiga titles built around thought rather than pure action.
