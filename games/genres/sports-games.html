Sports Games

Sports games bring stadiums, arenas and tracks to the Commodore 64 and Amiga. Football, tennis, athletics, boxing, winter sports and multi-event competitions all feature, with controllers taking the place of joysticks, bats and skis.

This section spans everything from arcade-style interpretations to more detailed simulations, often with multiplayer options that make them ideal for friendly rivalries.

Sports Football Events

From single events to full tournaments

Some Sports titles focus on a single discipline, such as football or tennis, while others offer multi-event packages where you compete across a series of challenges to chase medals or trophies.

Button-mashing sprints, carefully timed jumps, tactical passes and precision shots all live here, giving you plenty of ways to enjoy a bit of virtual exercise on classic hardware.
