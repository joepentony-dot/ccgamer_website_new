Platform Games

Platform games are all about timing, jumping and mastering the layout of each stage. Ladders, ropes, moving platforms and deadly drops are standard fare on the C64 and Amiga.

From single-screen challenges to sprawling platform epics, this genre showcases some of the most iconic characters and level designs in retro gaming history.
