Platform Games

Platform games are all about timing, jumping and mastering each screen. Ladders, ropes, moving platforms and deadly drops are standard fare on the C64 and Amiga.

From tight single-screen challenges to sprawling multi-stage adventures, this genre showcases some of the most iconic characters and level designs in retro gaming.
