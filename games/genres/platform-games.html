Platform Games

Platform games are built around running, jumping and climbing through hazard-filled levels. On the Commodore 64 and Amiga that meant ladders, moving platforms, disappearing ledges, enemies on fixed patrol routes and tricky leaps that demanded precise timing.

This section covers both single-screen platformers and scrolling adventures, from early ladder-climbing classics to more advanced multi-level epics.

Platform Jump & Run Side-Scrolling

Precision jumps and tricky layouts

Platform games usually reward pattern recognition and muscle memory. You will often need to learn enemy paths, time moving platforms and plan the safest way through each stage while scooping up collectables, rescuing characters or chasing a high score.

If you grew up on jumping across gaps and climbing endless ladders, this category is a direct hit of nostalgia and a great way to test your reflexes again.
