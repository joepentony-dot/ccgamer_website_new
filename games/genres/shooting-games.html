Shooting Games

Shooting games cover a wide range of blasters on the Commodore 64 and Amiga, from scrolling space shooters and helicopter assaults to run-and-gun platformers and static gallery-style challenges. The common thread is fast reactions and plenty of on-screen projectiles.

This section is ideal when you want to blow things up, dodge incoming fire and chase high scores with power-ups, extra weapons and big boss encounters.

Shooter Run-and-Gun Arcade Action

Blasting through waves of enemies

Shooting games tend to ramp up the intensity quickly, throwing more enemies, bullets and obstacles at you as each level progresses. Learning enemy patterns, using power-ups wisely and staying calm under pressure are all key to survival.

If you enjoy pure action with immediate feedback and dramatic explosions, this category pulls together the C64 and Amiga games that focus on exactly that experience.
