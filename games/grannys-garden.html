Game Summary

Granny's Garden is a Commodore 64 educational adventure and puzzle game released in 1987 by Satchel Software. Designed for schools, it mixes logic puzzles, riddles, and simple navigation with fairy-tale presentation, focusing on problem-solving and memory rather than reflex-based gameplay.
