      Objectives & Rulebook

      One route, three clear steps. Optional rooms make you stronger; they never block the exit.

      ◎

      1 · COMPLETE THE HUD GOAL

      Keys, generators, rescue, exploration or guardian. The five-minute reminder always shows what comes next.

      ◇

      2 · CLEAR THE SIGIL CHAMBER

      Enter the opened gate. It locks and summons two named enemies. Defeat every defender, then collect the Exit Sigil.

      ⇥

      3 · REACH THE EXIT

      Follow the radar marker back to the floor exit. The Sigil opens the route to the next floor.

      ✦

      DEATH STALKER

      Exactly one can appear on each floor. It can open ordinary room doors, and normal weapons only knock it back. Trade 3 artefacts or pay 10,000 score at a shop for a Banishment Flask, then press B nearby. Permanent banishment awards 15,000 score and 250 XP, and drops a Banishment Artefact with its existing reward choice.

      ▣

      DOORS & SECRETS

      Enemies stay clear of unopened room entrances and cannot shoot into the waiting corridor. Once you enter, they move normally. Cracked walls hide secrets; 5% of secret rooms conceal another cracked pocket.

      ★

      LOOT, AMMO & XP

      Pickups give 1–10 XP, one-time openings such as hidden walls, locked doors, chests, switches and puzzle vaults give 10 XP, ordinary enemies give 100 XP and named enemies give 250 XP. Entering rooms, repeatable doors and objectives give no XP. The first upgrade requires 1,425 XP.

      ⚠

      DEATH & WEEKLY VAULT

      Normal death moves half your score, the XP penalty and carried loot into one recoverable cache. Recover it before dying again; a second death permanently erases the old cache. You can lose at most one level and your most recent upgrade. Weekly Vault death ends that week’s attempt.

      ?

      ENEMY TACTICS & OPTIONAL CHALLENGES

      Enemies can reposition, flank and hide behind solid furniture. Tougher enemies assess cover more often and re-plan faster, but smarter tactics do not grant extra speed or damage. Clues, memory tiles, torch sequences, arenas, boulders, bridges and secret rooms offer extra score and stronger loot.
