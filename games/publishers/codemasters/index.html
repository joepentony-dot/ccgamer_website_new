About this publisher

Codemasters was founded in the 1980s by brothers David and Richard Darling. Its early output included sports games such as BMX Simulator for the Commodore 64, and the company first built its business around affordable releases for 8-bit home computers.

Documented company facts

  • Founded in the 1980s by David and Richard Darling.
  • BMX Simulator for the Commodore 64 was among its early sports titles.
  • Its early catalogue concentrated on budget-priced 8-bit games before later expansion.
