About this publisher

Alternative Software became a substantial British budget and re-release publisher across the 8-bit home-computer market. The company remains registered in West Yorkshire and the museum record preserves a large catalogue of Alternative releases.

Documented company facts

  • Companies House records Alternative Software Limited as incorporated on 4 June 1986, with its registered office in Castleford, West Yorkshire.
  • The Centre for Computing History catalogues 85 games under Alternative Software, including numerous Commodore 64 releases and later licensed titles.

Archive strengths
