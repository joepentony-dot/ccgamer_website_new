Publisher history

Elite: Publisher History

Elite Systems was incorporated in England in 1984 by Brian Wilcox and Steve Wilcox. It began as a developer and publisher for the ZX Spectrum and Commodore 64 before expanding to other computers and consoles.

Elite became particularly associated with licensed conversions of arcade games and television properties. Its catalogue included home versions of major coin-op titles, while the company later moved into handheld and mobile development.
