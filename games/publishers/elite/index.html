About this publisher

Elite Systems Limited was incorporated in England on 14 August 1984. The company began as a developer and publisher for the ZX Spectrum and Commodore 64, and its early licensing work included Airwolf before a wider run of licensed and arcade-derived releases.

Documented company facts

  • Elite Systems Limited was incorporated on 14 August 1984.
  • It began as a developer and publisher for the ZX Spectrum and Commodore 64.
  • Its 1984 licensing activity included an official game based on the television series Airwolf.
