About this publisher

Kixx was a major budget re-release label in the UK computer-games market. Its catalogue brought older titles back at lower prices across Commodore 64, Amiga, Spectrum, Atari ST and other formats, including many games previously released through U.S. Gold and other publishers.

Documented company facts

  • The Centre for Computing History identifies Kixx editions such as Indiana Jones and the Temple of Doom as re-releases of earlier U.S. Gold games.
  • The museum's Kixx catalogue includes Commodore 64 and Amiga reissues across racing, shooting, sports, platform and arcade-derived games.
