About this publisher

Gremlin Graphics developed and published a broad range of British computer games, including platformers, sports, racing and character-led action titles across the C64 and Amiga years.

Documented company facts

  • The Gremlin Graphics Archive presents an officially endorsed history of the Sheffield-based developer and publisher.
  • The Centre for Computing History catalogues 82 preserved game records carrying Gremlin Graphics publishing credits across multiple formats.
