About this publisher

Cascade Games was a British software publisher incorporated in 1983. The company became known early for the multi-game Cassette 50 compilation and later published titles including the ACE flight-combat series before changing its registered name to Artronic Products Limited.

Documented company facts

  • Companies House records the company as incorporated on 23 September 1983 under the name Cascade Games Limited and renamed Artronic Products Limited on 21 November 1988.
  • The Centre for Computing History records Cascade's Cassette 50 on the Commodore 64 and several other home computers in 1983.
  • The museum collection also documents later Cascade releases including ACE, Sky Runner and ACE 2.
