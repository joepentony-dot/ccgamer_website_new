About this publisher

Microdeal published a very large multi-format catalogue, with particularly deep roots in Dragon 32 software before expanding into later Commodore and Amiga releases.

Documented company facts

  • The Centre for Computing History preserves 131 games under the Microdeal publishing credit.
  • The museum records twelve Commodore games and seven Commodore Amiga games under Microdeal.
  • Its Amiga catalogue includes Goldrunner, Goldrunner II, Insanity Fight, International Soccer and The Karate Kid Part II.

Archive strengths

  • Dragon 32 software
  • Amiga games
  • Large multi-format catalogue

Evidence sources
