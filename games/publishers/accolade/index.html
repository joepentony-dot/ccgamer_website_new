About this publisher

Accolade was created in 1984 by former Activision founders Alan Miller and Bob Whitehead. The company concentrated on personal-computer and console entertainment, with early Commodore 64 releases including Law of the West.

Documented company facts

  • Alan Miller said that he and Bob Whitehead left Activision in late 1984 to start Accolade.
  • Miller said the founders funded Accolade themselves after being unable to attract outside investment and that the company became profitable after its first year of development.
  • Miller designed Law of the West for the Commodore 64 before moving fully into management at Accolade.
