About this publisher

Melbourne House published games, programming books and other software for home computers. Its Commodore 64 catalogue includes The Hobbit, The Way of the Exploding Fist, Fist II and J.R.R. Tolkien's War in Middle Earth.

Documented company facts

  • The Centre for Computing History preserves more than sixty games under the Melbourne House publishing credit.
  • Its museum collection contains fifteen Commodore 64 games, including The Hobbit and The Way of the Exploding Fist.
  • The museum also preserves Melbourne House programming and reference books written specifically for Commodore computers.
