About this publisher

Infogrames published a wide international catalogue spanning home computers and later consoles. Its Commodore-era releases included Hostages and North & South, while many titles were developed by external studios.

Documented company facts

  • The Centre for Computing History preserves eighty games under the Infogrames publishing credit across numerous computer and console platforms.
  • Its Commodore 64 collection includes The Sydney Affair, Hostages and North & South.
  • The museum records external developers such as Probe Software on several Infogrames-published home-computer releases.

Archive strengths

  • European publishing
  • Strategy and action games
  • Multi-platform catalogue
