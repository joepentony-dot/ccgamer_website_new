About this publisher

Quicksilva was an important early British software publisher whose broad 8-bit catalogue included experimental games, arcade-style releases and several notable Commodore 64 titles.

Documented company facts

  • The Centre for Computing History preserves forty games under the Quicksilva publishing credit.
  • Its Commodore records include Aquaplane, Booga-Boo (The Flea), Purple Turtles, Rupert and the Toymaker's Party and Pac-Land.
