About this publisher

Virgin Games published and distributed a varied computer-game catalogue across the 1980s and 1990s, spanning original titles, conversions and releases from associated studios and labels.

  • The Centre for Computing History preserves 64 game records published under the Virgin Games name across multiple computer and console formats.
