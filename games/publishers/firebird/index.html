About this publisher

Firebird was created in 1984 by Telecomsoft, British Telecom's software division. The label published games for the Commodore 64 and other home computers, using both budget and premium ranges while Telecomsoft explored software publishing alongside early ideas for network-delivered content.

Documented company facts

  • Firebird was created by Telecomsoft in 1984.
  • The label published for the Commodore 64, ZX Spectrum and other systems.
  • Firebird used both premium and budget ranges rather than serving only one price band.

Archive strengths

  • Budget and full-price ranges
  • C64 arcade and adventure games
  • Distinctive label identity
