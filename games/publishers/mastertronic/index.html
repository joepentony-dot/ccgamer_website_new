Publisher history

Mastertronic: Publisher History

The business that became Mastertronic was formed in London at the end of 1983 and began trading in 1984. Its central idea was to sell computer games at budget prices, with the best-known early range retailing at £1.99.

Mastertronic used an unusually broad distribution network that placed games in newsagents, service stations and other outlets as well as specialist shops. The company expanded through labels including M.A.D. and later merged with Virgin, while also becoming closely involved in Sega's European distribution.
