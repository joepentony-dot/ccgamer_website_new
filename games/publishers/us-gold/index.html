About this publisher

US Gold brought a substantial number of American and arcade properties to European home-computer owners. Its catalogue spans sports, action games, conversions and licensed releases across both C64 and Amiga.

Documented company facts

  • The Play It Again archive records that Geoff and Anne Brown founded U.S. Gold in 1984 to publish American games in the United Kingdom and arrange conversions for British home computers.
