Publisher history

Activision: Publisher History

Activision was established in California in 1979 by former Atari programmers and became an important early independent publisher of games made for another company's hardware.

Its Commodore 64 catalogue combined original computer releases with conversions and licensed publishing. The Activision name continued through several corporate changes and later became part of Activision Blizzard and Microsoft.
