About this publisher

Activision was founded in 1979 and developed, published and distributed interactive entertainment. Its CCG Commodore archive reflects only part of a much wider catalogue and includes early home-computer releases alongside later licensed and distributed titles.

Documented company facts

  • Activision was founded in 1979.
  • The company described its business as developing, publishing and distributing interactive entertainment.
  • Its historical operations included the United Kingdom alongside North America and other international markets.
