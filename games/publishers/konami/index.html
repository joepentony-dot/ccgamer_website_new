About this publisher

Konami began in Osaka in 1969 and moved into amusement-machine manufacturing after incorporation in 1973. Its arcade catalogue later supplied numerous games for home-computer conversions, including Commodore 64 releases.

Documented company facts

  • Konami's official corporate history records Kagemasa Kozuki founding the business on 21 March 1969.
  • Konami Industries Co., Ltd. was established on 19 March 1973 and began manufacturing amusement machines for arcades.
  • Konami began exporting products to the United States in 1979.
