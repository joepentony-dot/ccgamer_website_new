About this publisher

Ocean Software was founded in Manchester in 1983 by David Ward and Jon Woods. It began as Spectrum Games, adopted the Ocean name, and became strongly associated with licensed film, television and arcade properties across the British home-computer market.

Documented company facts

  • Founded in 1983 by David Ward and Jon Woods.
  • The business initially used the name Spectrum Games before adopting Ocean.
  • Ocean acquired the Imagine label in late 1984 and used it for many arcade conversions.

Archive strengths

  • Arcade conversions
  • Film and television licences
  • Large UK retail releases
