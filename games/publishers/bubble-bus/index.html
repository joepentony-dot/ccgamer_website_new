About this publisher

Bubble Bus published games across several 8-bit home-computer platforms during the first half of the 1980s. Its documented Commodore output began early in the C64 era and its catalogue later included the multi-format action-adventure Starquake.

Documented company facts

  • The Centre for Computing History records Bubble Bus releases on the Commodore VIC-20 and Commodore 64 from 1981 and 1982, followed by further C64 titles through the mid-1980s.
  • The museum catalogue documents Starquake under Bubble Bus on several platforms, including Atari 8-bit, ZX Spectrum, BBC Micro and Amstrad CPC.
