The Adventures Of Robin Hood

Overview

Game Summary

The Adventures of Robin Hood on the Commodore Amiga challenges players to restore Robin's reputation, transforming him from outlaw to hero through a series of brave acts across Sherwood. The overarching goal is to reclaim Robin's stolen castle by assembling the Merry Men and eliminating the Sheriff of Nottingham and his forces.
