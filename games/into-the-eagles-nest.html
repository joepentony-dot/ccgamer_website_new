Overview

Game Summary

Released on the Commodore 64 in 1987 by Mindscape, Into The Eagle’s Nest is an arcade-style action game inspired by World War II infiltration missions, tasking you with sneaking through a heavily guarded fortress to rescue prisoners. The C64 version blends shooting, exploration, and stealth elements, standing out for its tense pacing and methodical approach rather than constant run-and-gun action.
