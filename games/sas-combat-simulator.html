Game Summary

SAS Combat Simulator is a military-themed arcade action game released for the Commodore 64 in 1989. Developed by Vision Software and published by Codemasters as a budget title, it blends multiple gameplay styles inspired by retro classics like Commando, Ikari Warriors, and Green Beret
