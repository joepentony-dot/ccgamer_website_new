Game Summary

B.C. Bill is a Commodore 64 arcade game released by Imagine in 1984. Set in a prehistoric world, you guide a caveman through obstacle-filled levels while collecting items and avoiding hazards. The game is remembered for its simple mechanics and cartoon-style presentation.
