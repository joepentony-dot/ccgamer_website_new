Commercial transparency

Affiliate Disclosure

Cheeky Commodore Gamer uses a small number of selected Amazon.co.uk affiliate links for retro-gaming hardware, books, controllers and related products that are relevant to the site's audience.

Updated: 1 September 2026Amazon affiliate links active

Amazon Associates

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

When you follow a qualifying Amazon link from Cheeky Commodore Gamer and make a purchase, the site may receive a commission. Using an affiliate link does not add an extra charge to the price you pay.

Affiliate links are marked as sponsored links in the website code and are accompanied by disclosure wording where product recommendations are displayed.

How products are selected

Product recommendations are kept focused on items that fit the subject of the page, such as Commodore 64 and Amiga hardware, retro controllers, emulation accessories and relevant books. The presence of an affiliate link does not mean a product receives favourable coverage.

Prices, stock, specifications and retailer terms can change. Cheeky Commodore Gamer does not present Amazon prices as live or guaranteed; check the current Amazon listing before purchasing.

Editorial independence

Affiliate commission does not determine reviews, rankings, criticism or editorial decisions. Products can be recommended, criticised or omitted regardless of whether an affiliate relationship exists.

Other commercial relationships

Sponsored work, gifted products or paid placements are separate from ordinary affiliate links. Where they appear, they will be identified using direct wording such as Advertisement, Sponsored, Paid promotion or Gifted product.

More information

General website terms are available on the Terms of Use page. Partnership information is available on Work with CCG. Questions about a product recommendation or disclosure can be sent through the contact page.
