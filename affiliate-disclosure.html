Commercial transparency

Affiliate Disclosure

Some links can earn Cheeky Commodore Gamer a commission. This page explains how those links are identified and how commercial relationships are handled.

Last updated: 6 August 2026Editorial independence retained

Affiliate links

When a link is marked as a Paid link, Affiliate link or similar wording, Cheeky Commodore Gamer may receive a commission if you follow the link and make a qualifying purchase. This does not normally increase the price you pay.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

How links are labelled

  • Amazon product links are automatically marked as sponsored links and accompanied by a visible paid-link label.
  • Pages containing Amazon affiliate links display a disclosure near the affiliate content.
  • Sponsored articles, videos or placements will be identified using direct wording such as Advertisement, Sponsored or Paid promotion.
  • Gifted or loaned products will be disclosed where they are relevant to the coverage.

Recommendations and reviews

Commercial relationships do not guarantee favourable coverage. Recommendations are selected because they are relevant to Commodore, Amiga, emulation or retro-gaming visitors. Opinions, criticisms and final editorial decisions remain with Cheeky Commodore Gamer.

Prices, availability, specifications and retailer terms may change after publication. Check the retailer’s current listing before buying.

Retailer data

When you follow an affiliate link, the retailer or affiliate network may record the referral and use its own cookies or identifiers. Those systems are controlled by the third party and are covered by its privacy and cookie information. Cheeky Commodore Gamer does not receive your full payment-card details.

Questions

Questions about a label, recommendation or commercial relationship can be sent to info@cheekycommodoregamer.co.uk.
