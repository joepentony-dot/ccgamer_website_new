## Confirmed source problems and R137 narrow repairs
1. `v10-16-voice-director.js::finishActive()` clears a voice timer and nulls the active owner but **did not pause the HTMLAudioElement** on end/watchdog expiry. An Ogg sprite can continue across adjacent recorded phrases after the owner is released. Candidate now pauses the audio and drops old `onended`/`onerror` handlers. Source-executed audio watchdog test added; exact-clip browser audio QA still required.
2. `v10-41-stage8-npc-dialogue.js::wrapRescueOwner()` emitted the same `scout.following` message repeatedly during ordinary movement every repeat interval. Candidate removes the proximity loop; initial scout discovery still speaks and `updateRescue()` now explicitly calls `Stage8NpcDialogue.presentScout(...{stateKey:"rescued"})` once when Scout actually enters sanctuary. Ordinary sanctuary-keeper greeting is suppressed during an active escort, and the completion-toast voice is explicitly delegated to Scout. Preserve existing supporter voices and no-fallback-recreation policy.
3. `game-play.js::triggerSequenceTorch()` previously hard-rejected invocations flagged `deliberate=false` despite verifying exact player position, room, and a per-player pad transition ledger. Candidate permits actual occupied-tile entry regardless of this ancillary flag, retains first-entry-only progress, no weapon-trigger activation, correct sequence and exactly-once award. This alone does not prove the owner's torch spot or seed is fixed. Need actual mobile/gamepad/keyboard walk-on browser acceptance.
4. `game-play.js::spawnArenaWave()` previously selected enemy tiles arithmetically without ensuring player-walkability, reachable graph connectivity, non-overlap with room props, active pickups, generators, chests or enemies. A live unreachable arena enemy can hold `updateArena()`'s door-release condition indefinitely. Candidate flood-fills the triggered arena from player cell using canonical `W.playerWalkable`, then spawns only on reachable unoccupied cells; if no cells exist, existing arena update logic proceeds to clear/reopen the doors. **Does not modify ordinary Count Loadula or the Sigil Warden boss damage rules.**
5. `v10-42-artefact-shop-stability.js` conflated an already-carried Ward-Break Charge (one-per-carrier) and a legitimately full inventory with a single generic “INVENTORY FULL” after a failed transaction. Candidate preflights owned charge separately; when a slot really is locked, shows actual occupied/unlocked count, retains Essence; if insertion fails unexpectedly despite spare slot, rolls back inventory/Essence and reports a distinct transfer failure for further diagnostics. **The one-charge limit and Flask economics are unchanged** pending owner's save/seed.
6. Added exact-source Node VM contracts `v10-42-r137-voice-scout-torch-arena.mjs` and `v10-42-r137-flask-shop-capacity.mjs` proving core before/after paths. These tests were added to source but are **not yet verified in CI** until a PR triggers canonical/full workflow. Do not claim current build/floor crash verified from them.
