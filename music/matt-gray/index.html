Matt Gray

5 linked game credits

Born: 1970-05

Birthplace: Kent, England

Nationality: English

Matt Gray is an English producer and composer best known in game-music circles for his Commodore 64 work. His soundtrack for Last Ninja 2 is among his most recognised game scores, and he has remained connected with C64 music through later recordings and reworkings of his catalogue.
