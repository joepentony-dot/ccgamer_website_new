Raphaël Gesqua

Born: 1972-02-11

Birthplace: Paris, France

Nationality: French

Also known as: Audiomonster, Raphael Gesqua

Raphaël Gesqua is a French composer and sound designer who began in the 16-bit demoscene under the name Audiomonster before moving into commercial game audio. His early work includes the Amiga version of Flashback, followed by music and sound for Mr. Nutz, Shaq Fu, Fade to Black, Moto Racer and later film and game projects.
