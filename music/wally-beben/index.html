Wally Beben

Born: 1953-09-05

Nationality: English

Also known as: Wallace Beben, Hagar, Wal Beben

Wally Beben is an English former game musician best known for a large body of Commodore 64 and Amiga music. Among his best-known pieces is the extended C64 soundtrack for Tetris, and his career also included original music, arrangements and sound effects across numerous home-computer releases.
