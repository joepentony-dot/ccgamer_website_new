Paul Norman

Commodore 64

4 linked game credits

Born: 1951-12-18

Nationality: American

Paul Norman is an American musician, programmer and game designer who became closely associated with Cosmi's Commodore 64 releases. He combined programming, game design and original audio on titles including Forbidden Forest, Beyond the Forbidden Forest and Aztec Challenge.

In the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive, Paul Norman is linked to 4 recorded game-music credits (4 C64) spanning 1983–1989. Linked releases include Aztec Challenge, Beyond The Forbidden Forest, Chomp! and Forbidden Forest.
