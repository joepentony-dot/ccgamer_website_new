Peter Clarke

Born: 1958

Died: 2025-07

Nationality: British

Peter Clarke was a British composer whose C64 work became associated with Ocean Software and related projects. His credits include Bubble Bobble, Head over Heels and other home-computer titles, drawing on a background in keyboards, guitar and electronic music production.
