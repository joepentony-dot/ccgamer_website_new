Jeff Minter

Born: 1962

Birthplace: Reading, England

Nationality: English

Also known as: Yak

Jeff Minter is an English programmer and game designer who founded Llamasoft and became one of the most distinctive independent developers of the early home-computer era. His C64 catalogue includes Gridrunner, Hover Bovver, Revenge of the Mutant Camels and Sheep in Space, and he also created music and light-synthesizer software alongside his game design work.

Sources: Centre for Computing History - Jeff Minter
