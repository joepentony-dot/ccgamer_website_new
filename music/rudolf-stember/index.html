Born: 1969-03-26

Birthplace: Cologne, West Germany

Nationality: German

Rudolf Stember is a German composer and audio director whose career began with home-computer music before moving into major game-development roles. He worked with Rainbow Arts and later Factor 5, with CCG-linked credits including Curse of Ra and The Secret of Monkey Island.
