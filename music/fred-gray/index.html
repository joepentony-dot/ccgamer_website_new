Fred Gray

Commodore 64

13 linked game credits

Nationality: English

Fred Gray is an English game composer closely associated with Commodore 64 music of the mid-to-late 1980s. His credits include Shadowfire, Mutants, Nodes of Yesod, Frankie Goes to Hollywood and many other titles, with work for companies including Imagine, Ocean and Odin. Published sources conflict on his birth year, so it is intentionally omitted.

In the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive, Fred Gray is linked to 13 recorded game-music credits (13 C64) spanning 1984–1988. Linked releases include Army Moves, Batman: The Caped Crusader, B.C. Bill and Bounces.
