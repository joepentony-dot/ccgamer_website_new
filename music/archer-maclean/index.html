Archer MacLean

Amiga

1 linked game credit

Born: 1962-01-28

Died: 2022-12-17

Nationality: British

Also known as: Archer Maclean

Archer MacLean was a British programmer, designer and game creator best known for Dropzone, International Karate, IK+ and his later snooker and pool games. He was primarily a designer and programmer rather than a career composer, but his multi-discipline credits include sound work represented in the CCG Amiga archive.

Archer MacLean is represented in the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive by one recorded Amiga music credit, Jimmy White's Whirlwind Snooker (1991).
