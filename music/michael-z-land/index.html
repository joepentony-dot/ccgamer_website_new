Michael Z. Land

Amiga

1 linked game credit

Born: 1961

Birthplace: Boston, Massachusetts, United States

Nationality: American

Michael Land is an American composer and audio programmer best known for his work at Lucasfilm Games and LucasArts. He wrote the defining music for The Secret of Monkey Island and, with Peter McConnell, developed the iMUSE interactive music system that became central to many LucasArts adventures.

Michael Z. Land is represented in the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive by one recorded Amiga music credit, Monkey Island 2: Lechuck'S Revenge (1992).
