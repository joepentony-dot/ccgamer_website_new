Born: 1954-02-08

Birthplace: Romford, Greater London, England

Nationality: English

Steve Turner is an English programmer, designer and composer who founded Graftgold in 1983. His home-computer career combined code and music, with CCG-linked credits including Uridium, Gribbly's Day Out, Alleykat and Rainbow Islands.
