Graham Jarvis

Commodore 64

4 linked game credits

Born: 1952-04

Nationality: British

Graham Jarvis is a British media composer whose game work included a partnership with Rob Hartshorne through Clever Music. He contributed to a range of C64 projects before moving further into television and production music, with CCG-linked credits including Back to the Future, Blade Runner, The Rocky Horror Show and Shao-Lin's Road.

In the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive, Graham Jarvis is linked to 4 recorded game-music credits (4 C64) spanning 1985–1986. Linked releases include Back To The Future, Blade Runner, The Rocky Horror Show and Shao-Lin's Road.
