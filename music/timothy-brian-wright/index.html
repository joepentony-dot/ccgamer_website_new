Timothy Brian Wright

Amiga

2 linked game credits

Born: 1967-07-31

Birthplace: Cymau, North Wales

Nationality: Welsh

Also known as: Tim Wright, CoLD SToRAGE

Tim Wright is a Welsh composer and musician whose game career includes major work for Psygnosis. His Amiga credits include Lemmings and Agony, while later projects include the Wipeout series and music released under the name CoLD SToRAGE.
