Ben Daglish

Commodore 64

16 linked game credits

Born: 1966-07-31

Birthplace: Chiswick, London, England

Died: 2018-10-01

Nationality: English

Ben Daglish was an English composer, musician and programmer whose work became a major part of British 8-bit game music. He wrote and arranged numerous Commodore 64 soundtracks, including The Last Ninja, Deflektor, Gauntlet and Jack the Nipper, and was also associated with the W.E.M.U.S.I.C. partnership and the wider Gremlin-era music scene.

In the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive, Ben Daglish is linked to 16 recorded game-music credits (16 C64) spanning 1986–1991. Linked releases include 720 Degrees, Auf Wiedersehen Monty, Cobra and Defenders Of The Earth.
