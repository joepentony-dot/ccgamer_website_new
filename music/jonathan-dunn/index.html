Jonathan Dunn

C64 & Amiga

11 linked game credits

Born: 1968-10-08

Birthplace: Preston, Lancashire, England

Nationality: English

Jonathan Dunn is an English composer and sound designer best known for his work at Ocean Software. His C64 output includes RoboCop, Platoon, Operation Wolf and many other licensed or arcade-derived games, and he later continued in professional game audio including work connected with Electronic Arts.
