Julie Dunn

Commodore 64

6 linked game credits

Born: 1949-06-16

Nationality: English

Also known as: David Dunn

Julie Dunn is an English former composer, sound designer and programmer whose Commodore 64 work includes Chiller, Finders Keepers, High Noon, The Trap Door and music programming and arrangement for Elite. She trained at the Royal College of Music under Alan Rowlands and Antony Milner, later taught at the Centre for Young Musicians and held senior music-teaching roles. Her route into C64 development began after meeting Anirog co-founder Anil Gupta, leading to music for Flight Path 737 and further work for several British software houses. She later studied psychiatric nursing at King's College London and worked in mental-health care before retirement. Her original 1980s game credits appeared under the name David Dunn.

In the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive, Julie Dunn is linked to 6 recorded game-music credits (6 C64) spanning 1984–1986. Linked releases include Chiller, Daley Thompson's Decathlon, Elite and Finders Keepers.
