Reyn Ouwehand

Commodore 64

1 linked game credit

Born: 1973-08-02

Birthplace: Katwijk aan Zee, Netherlands

Nationality: Dutch

Reyn Ouwehand is a Dutch composer, producer and multi-instrumentalist whose professional music career began with computer-game commissions while he was still a teenager. His C64 work includes Last Ninja 3 and Flimbo’s Quest, after which he built a wider career as a producer, arranger and studio musician.

Reyn Ouwehand is represented in the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive by one recorded Commodore 64 music credit, Flimbo's Quest (1990).
