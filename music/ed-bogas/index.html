Ed Bogas

Born: 1942-02-02

Birthplace: San Francisco, California, United States

Nationality: American

Ed Bogas is an American composer whose career spans records, film, television and video games. Alongside screen work including animation, he contributed music to a number of 1980s computer games; Game credits include Card Sharks, Law of the West, Murder on the Mississippi and Park Patrol.
