Paul Butler

Commodore 64

3 linked game credits

Nationality: Canadian

Paul Butler is a Canadian game designer, programmer and musician who co-founded Artech Digital Productions in Ottawa in 1982. His C64 music credits in the CCG archive include Ace of Aces, Apollo 18 and The Train: Escape to Normandy, alongside wider design and production work.
