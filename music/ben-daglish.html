Ben Daglish was an English composer and musician whose work became a major part of 1980s home-computer gaming. He is best known for C64 music on titles such as The Last Ninja, Krakout and Deflektor, and he also composed for Amiga releases. Daglish remains one of the most celebrated names in classic game music.
