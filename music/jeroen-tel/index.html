Jeroen Tel

Born: 1972-05-19

Birthplace: Eindhoven, Netherlands

Nationality: Dutch

Also known as: WAVE

Jeroen Tel is a Dutch composer who became one of the best-known musicians of the later Commodore 64 era. He co-founded Maniacs of Noise with Charles Deenen in 1987 and produced music for a wide range of games, including Cybernoid, Hawkeye, Myth and Turbo Out Run in the CCG archive.

In the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive, Jeroen Tel is linked to 9 recorded game-music credits (8 C64 and 1 Amiga) spanning 1988–1992. Linked releases include After Burner, Agony, Cybernoid and Dan Dare III - The Escape.
