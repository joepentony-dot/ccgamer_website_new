Shahid Ahmad

Amiga

1 linked game credit

Nationality: British

Also known as: Shahid Kamal Ahmad, Quality Notch

Shahid Ahmad is a British programmer, composer, producer and games-industry executive who began selling games while still a teenager. His early work includes the C64 conversion of Jet Set Willy and the game Chimera; he later held senior production and developer-relations roles, including a long period at Sony PlayStation.

Shahid Ahmad is represented in the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive by one recorded Amiga music credit, Floor 13 (1992).
