Shahid Ahmad

Nationality: British

Also known as: Shahid Kamal Ahmad, Quality Notch

Shahid Ahmad is a British game programmer, composer, producer and games-industry executive who entered commercial development while still a teenager. His 1980s work includes the Commodore 64 conversion of Jet Set Willy and the game Chimera; he later spent a long period at Sony PlayStation working in developer relations and indie publishing.
