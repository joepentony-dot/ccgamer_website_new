Born: 1966-12-31

Birthplace: Strømmen, Norway

Nationality: Norwegian

Also known as: Dr. Awesome

Bjørn Lynne is a Norwegian composer who first became widely known in the Amiga demoscene under the handle Dr. Awesome. He later moved into commercial game music, including work connected with Team17 and the Worms series, while maintaining a long independent recording career.

In the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive, Bjørn Lynne is linked to 2 recorded game-music credits (2 Amiga) spanning 1991–1992. Linked releases include Escape From Colditz and Project-X.
