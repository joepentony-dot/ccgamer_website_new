Frédéric Motte

Amiga

1 linked game credit

Born: 1970

Also known as: Fred Motte, El Mobo, ElMobo, Moby

Frédéric Motte is a French game composer, sound designer and demoscene musician also known as El Mobo. After early computer and demo music, he became an in-house musician and sound designer at Atreid Concept and later led audio at Kalisto Entertainment before founding his own studio.
