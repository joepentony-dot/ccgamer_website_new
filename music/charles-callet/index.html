Charles Callet

Birthplace: Bron, near Lyon, France

Died: 1995-10-04

Nationality: French

Charles Callet was a French composer and game musician associated with the French computer-game industry, including work for Infogrames and related studios. His Amiga credits in the game catalogue include Hostages, North & South and Ween: The Prophecy.
