Born: 1970-11-29

Birthplace: Westminster, London, England

Nationality: British

Allister Brimble is a British game composer and sound designer whose career spans the home-computer era through to modern games. His Amiga work includes music and audio for titles such as Alien Breed, Project-X, Superfrog and Zeewolf, while his wider career covers hundreds of game soundtracks across numerous platforms.

In the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive, Allister Brimble is linked to 12 recorded game-music credits (4 C64 and 8 Amiga) spanning 1990–1995. Linked releases include Agony, Alien Breed, Arcade Pool and Danny Sullivans Indy Heat.
