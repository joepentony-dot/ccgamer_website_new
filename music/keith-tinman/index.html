Keith Tinman

C64 & Amiga

6 linked game credits

Nationality: British

Keith Tinman is a British game composer associated with Odin Computer Graphics and later Ocean-era releases. His Commodore credits include Heartland, Cabal, Midnight Resistance and Toki, with work extending from original music to arrangements for home-computer conversions.
