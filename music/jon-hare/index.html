Jon Hare

Born: 1966-01-20

Birthplace: Ilford, Essex, England

Nationality: English

Jon Hare is an English game designer, artist and musician best known as the co-founder of Sensible Software with Chris Yates. His work includes Sensible Soccer, Cannon Fodder and other major 16-bit releases, where he contributed creatively across design, graphics, music and production.
