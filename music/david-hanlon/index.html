David Hanlon

David Hanlon is a game musician and sound designer known for the Commodore 64 music for Druid and Druid II: Enlightenment. He later provided in-game sound for Bullfrog's Populous on the Amiga, and has described how the arrival of sampled Amiga audio changed his interests in computer sound.

Sources: C64.COM interview with David Hanlon · Lemon64 — Druid credits and David Hanlon note
