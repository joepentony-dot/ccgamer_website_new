Oisten Eide

Nationality: Norwegian

Also known as: Øistein Johan Eide, Boom Jinx

Oisten Eide, whose full Norwegian name is Øistein Johan Eide and who is better known professionally as Boom Jinx, is a Norwegian composer, producer, sound designer and electronic musician. He began writing music in 1989 and contributed to early-1990s computer games, including music for RoboCop 3, before developing a wider career in commercial film and television music and later electronic dance music. As Boom Jinx he became associated with labels including Anjunabeats and Anjunadeep.
