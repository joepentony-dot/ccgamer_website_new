Alan McKean

Commodore 64

2 linked game credits

Nationality: American

Alan McKean is a game composer and audio director whose credits span the late-1980s and 1990s, including a substantial run of work with Dynamix. His C64 credits in the CCG archive are Caveman Ugh-Lympics and Project Firestart, followed by audio work on titles such as A-10 Tank Killer, Red Baron and The Adventures of Willy Beamish.

In the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive, Alan McKean is linked to 2 recorded game-music credits (2 C64) spanning 1988–1989. Linked releases include Caveman Ugh-Lympics and Project Firestart.
