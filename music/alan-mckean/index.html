Alan McKean

Nationality: American

Alan McKean is a game composer and audio director whose credits span the late-1980s and 1990s, including a substantial run of work with Dynamix. His C64 credits in the game catalogue are Caveman Ugh-Lympics and Project Firestart, followed by audio work on titles such as A-10 Tank Killer, Red Baron and The Adventures of Willy Beamish.

Sources: MobyGames Alan McKean profile · Project Firestart manual credits
