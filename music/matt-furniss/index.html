Matt Furniss

Commodore 64

6 linked game credits

Born: 1973-03-06

Birthplace: Sheffield, South Yorkshire, England

Nationality: English

Matt Furniss is an English composer and sound designer who began professional game-audio work as a teenager. He worked at Krisalis and on a large number of computer and console conversions, with C64 credits in the CCG archive including Chase H.Q., Klax and Escape from the Planet of the Robot Monsters.

In the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive, Matt Furniss is linked to 6 recorded game-music credits (6 C64) spanning 1989–1991. Linked releases include Badlands, Castle Master, Chase H.Q. and Escape From The Planet Of The Robot Monsters.
