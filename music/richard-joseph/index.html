Richard Joseph

C64 & Amiga

17 linked game credits

Born: 1953-04-23

Birthplace: England

Died: 2007-03-04

Nationality: British

Richard Joseph was a British composer, sound designer and audio director whose career covered the C64, Amiga and later game platforms. He worked on titles including Barbarian, Cannon Fodder, The Chaos Engine and Sensible Soccer, and became known for pushing interactive music, speech and licensed-artist collaboration in games.

In the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive, Richard Joseph is linked to 17 recorded game-music credits (5 C64 and 12 Amiga) spanning 1986–1995. Linked releases include Barbarian: The Ultimate Warrior, Barbarian II: The Dungeon Of Drax, Brutal Sports Football and Cadaver.
