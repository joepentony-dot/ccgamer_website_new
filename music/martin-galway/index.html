Martin Galway — Game Music

Martin Galway

Born: 1966-01-03

Birthplace: Belfast, Northern Ireland

Nationality: British

Martin Galway is a British game composer whose Commodore 64 work became strongly associated with Ocean Software. His scores and loader music include Rambo: First Blood Part II, Wizball, Green Beret and many other releases, and he became noted for technical experimentation with the SID chip, including sampled sound.
