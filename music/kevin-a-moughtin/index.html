Kevin A. Moughtin

Nationality: British

Kevin A. Moughtin is a British programmer, designer and musician from the early Firebird era. He created and scored Booty on the Commodore 64 and is also credited on Thunderbirds and other C64 projects, combining code, design and music rather than working only as a composer.
