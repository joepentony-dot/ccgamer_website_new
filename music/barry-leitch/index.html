Barry Leitch

C64 & Amiga

8 linked game credits

Born: 1970-04-27

Birthplace: Paisley, Renfrewshire, Scotland

Nationality: Scottish

Also known as: The Jackal

Barry Leitch is a Scottish video-game composer whose professional career began in his teens. After early C64 work he joined Imagitec and went on to contribute music and sound to a large range of home-computer and console games, including Lotus Turbo Challenge 2, HeroQuest and Super Cars II in the CCG archive.

In the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive, Barry Leitch is linked to 8 recorded game-music credits (5 C64 and 3 Amiga) spanning 1988–1991. Linked releases include Double Dragon, Emlyn Hughes International Soccer, Fiendish Freddy's Big Top O' Fun and Heroquest.
