Chris Hülsbeck

C64 & Amiga

6 linked game credits

Born: 1968-03-02

Birthplace: Kassel, Germany

Nationality: German

Also known as: Chris Hulsbeck, Chris Huelsbeck

Chris Hülsbeck is a German game composer who began creating music on the Commodore 64 as a teenager. After winning a German computer-magazine music competition he built a career that includes The Great Giana Sisters and the Turrican series, later expanding into console and orchestral game-music projects.
