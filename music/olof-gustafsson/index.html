Olof Gustafsson

Born: 1971-07-25

Birthplace: Sweden

Nationality: Swedish

Also known as: Blaizer

Olof Gustafsson is a Swedish composer and programmer who emerged from the demoscene before becoming a co-founder of Digital Illusions. His Amiga work includes music for Pinball Dreams and Benefactor, and his later career continued through games, technology and independent music projects.
