Steve Crow

Commodore 64

1 linked game credit

Born: 1965

Nationality: British

Also known as: Steve Crow, Steve J. Crow, Steve Crown

Stephen 'Steve' Crow is a British game programmer, artist and musician whose 1980s work included Starquake, for which he handled programming, graphics and music on the Commodore 64. His later career extended through game art and development work at studios including Blizzard Entertainment.
