Jason C. Brooke

Commodore 64

4 linked game credits

Birthplace: Kippax, West Yorkshire, England

Nationality: English

Jason C. Brooke is an English composer, programmer and sound designer whose C64 work includes a substantial run of arcade conversions and licensed games. He worked with companies including Binary Design and Musicon, where he collaborated with David Whittaker on music technology and production.

Sources: C64.COM David Whittaker interview
