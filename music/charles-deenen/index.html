Charles Deenen

Commodore 64

1 linked game credit

Born: 1970-01-15

Birthplace: Holthees, Noord-Brabant, Netherlands

Nationality: Dutch

Also known as: TMC, The Mercenary Cracker

Charles Deenen is a Dutch audio director, composer, sound designer and mixer who began experimenting with computer sound as a teenager. He co-founded Maniacs of Noise with Jeroen Tel and contributed to C64 and Amiga game audio before building a long international career in interactive and film sound.

Charles Deenen is represented in the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive by one recorded Commodore 64 music credit, Zamzara (1988).
