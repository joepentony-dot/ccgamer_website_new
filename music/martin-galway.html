Martin Galway is a British composer strongly associated with Commodore 64 and ZX Spectrum game music. He is especially remembered for Ocean-era scores and loader music, with standout C64 credits including Rambo: First Blood Part II, Wizball and Arkanoid loader music. His work is often cited among the most recognisable examples of classic 8-bit game audio.
