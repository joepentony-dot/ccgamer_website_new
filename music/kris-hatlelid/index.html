Kris Hatlelid is a programmer and musician associated with Canadian C64 development, with music credits including Grand Prix Circuit, Test Drive II and other Distinctive Software-era releases. Preservation projects such as HVSC have also documented and verified his SID work, although reliable public birth details are not readily available.

In the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive, Kris Hatlelid is linked to 3 recorded game-music credits (2 C64 and 1 Amiga) spanning 1989–1990. Linked releases include Grand Prix Circuit, Teenage Mutant Hero Turtles and Wings Of Fury.
