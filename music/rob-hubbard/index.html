Rob Hubbard — Game Music

Rob Hubbard

Birthplace: Kingston upon Hull, England

Nationality: English

Rob Hubbard is an English composer and programmer whose Commodore 64 music became one of the defining sounds of 1980s game audio. His C64 catalogue includes Commando, Monty on the Run, International Karate, Master of Magic and many other titles, and he later worked in the United States for Electronic Arts.
