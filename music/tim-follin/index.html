Tim Follin

Commodore 64

1 linked game credit

Birthplace: St Helens, England

Nationality: English

Also known as: Timothy J. Follin

Tim Follin is an English composer renowned for technically ambitious music on 8-bit and 16-bit systems. Working both independently and with Software Creations, he produced celebrated scores on the C64, Spectrum, NES, Amiga and other platforms before later moving away from full-time game composition.

Tim Follin is represented in the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive by one recorded Commodore 64 music credit, Bionic Commando (1988).
