Sid Meier

Born: 1954-02-24

Birthplace: Sarnia, Ontario, Canada

Nationality: Canadian-American

Also known as: Sidney K. Meier

Sid Meier is a Canadian-American game designer and programmer best known for co-founding MicroProse and creating or leading landmark strategy and simulation games including Pirates! and Civilization. Although music is not his principal discipline, the game catalogue contains a recorded music credit connected with one of his early computer-game projects.
