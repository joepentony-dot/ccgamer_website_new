Russell Lieblich

Commodore 64

8 linked game credits

Birthplace: New York City, United States

Died: 2005-01-26

Nationality: American

Russell Lieblich was an American composer, musician and sound designer who moved from academic music and physics into the early games industry. He worked at Mattel and Activision and wrote music for titles including Master of the Lamps, Little Computer People and Ghostbusters; later work extended into Electronic Arts and other game-audio projects.

In the Cheeky Commodore Gamer archive, Russell Lieblich is linked to 8 recorded game-music credits (8 C64) spanning 1984–1988. Linked releases include Aliens: The Computer Game, The Activision Decathlon, Ghostbusters and Little Computer People.
