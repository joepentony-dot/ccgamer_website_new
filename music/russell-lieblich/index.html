Russell Lieblich — Game Music

Russell Lieblich

Birthplace: New York City, United States

Died: 2005-01-26

Nationality: American

Also known as: Russ Lieblich, Russel Lieblich

Russell Lieblich was an American composer, musician, sound programmer and game designer whose career crossed Mattel Electronics, Activision and later Electronic Arts projects. After postgraduate study in music at the University of California, San Diego, he worked on Intellivision sound before joining Activision, where his game work included Web Dimension, Master of the Lamps, Little Computer People and Aliens; later credits included MechWarrior and PGA Tour titles.
