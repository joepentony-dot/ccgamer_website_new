FishyFish 2004-04-18
Loved this game and still have my original copy. Kind of a Pac-Man clone where you control a mouse in a maze who has to eat fruit stored in locked sections of the maze. You have to collect keys to get at the fruit, but after having eaten a certain amount the mouse gets fat and cannot get through certain passages until his stomach deflates. This can lead to being trapped by the baddies if you're not careful! Usual early C64 ragtime theme music but with an excellent rendition of Nobody Does it Better on the High Score screen.
