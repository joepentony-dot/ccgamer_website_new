Gurt 2008-03-30
This is a pretty good Monty game, but not as good as Monty on the Run. There's lots of annoying features, like the wine and the random breakdancing getting you killed, and there's lots of interesting 'features', like how if you keep jumping on spikes/mines you don't die, or the secret passages.

Zzap! Were pretty far off the mark here but some of their points are valid - there's far too much back and forth for a fairly linear game, and the puzzles are annoying (sometimes too obscure because you can't tell what the item is, or because it's not logical enough - selling the Mona Lisa to the mafia, for example). The flight scene is fun but tedious, and if you beat it, the end isn't rewarding. It's otherwise well presented.

All that being said, it's a fun platformer in a lot of ways - but not a great one. It's a shame. Monty fans will like it (maybe love it), but others may not.
