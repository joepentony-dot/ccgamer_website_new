Really hard to grade this one as the subsequent games are so much better. At the time, this was pretty revolutionary stuff and showed what these new-fangled computer things might be capable of. But, comparing this to the later Ultimas, Wizardries and Gold Box games it really is a very simple game and is very easy to complete. Still, its an important piece of gaming history and can still be enjoyed 40 years after its release.

A not too faithful 1986 remake of the 1980 original Ultima (only available for Apple II since it was largely written in hard-to-port Applesoft BASIC). Revolutionary in 1980, in 1986 it was already a bit sub-par and thus didn't sell too well.

Devin de Gruyl 2002-03-11
This is where it all started for one of the longest-running and most popular computer RPG series in history. Surprisingly, it holds up very well to modern eyes; it's just plain fun to build your character up, take on the Kings' Quests, and collect the info needed to destroy the evil wizard Mondain. There's even an odd section of the game that puts you in outer space, blasting away at enemies that look suspiciously like TIE fighters! Note that this is the 1986 re-release of the game; the 1980 original does not appear to have been made for anything other than the Apple ][.
