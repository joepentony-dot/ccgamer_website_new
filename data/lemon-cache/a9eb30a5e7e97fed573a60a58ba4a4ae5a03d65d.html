Nayphee 2025-11-13 (2/10)
The first "Street Fighter" arcade game was quite terrible, but somehow the developers managed to find a way to make this conversion even worse. It looks, sounds, and plays terribly. Special moves and bonus rounds are absent. It's barely playable and not very fun anyway.
You're better off playing the much improved US version - which despite having problems of it's own - at least plays closer to the source material.

Pavle Majonez 2024-07-16 (2/10)
Ouch, I don't know where to start.
On a positive side, all fighters are here, the background scrolls when a charcter reaches the end of screen, the gameplay is sort of quick, and Birdie's theme is the standout music track here.
But then, the game is very messed up, the graphics are really blocky, not all fighters have their own unique stage, they share one in which country they are in, one bonus stage is missing, and the most important thing, the special moves are completely missing!
The opponent AI is broken, it's either braindead, you can eventually KO it by just spamming the left and right leg sweep kick, or it's stupidly challenging, Geki can turn invisible and throw shurikens at you, Lee has a weird hitbox, and it can be tricky to hit him, and Sagat can hurl fireballs at you and you can't avoid them even if you crouch and he can knock you out in just two moves! I still can't beat Sagat!
The controls sometimes want to work, and at other times, it doesn't register your input.
Overall, this is a very disappointing conversion.

compnded 2023-09-22 (2/10)
The rubbish that Tiertex churned out is legendary and this conversion of a middling arcade game plumbs new depths. It's difficult to tell at times exactly what the pixelated mess is that u are supposed to be controlling and the moves consequently feel vague and unsatisfying. The only plus is that the backgrounds scroll but they look awful with crude definition and very odd colour choices. There is nothing whatsoever to recommend the game and better fighting games preceded and succeeded it. The ratings from the mags at the time where all very kind on it but from the comments on Lemon, the C64 community is pretty much in total agreement that this is a total duffer.
