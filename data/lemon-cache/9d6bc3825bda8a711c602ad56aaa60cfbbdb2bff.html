Maff Rignall 2003-09-21
A classic. The aesthetics are crude, but they're also evocative - Yamo and the Knight are my two favourite VG villians *ever*. The platform action is great fun, but what gets me is the attention that's gone into making the game world feel real and self consistent (in spite of how bizarre it may look - purple kinetic sponge ladders, anyone?). The two bad guys both have simple AI: they don't just follow predetermined patterns, they react to your movements and try to stop you, and they can accidently hurt each other. They can also by killed by traps. This sort of detail is standard today, but in 1984 was rare. It enhances the gameplay too - luring the baddies into traps is great fun. The two player mode is worthy of mention too - the second person plays as one of the bad guys and, with their AI controlled partner in crime, has to try to stop player one from progressing. It may be easy to finish (10-15 minutes to play through the entire game), but it's great fun regardless. 10/10.
