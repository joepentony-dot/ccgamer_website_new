hazzard 2024-02-04 (9/10)
Zzap!64 fumbled the ball on this game big time; it's worth far more than 50%. As far as adventure games go, this one is incredible. Exploring the labyrinth and solving all the puzzles feels so satisfying, plus the game follows the plot of the movie brilliantly, giving it a very fulfilling feeling.
The graphics capture the look & feel of the movie so well, featuring large, detailed sprites that animate fluidly. All the major locations from the film appear in the game as well (hedge maze, brick hallway, wall of hands, etc).
Special mention must go to the soundtrack which creates tension and is foreboding. You really do get the feeling that Jareth is looking over your every move.
I had this game on tape as part of the Six Sizzlers pack meaning it was somewhat hindered by an annoying multiload, but I still persevered with it and managed to get to the end.
Highly recommended game for adventure and C64 fans alike!

This game is always overlooked in discussions on the first graphic adventure. There is a 'making of' story about it in an old c +vg, if anyone still has it. It was FULL of puns, riddles and puzzles that stayed true to the movie. Eg You used ludo to call rocks to get across the bog of eternal stench, and you threw them to knock out the goblins in the city. You used the fireys arm to reach high things, used its leg to steady yourself across the bog, and threw his head in the air to find your way through the forest. Etc etc. It also deliberately underrated itself! If you load up the game now, it begins with a classic text adventure - putting off all but the most imaginiative players, but rewarding them when the graphic adventure kicked in!
