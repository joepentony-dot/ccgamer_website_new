This game is mesmerizing from a technical perspective; all the levels from the arcade are present, with brightly-coloured sprites that animate beautifully. Jonathan Dunn's soundtrack, as usual, kicks serious ass, and the sound FX are pretty nifty as well. The gameplay is incredibly intense and frantic, but trying to play this with a joystick is an exercise in frustration, since the crosshair flies all over the place and the collision detection is somewhat off. I did manage to complete this game with a trainer when I was a kid, but I suspect it would've been more playable with a mouse.

OceanBorn 2022-07-15 (9/10)
Arcade conversations are always a tricky situation, on one hand the game needs to represent the quality of the arcade and on another there are limitations on how close it can actually be. Operation Wolf is a pretty solid home version of the famous arcade game, even without the UZI sub machine gun! All stages made it over and they don't look half bad, the enemy is also well represented with boats, tanks and helicopters. I played this with the quick shot 2 joystick and it was not easy to beat, especially since space bar is used for frags. Apparently the mouse makes it easier but alas I wouldn’t
know. Operation Wolf is a hard but rewarding shooter that still deserves your play time today.
