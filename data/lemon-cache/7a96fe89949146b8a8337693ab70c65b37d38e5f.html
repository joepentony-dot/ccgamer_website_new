The instructions tell you what you have to do, but not that any of that helps you because they are so confusing and obtuse. Basically this game comes down to putting objects in certain places, but the game doesn't tell you what or why you should. That just leads a path to utter confusion, bewilderment, and frustration. The tacky production quality of the game doesn't help it any. These sorts of games are an acquired taste, and I really didn't acquire one for it. Here I was thinking that games should be at least fun, but some people enjoy arduous tests of their patience with byzantine mazes and trial and error apparently.
