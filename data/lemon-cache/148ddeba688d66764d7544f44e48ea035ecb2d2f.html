Kelley 2001-12-29
Sequel to the C-64 classic which captures the action and adventure of the hit movie sequel. First we head down Van Horne to catch some slime. Next, we rock New York in the Statue of Liberty as you make your way to the Museum of Art. Finaly, arm your Proton Packs and Slime blowers to take down Yanosh (damn you Ally Mcbeal friend!) and Viggo (the master of evil!). Got some good graphics, excellent cinematic graphics and once again killer tunes including the GB's theme song and Higher & Higher! Gameplay is arcade mixed as you'll flex your wrist muscles (don't get sick on me homies!) a lot. Rember to press SPACE BAR to switch between your weapons/items in Van Horne. Hit SPACE BAR for sending your troops out to collect slime to power up Libby when you blow away some ghosts in the Statue of Liberty level. And again use SPACE BAR at the finale to switch between characters or weapons (either one or the other, can't remeber) when battling Ally's buddy and Viggo. PEACE and HAVE FUN!
