PonPon 2023-06-01 (7/10)
This game is utter jank, which is what makes it so appealing. Hard to master, but quite varied: You start off by riding on your horse until you encounter a cliff, from there you travel on foot through various scenes to overcome obstacles. There are also epic fighting scenes, a labyrinth, and of course the treasure! The title music is nice (for the best version of the tune, use 6581 and play the Interdisc version opposed to ECP).

The game was first released on tape by Interdisc in 1984. A few years later it was re-released on tape by ECP on two compilations: "U-Bewdy All Aussie Pak" (1987) and "Gold Ribbon Game Pak". There was also a floppy release of the "U-Bewdy All Aussie Pak". Every release had different cover art.
