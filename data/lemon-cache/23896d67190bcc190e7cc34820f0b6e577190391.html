MaceWindow 2023-08-20 (10/10)
Honestly 1 of my favorite games ever, on any system. The graphics, the sounds, the story, truly every aspect of this game had me riveted.
The progression of this game strikes me as perfect to this very day; open world before open world was a thing. But the genius of it was how you could not access certain areas/enemies that would have been far too advanced for your current level!
Brilliant mini games, like Stones of Wisdom, that were so good on their own, I've looked for things like them on my phone!

The 1st RPG I ever completed and why I love the genre today!

humourguy 2006-10-19 (10/10)
If you have a problem with D64's of this game, get a a D64 file that you have not loaded into an emulator yet and make it read only. The original game was read only and part of the protection was to write to the disk. If it couldn't write it assumed an original disk, if it could it intentionally corrupted files. The program will do this to the D64 as well so although it will seem to work first time, when you reload to play a second time you will not be able to.
