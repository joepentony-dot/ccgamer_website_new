BigM 2023-08-02 (8/10)
A really nice port of David Crane's amazing design for Atari 2600. How was he able to accomplish so much on 6KB?
However, credit should also go to Timothy Smith. He designed the very first Olympic themed game for the TRS-80 and the Apple back in 1980!

My favorite C64 event is the high jump. Really nice animation and playability here. I was even surprised when I received a fanfare and a cheer for a high score!

Otherwise, I'm still a bit disappointed. There are too many quiet parts. More should have been done in the backgrounds too. Even the Atari 5200 version has a scoreboard and backgrounds for the field events!
Recommended! ...but didn't get a 10 for those reasons.

And finally.. Does anyone really like playing the 1500m race?!

Sparky-D 2019-09-03 (10/10)
Best joggle-game on the C64 in my opinion. Okay, you have not such things like "best angles", that some other games have and which you must hit, to reach good distances in jumps or throwing competitions, but therefore, the controller-polling here is really good. You can try joggling on your joystick as hard as you can and the game really converts all these movements. Some similar games, like "Track & Field" or "Daley Thompsons Decathlon" for example, sometimes have problems with this, when the player joggles to hard and when his movements on the controller, are not in a certain left/right moving-limit. This game here not and therefore I really like it. I already played the Atari-2600 version of this game alot and even there on the console, it was really fun to play, especially against other human players. But this C64 port here, is even better than the Atari-2600 version. I destroyed a lot of joysticks with this game back then, but nevertheless I had fun. Especially when I played it against some friends. We all remember the 400-meter run, when the whole arm hurts on the last 100 meters and both players are still head to head. Sometimes in those days, we played, until we had some blisters on the hands from the joggling, haha.
