Nayphee 2025-11-28 (8/10)
Andrew Spencer's masterpiece. It may be an early game for the system, and not exactly the most precise soccer simulation with it's blocky graphics aesthetic. As an arcade head-to-head soccer game however, this one was pretty fun all things considered. You could even play solo with some rather tough Computer AI if you were missing a friend to play against. This was the pack-in game on many commodore 64 computer sales, so international soccer carts are very common and easy to get.
