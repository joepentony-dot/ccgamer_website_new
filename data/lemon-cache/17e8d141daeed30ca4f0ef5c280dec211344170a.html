hbhzth 2011-05-29 (9/10)
Asphodel; "All sprites in Hi-Res"?? Are you wasted dude? I do not think I have ever seen a C64 game where all sprite objects are in MC (multicolor) Hi-Res. It's the main character or object that has Hi-Res sprites (2 or 3 sprites on top of each other to make multicolors), like in Mayhem, Rambo or Combat School, etc. You have 5 sprites free for other objects (or more in Y pos if you use multiplexer routine). The C64 have max 8 sprites X pos (being 21 pixels high). Even if Creatures use standard 1 on 1 MC sprites + hires sprites the graphic's very good IMO. Notice the snow effect in the Winter Level, that's hires graphics. The choice of color combos in all levels are just perfect. It can't be done any better on the C64. Take Mayhem in Monsterland; they scroll the whole screen using bitmapped graphics, fast & smooth - something that was impossible to do until a "bug" in the VIC chip was found. Awesome controls, timing and sound too.
