Robotron 2084 2025-04-14 (8/10)
Truly hectic puzzle game that requires both planning skills and patience when the game denies you the next puzzle piece and spams you with bombs instead. It's never unfair though, as the omnipresent bombs can be dealt with if precautions are taken. Trying to maintain this prudence within the strict time limit is a real challenge though. Also for some reason I find the game's atmosphere slightly creepy but in a good way.

compnded 2023-09-19 (8/10)
Probably the best thing Domark ever did - I like the balance between the puzzle elements and the lightening quick reflexes that were needed to defuse the bombs etc. Good music and graphics rounded things off nicely. It is very demanding however and perhaps for that reason does not have general appeal.

Looking back at the 'personalities' they selected, who would have thought things would have turned out the way they did? Two royal divorces since and now one king and the other a leper.
