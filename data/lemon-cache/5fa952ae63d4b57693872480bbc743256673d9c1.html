Devin de Gruyl 2002-09-16
Take the sleeper-hit early '80s arcade game Rally-X, replace the cars and flags with mice and cheese, and you've got Radar Rat Race. Maneuver your blue rat through the maze, gobbling up hunks of cheese while avoiding the red rats who're on your tail (so to speak). If they get too close for comfort, you can use your Star Screen to blind them (this was a Smokescreen in Rally-X; I *don't* want to know what this replacement consists of!). You can use the radar screen (hence the first word of the title) to learn where the cheese and red rats are in the maze (though you can't see the maze itself). Unfortunately, the game suffers from the single most annoying tune in gaming history - "Three Blind Mice, " played over and over again ad infinitum. After two minutes, you're ready to smash your speakers! Play with the sound off, however, and you've got a decent port of a fun arcade title (despite the inexplicable sprite-swap).
