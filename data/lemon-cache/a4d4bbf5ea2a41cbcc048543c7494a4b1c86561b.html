ellis 2003-12-01
Fiendishly difficult puzzle game with three levels of 3D mazes. On the first level you try to collect hieroglyphs by chasing and running into ghosts (the blue dude in the pictures). On the second level you run around a bigger maze, luring spiders out of their lairs, and then running round the block to view the wall where the spider was and match up the pieces you got in the first level with lines in the bigger puzzle; take too long and the spider came back and bit you (scaring the hell out of you with the sound effect), eventually causing your demise. In the third level, you run around collecting potions, again matching heiroglyphs, whilst dodging these zombie dudes, take the right potions and your health is improved, take too many of the wrong ones and you die. Finally, you try to solve the Pharaoh's Key: a huge multi-sided puzzle to unlock the fabulous treasure (?)
