compnded 2025-01-05 (2/10)
A crap conversion of a dull and limited coin-op and given the relative simplicity of the original a much better attempt could and should have been implemented. The first stage ditches the diagonal perspective, even though games like Mission Impossibubble demonstrate that this would have been possible on the C64. The remaining two stages are better implemented but feel slow and the graphics are uniformly poor. Another miss is the terrible music, which was one of the arcade game's strengthens. Just a typically turgid US Gold conversion.
