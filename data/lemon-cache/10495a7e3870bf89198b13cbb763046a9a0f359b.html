Sonance 2003-08-02
Odin were clever enough to model themselves after Ultimate, with gorgeous cover artwork, an airbrushed logo and games of a very similar appeal (Nodes of Yesod is practically a remake of Underworld, while Robin of the Wood has a subtle Sabre Wulfe-esque feel). Even their parent names were similar -- Odin Computer Graphics vs Ashby Computer Graphics. Odin filled a gap in the C64 market for Ultimate style games -- at least Ultimate's Spectrum titles anyway. Ultimate's own C64 titles were very poor. However, Odin themselves soon lost their way. Hypa-Ball and ICUPS were pretty bad, although Mission AD and Heartland were something of an improvement. After that, Odin became swallowed up by Firebird and disappeared.

Brett Buckley 2002-10-19
It is essential to make a map. The forest seems bigger than it is (16x20 screens) because it wraps around. Map the Hermit's hut, the Ent (tree with a face) and the two castle entrances.

To finish the game, find the fat nobleman (I think he is a bishop) who wears red and pink robes. Kill his bodyguard and you will take two bags of gold from him. When you have at least 3 bags of gold, visit the Ent. He will take 3 bags from you and give you an item. The first 3 bags gets you a sword (a bit pointless since it does the same as the staff). The next 3 bags gets you a bow which is fun to use (once you have collected the quivers of arrows from around the forest). Further visits to the tree will trade 3 bags each time for a special red arrow. When 3 arrows have been collected, go to the castle - at which point the game will end.

They key is to find the fat nobleman as many times as you can. You tend to encounter him every 30 minutes or so on average - making for a lot of wandering!
