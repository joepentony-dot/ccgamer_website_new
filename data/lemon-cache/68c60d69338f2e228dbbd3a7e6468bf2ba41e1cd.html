Kelley 2001-12-29
Were do I begin...

It's always a hit and miss afair with "movie to video game" translations (from the Atari 2600 E. T. To today's Gameboy Color Austin Power's games). But Game Designer David Crane kept it real by capturing the same magic and humour that engulfed the motion picture. It still remains one of my favourite games (as well as one of my favourite movies) of all time.

Much fun to be had cruising around New York and catching Slimers for cash! Sneak past the Marshmallow Man and Zuul in the can! Save you account number at the end of it all and start over again with cooler equipment and faster cars! Great stuff!

One of the cool games during the early 1980's C-64 era. One of the only games to feature voice synthesis (WOOOHOOO!) during that time and it had a killer rendition of the Ray Parker Jr. Theme song (hitting the SPACE BAR at the title screen is half the fun!).

Definetly a benchmark for movie to game translastions!
