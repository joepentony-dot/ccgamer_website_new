Nayphee 2024-12-04 (8/10)
The sequel to the classic "Way of the exploding fist", Fist II builds on it by creating an adventure game to go with an improved fighting game engine. The adventure game won't be to everyone's liking due to the huge amount of walking around with fights happening about as rarely as they did on the Kung Fu TV series. This is really a game you play for the atmosphere and exploration - as you have to find scrolls and their respective temples across the landscape. The power of the scrolls will allow you to pass challenges to reach the volcano of the evil warlord. The fights are a tense affair with death meaning a loss of life and return to the start. While the music is great and suits the game well., it's not all perfect as one or two karate moves can be used to cheese opponents during the fights (especially once you figure out which ones). The background graphics aren't that good either, and the screen scrolling seems like it can't catch up. I still enjoyed this one, but it's a game for the patient.
