I was kinda suprised when I saw SNES' Choplifter III mentioned by one of the reviewers here. They are very similar but Apocalypse has more unsettling atmosphere visually just like Walker. Mowing people on the ground with vulcan machinegun or dropping mines from the chopper is really brutal depiction of war. It's clearly inspired by violent holywood Vietnam war movies like Apocalypse Now, Full Metal Jacket and Platoon. The makers achieved similar feeling in the game which is very impressive. I think that Apocalypse deserves 8 out of 10.

mailman 2008-12-21 (5/10)
"Patience" and "keep your nerves on the leash" - these two comes to my mind when I think of this game. It has great atmosphere, nice graphics but it lacks playability due to some unbelievably high difficulty level. There are only five stages in the game and you have to be extremely focused when playing. Each stage is huge challenge and brings totally different obstacles you need to deal with. At the beginning you fancy all the surprises which were prepared for you by the authors. They make you really feel like being out there, in the Vietnam or Korea sitting in the chopper's cockpit but in the long-term it becomes annoying and irritating when you have no idea who fired and what it was that took you down to the ground with the blast. The first stage is really a struggle due to the long range cannon which fires unexpectedly. The second one is rather much easier but still does not let the player really enjoy the game. Much effort (and time) was put into this game but I have a strange feeling that it was an overkill. It comes on three disks and apart from five levels (rather not long but extremely difficult), it contains no ingame menu, no options screen. I have seen more playable and interesting games which had more data squeezed on one or two disks than this title has on three. It could have been great game but it was pretty much spoiled.
