Bolch 2007-06-03 (10/10)
I have many pleasant memories about this game, as this was the very first adventure that I completed without using any help whatsoever. The best thing in it is the living world around you: all of the villagers have distinct personalities, and they all go around their everyday lives: working, drinking, shopping, talking with you and each other etc. I also loved the carefree, ironic tone of the game and the relatively easy puzzles. The graphics are great, and the many different sounds (barking, the clinging of the blacksmith's hammer) add much to the atmosphere. I must mention the awesome intro, too. Another unique feature of the game is the ability to give complex orders to other characters, which is sometimes crucial to your success ("go to entrance cavern and pull left skull and then return". The game also has some flaws, however: in half of the game, you simply have to walk the village and ask everyone about everything again and again. Also, it's way too short, and some of the - mostly great - jokes seemed a little forced. Still, it's a revolutionary and beautiful adventure that you surely shouldn't miss.
