Takumaji 2008-08-22 (9/10)
Awesome game, very good graphics, sprites and animations. Hacking evil ninjas, nasty beasts and the occasional peasant (just kidding... ) to pieces was big fun. There was nothing like it on the C64, Usagi isn't just another hack-and-slay sidescroller but also offers action-adventure elements and a game environment that changes according to your actions. I didn't know about the Yojimbo comics back then so I found the choice of a rabbit-Ronin a bit weird when I first saw Usagi scrolling over the screen but that didn't stop me from playing the game to bits and pieces. High replayability, excellent visuals and sound (I used to hum the simple but effective in-game tune for hours after I played it) and cool presentation. 9/10.
