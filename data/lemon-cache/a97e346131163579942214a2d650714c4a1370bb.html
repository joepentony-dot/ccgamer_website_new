This is one of those "great, but why"-games.

Great, as in 'magnificent conversion' with superb playability and good sound effects, pretty much glorious visuals - as a conversion, this is definitely fantastic!

But why, as in - but why would anyone choose THIS particular version over the other versions, which include the original Arcade version that can be played pretty much the same as in the arcades (if you have an arcade-controller and something like a VGA2Scart-cable (+Soft15kHz of course) connected to a bright-enough, authentic, curved-surface CRT television) with MAME, and of course..

.. The INCREDIBLE Amiga-version!

When you play this first, and then immediately play the Amiga version right after, you will be impressed out of your socks by the latter. What a massive sound! What great graphics and impacting explosions! What neat parallax-scrolling and what detail in the scenery!

Monk 2014-08-17
This is one of those few C64 games that are actually excellent conversions (or originals), but where an even greater version of the game exists, overshadowing the C64-version, and rendering it obsolete and meaningless.

Some other examples include: The "Turrican"-series (C64 versions were the originals, but the Amiga versions are the DEFINITE versions, I don't think even the most stubborn Taurus will deny this fact), the unbelievably well-made "Midnight Resistance" (just look at the Amiga and Arcade versions, and you won't want to play the C64-version much, except for curiosity, as great as it is), and.. Well, there were some others, but I forgot for now.

A great game, but if you really want to play this exact game, there's really no reason to choose the C64 version instead of the Arcade or Amiga version.

There are other games that were the exact opposite - the Arcade versions can be quite good, but the C64 version might have a definite charm, an original SID tune, or atmosphere, graphics, specific, different playability, that makes it a good choice even if you like the Arcade version. Paperboy, Commando, Bomb Jack - seems to me that most Elite games were like this. And Slap Fight. Ght.
