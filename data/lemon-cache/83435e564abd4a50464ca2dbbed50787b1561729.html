Pavle Majonez 2024-05-04 (7/10)
This is a vastly superior version than the 1983 Parker Brothers one, because it's much more true to the arcade.
You can choose the wave you want to start at, the ship movements and the vector graphics are nicely done on the C64 and there is the Death Star sequence when you destroy it.
The gameplay and the look feel good and the controls aren't that bad.
But, it had inverted controls that can't be changed, and when there's too many objects at once or when you get to the ground turrets and the tower section, the slow down comes in and it makes it difficult to avoid projectiles, or even shoot at them.
Anyways, this is a nice attempt to bring over the vector based coin-op to the C64 and it's quite fun and worth a try.
