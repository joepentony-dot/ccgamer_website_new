Takumaji 2013-05-13 (7/10)
EHIS is a mixed bag for me. It sure is a quality release that is filled to the brim with options, not exactly intuitive but nonetheless well thought-out gameplay and unspectacular yet passable visuals. The first hurdle you have to overcome is the passing and kicking system. A player passes or kicks the ball on release of the firebutton, the longer you hold it, the stronger it will be. This may sound simple on paper but is rather tricky to do during the first few goes. Once you have mastered the basics, zooming around on the field and scoring a goal after a series of well-timed passes feels very rewarding. However, EHIS seems like a simulation first and footie game second to me, I like the sport and soccer games in general but don't really want to put so much effort in them. That's why I prefer MicroProse Soccer over it (my fave Commie footie game) which is way more accessible and ultimately more fun for me. Fans of football sims should give EHIS a try, it might just the game you were always looking for (on 8bit systems, that is). 7/10.
