grenert 2024-12-12 (9/10)
Frequently has to load new data so you're always waiting around for loading times. Other than that, first-person racing on the C64 doesn't get any better. Amazing how well they got the steering to work given that a steering wheel is a continuous analog input, while all the Commie has is a simple on-off Right-Left joystick. So many racing games make me want to throw the joystick in frustration, but not this one. I recently tried this under C128 emulation and the framerate seems to be significantly improved, but even on the C64 it's not bad at all considering the detail (and the fact this was an 8-bit computer from the early eighties!).

White Demon 2024-09-30 (10/10)
Poke 56334,129
this is the command the helpful sales assistant at k-mart gave to me (and demonstrated with the disk instore) to enter into my commodore every time i loaded the game after i took it back complaining it didn't work, as australian distributor ozisoft, in their infinite wisdom, decided to import the original ntsc version of the game into australia without first checking if it needed modification to work on pal c64s!

once i had that poke entered (which changed a single bit in the computer telling the game to load using pal timings instead of ntsc), i had an absolute blast. I played this game a lot, it is the perfect blend of arcade action and f1 simulation, and i still play today. The cockpit view is amazing, making you feel as if you are actually there, with great controls and a proper learning curve for those prepared to put the time in, and the game plays so well with a great frame rate (even better on my c128d, although i didn't even know at the time), much faster than those other racing games on the c64 with a similar viewpoint, and great presentation throughout, from the intro sequence to post-race celebrations (if you did well enough with a podium finish).

like epyx, accolade knew what they were doing with the c64 and made great games every time, and grand prix circuit was no exception. 10/10
