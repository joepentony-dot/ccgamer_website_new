Demon 2015-06-01 (8/10)
This game is probably intended for younger kids, but nevertheless it's pretty good for all.

The idea is for Donald Duck to earn money by taking a few jobs, each of which is a different minigame of its own, then spend the money to buy elements from different stores and build a playground for the nephews (which can then go and play with it).

All the the minigames are actually pretty good and different from each other. The game is designed so that you cannot lose, in the worst case you'll just earn less money and build the playground more slowly, but this means that even very young children can enjoy the game without knowing disappointment. On the other hand, getting a perfect score won't be easy for grown-ups either.
