Devin de Gruyl 2002-03-22
As usual with a home conversion of GORF, the third stage (Galaxian) is strangely absent. None of the myriad GORF ports have ever had this stage - not the VIC-20, not the Atari systems, not Colecovision, and not the C-64 either. Even with this glaring omission, Gorf is an outstanding, multi-level shooter, with four distinct challenges - Astro Battles (which may as well just say Space Invaders on the screen), Laser Cannon (enemy ships fly in formation, with a huge laser cannon at their disposal), Space Warp (shoot ships as they emerge from a black hole), and Flag Ship (destroy the invaders' mothership by hitting its one weak spot). None of the four challenges are great in and of themselves; it's when they're combined in the tapestry that is GORF that the game becomes a classic.
