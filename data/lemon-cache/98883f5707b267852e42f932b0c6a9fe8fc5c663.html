BigM 2025-08-10 (10/10)
Atarisoft Succeed in Early Arcade Ports!

The Good
Another successful early arcade port. This time for the C64. Really nice use of colour…..take a load of those colourful laser shots….cool!….. Really nice sprite work, too. Some nice arcade like sounds, too. Notice the signal that an Alien has got hold of a humanoid…. Oh no….. Hurry!….save them!

The Bad
I’m a former C64 owner but this is emulation. I chose Novice…..seems still kinda hard…..oh well………….Let me address some complaints people say,”It’s too slow; 2600 is way better.”………yes it’s slower than most ….embrace the differences…it’s still fun. 2600 is better?! Give me a break! While successful the inferior graphics and crazy flickering disqualifies it.

The Bottom Line
That means I had to move slowly and had to use warping and bombs to succeed! Being slow feels different but still a fun game with terrific audio visual!
