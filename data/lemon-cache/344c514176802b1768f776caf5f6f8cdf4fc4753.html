Monk 2014-11-02 (1/10)
As for the game itself..

I never cared for the spectrum graphics style anyway - it was too colorless and depressing (just look how much black there is in these graphics).

Pyjamarama could've been a fun, little game, if it had been clear as to what the heck to do, and how to do it - and if it hadn't been so inhumanly hard.

How anyone could figure out which objects you had to switch at which time to which other objects, was completely beyond me - was it just memorization, trial and error, or was there a logic to it?

Pyjamarama does have a strange, little atmosphere to it, but of course nothing major, like Maniac Mansion, Way of the Exploding Fist, Bruce Lee or Wizball.

Why did they call it Pyjamarama? I guess, because it's a sort of 'drama' that you have to get through wearing pyjamas (they seem to be called 'pajamas' in the USA usually, though).

"Rama" also means "mad / crazy" in Pitjantjatjara.
