Unfortunately the Amiga and C64 versions of Shinobi were fairly ropey conversions. Although this version certainly outshines the dismal affair on the Amiga.

At least here the framerate is full on, the graphics are rather colourful and the controls don't get in your way.

For some reason the programmers decided that just touching an enemy is instant death, which is plain silly. The sound is sparse, spot effects and no music.

If you want 8-bit Shinobi then play the Master System version. IMO there isn't a more playable version on any other platform.
