Thelead 2024-12-11 (3/10)
A poor space shooter with little to recommend even on release it was archaic in the extreme when put up against games like Aztec Challenge, Forbidden Forest and Manic Miner. It's playable but inferior to literally hundreds of other shooters on the system. People complaining about the low review scores in the magazines really are clueless. Compare this to Alleykat, Nemesis, Iridis Alpha or Parallax which were all released in 1986 and suddenly giving this 9/10 looks breathtakingly silly.

Indeed, this is not a bad game at all, for 1983: four different enemy-blasting stages + rock blasting/dodging stages in the middle. Simple & addictive game, with enemy attack patterns reminding me of Phoenix from the arcades.
I am pretty sure Zzap! 64 slated this game because they compared it to games from 1986 - obviously superior - and this is clearly a mistake on their part.
Maybe publishing a 1983 game in 1986 and charging more-than-a-budget price was a mistake as well...
