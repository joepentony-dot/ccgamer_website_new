Ravyn 2004-04-19
Very addictive game for me. I love the randomly generated dungeons (did Blizzard really copy that, or was it just coincidence?), and the ominous Jaws-like theme when a creature was nearby. It was even scarier if the creature was in an unexplored area, because you knew you had to explore there, but you didn't want to. The only gripes I have about the game are that you can't move when the sound is playing and that the fights are not at all interactive... But it's kinda neat to see "growl... Slash", etc in the top left corner as your knight fights whatever enemy he's fighting.
