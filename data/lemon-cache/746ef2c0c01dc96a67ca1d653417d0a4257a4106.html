Nayphee 2024-02-16 (6/10)
An iconic game because of it's weirdness and relation to it's similarly offbeat stage-play and film. Exploring the castle is fun at first, but sadly the game's brutal difficulty is going to leave you very frustrated instead of *ahem*... Liberated, which seems a bit off-brand.
"Let's do the Time Warp again" is quite accurate for this game, because it's close to impossible to complete within the time limit! Sometimes you just have to get lucky with the placement of the parts you need to collect and hope they're all not too far away. Then you have to hope you don't get your path annoyingly blocked, or have get your clothes ripped off by one of the characters, which slows you down further. Then of course there's a couple of characters who can unfairly kill you dead in one shot after all your hard work.
"It's not easy having a good time!" - you got that right!
