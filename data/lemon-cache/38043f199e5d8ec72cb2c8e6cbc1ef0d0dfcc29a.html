Ocean put more effort in the presentation and the graphics are slightly better than the previous version; also the game design seems more complete and faithful to the arcade. The gameplay though is even worse, with sluggish moves and horrible collision detection, making it a chore to play through and no fun at all. The other version, while poor, at least played smoothly. What a missed opportunity. Still, the first DD game sucked on every home computer anyway, so C64 users were not alone in their misery.
