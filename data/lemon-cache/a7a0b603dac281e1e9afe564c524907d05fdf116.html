One of the few games that use smooth scrolling and do not rely on blocky multi-color mode but instead offer full detailed hi-resmode. Why such a small number of games use hi-res mode on the C-64, that is the question... Sadly the game itself is rather uninspiring and boring.

olympic_skier 2008-07-03 (7/10)
Unlike other joystick-wiggle games, this one goes in circle, and as far as I can recall, you go with by the speed of the bicycle. That means, it isn't at all pure joystick killer like decathlon, but with an inventive, challenging control! The music is nice... It all fits together. The levels/tracks seemed long and arranged with care.

Fruitbat 2004-07-21
How the hell this ever got released i'll never know! Like the 1500 meters in Daley Thompson's Decathlon (which everyone ALWAYS complains about) except it's longer. Yes you get to steer your little bike around the track, but it's basically waggle, waggle, waggle, fire & turn, waggle, waggle, waggle... Zzzzzzzzzz
Actually, I did play it quite a lot for a while, but I think it was purely down the fact that I loved the music. Nothing else to recommend it whatsoever. Download it and listen to the music, but you won't want to play the game for too long. Activision, what WERE you thinking?!!! :-(
Tommy E 2004-01-17
