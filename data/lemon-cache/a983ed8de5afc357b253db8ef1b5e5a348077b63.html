Pyeman 2001-12-30
A game designed around the scene in the film where Deckard (Harrison Ford) chases the android Zhora through crowded city streets and attempts to shoot her in the back without hitting any pedestrians. While extremely difficult, the game is fun to play and I like the city navigation where you drop in on naughty replicants with your hover-car. Worth the download for the great SID adaptation of the Vangelis score alone.
