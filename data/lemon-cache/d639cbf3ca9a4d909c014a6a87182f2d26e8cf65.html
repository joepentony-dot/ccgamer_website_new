Devin de Gruyl 2002-09-16
When I was a kid, this game usually scared me, especially on the higher levels - the Guardian saucers could come from literally any direction, and usually it was from behind (or at least out of line-of-sight). I'd usually get pasted for no good reason, by an enemy that was for all intents and purposes, invisible! You also never knew if that Hulk you just sent your robot out to raid was legit, or if it was a metal-munching alien... An impressively deep game for its day, and it holds up very well today. Lucasarts managed to pull off real-time fractal landscapes on a 1 MHz processor; the effect must be seen to be appreciated. This is something my 800 MHz Athlon cannot do, and the 64 pulls it off without breaking a sweat! A treat for the eyes (though I must be honest and say the original Atari 800 version is a *bit* better looking) and a feast for the gaming palate, Koronis Rift is definitely worth your time... Just watch out for those darn saucers!
