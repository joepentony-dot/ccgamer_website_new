C64 Mat 2025-10-06 (10/10)
What is there NOT to like about the AD&D "Gold Box" games? Huge RPGs covering 6-8 disk sides, with a 3D world view and isometric, tactical battles. Proper role playing with party based combat, and even automapping! You had a massive manual and adventurers journal full of tips, tales, rumours and gear stats. Loads of quests, loads of maps, and the ability to import your party from the previous game, export your party into the next adventure. Just phenomenal.
