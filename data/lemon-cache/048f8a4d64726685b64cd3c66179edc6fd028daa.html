Blizzard 2023-03-15 (8/10)
I’m not really a fan of pinball, but this came up in the games competition so I played it and found it quite fun in small bursts.

Pros: Graphics, sound and music are very impressive along. The game mechanics are nicely done.

Cons: Can be frustrating, especially when you’re on a run and the ball flies helplessly towards the side lanes and there’s nothing you can do, but I guess that’s pinball! Also, the Ignition table feels like it belongs in a different game as it's nowhere near the quality of the others.

Overall: 1 table is poor, but you have 2 excellent and 1 above average so it’s good value. As mentioned, even though pinball isn’t my thing you have to appreciate the work that’s gone into this game and put into context that there was nothing else like this at the time. I did have fun once I stopped playing on Ignition!
Overall 8/10.
