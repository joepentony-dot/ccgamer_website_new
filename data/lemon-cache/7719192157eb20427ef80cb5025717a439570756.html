Nayphee 2023-11-17 (4/10)
Cobra is ruined by abysmal collision detection and wonky controls. Enemies, bullets, jumping, and platform collision detection are all completely off. The graphics are cartoonishly ugly. As far as game design is concerned, there's also absolutely no excuse for the motorbike running you over the second you start the game without warning. Cobra is still strangely playable though once you learn to work around it's jankiness, and while it is possible to get good at playing - it's still very much a below average game. The Ben Daglish music though, is a perfect rendition of Sylvester Levay's "Skyline" and deserved much better than this rush-job of a game involving a whole lot of senseless murder.
