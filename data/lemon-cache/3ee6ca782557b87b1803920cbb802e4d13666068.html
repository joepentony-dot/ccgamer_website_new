Stevengton 2025-03-13 (3/10)
Exciting premise bursting with potential ruined by rubbish game design. Castle Colditz is a tedious near-impossible to map maze of identikit rooms and corridors. There's no way to see into a room you want to enter so you always risk running into patrolling Fritz's loving embrace and the guard behavior is generally illogical.
Clumsy controls make entering doorways and climbing stairs a hassle and the constant need to use keyboard for item/inventory manipulation is awkward.
Graphics and animation are poor, sound effects barely existent. The possibilty to save the game would have helped tolerate some of the issues, without it the game is just not worth the effort.

meman887 2011-11-11 (8/10)
Remember playing this game back in 1997 (skidrow cracked copy), when I saw the title of the game on the disk I thought to myself "This looks interesting." Anyway I had a feeling it was something to do with WWII, POW's and so forth from the title itself.

After the game itself loaded showed the title screen that you see on here to the right my first thoughts were correct. But anyway when I started to play it, the only thing that took me a bit getting used to was the movement controls (felt slightly awkward at first) but gotten used to them after a while.

Anyway the only other drawback (first one being movement controls) with this game was the lack of save feature, to which if you screwed up somewhere you would have to collect the stuff over again or worse yet start from the beginning all over again. But to be honest though it did make the game more difficult and challenging nonetheless.

Also there is another game like this but in 3D released in 2002 on PC, XOX and PS2 called "Prisoner Of War". Whilst that one is good too it felt it lacked a few things that this has, what those were I couldn't tell you but I'm sure you'll find out if you ever play it.

Anyway despite the two drawbacks, the game itself is good and you should give it a go and I'm sure you'll enjoy it as much as I have.
