Sternhammer 2021-10-18 (7/10)
It was panned by the then gaming press but whilst the 'Reflex' control system takes a LOT of perseverance I found myself quite liking this and there is a good game in there.
Its a very original spin on the 2000AD Slaine license who in the hands of most developers would have just ended up a scrolling or platform based beat 'em up. So congratulations to Martech for originality and trying something different with the command menu control interface.
Does the gameplay hang together though? Well, no, not entirely and its not going to be on anyone's favourite games list but I did still quite enjoy it despite the high frustration levels caused by the control words entering and leaving the screen so quickly. The location descriptions are on the sparse side as well and certainly don't provide the beautiful atmosphere of say a level 9, Magnetic Scrolls or Infocom adventure.
Graphics are primarily monochromatic but definitely convey a sense of the comic with both the style and excellent paneling effect.
Music is superb and one of my favourite David Whittaker compositions. Its vaguely reminiscent, in parts, of Human Race by Rob Hubbard released two years earlier which I also adore but is more laid back and soothing.
Anyway, the 'reflex' control system is certainly not for everyone but worth a try if you've not come across it before or are a die hard Slaine fan like myself.
7 out of 10.

However, really cool is the fighting system: After some action of your opponent, you have a few seconds to search and click the appropriate counter action in the window. There, this weird menu concept really makes sense and is also quite original. I have never seen sth similar before or afterwards.
Also, you can drink some ale and your hand is staggering. Thus, you will constantly click the wrong commands. Quite funny
