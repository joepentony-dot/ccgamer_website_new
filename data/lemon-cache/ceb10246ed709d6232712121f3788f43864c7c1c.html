Colonel Kurtz 2010-11-30 (7/10)
A game that was way ahead of its time, and that partly suffered the consequences of its originality and being a child of flight simulator specialists. It also did benefit from coming way before the terrible movie, which was being held back by Orion's financial strain. The game's story is likely closer to Frank Miller's script, before it was butchered and toned down (again) for the big screen.

With RoboCop 3, Digital Image Design delivered primitive implementations of features that would become the cornerstones of modern gaming: first-person shooting and versus fighting on a full 3D engine, free-roaming areas, absurd copy protection schemes ... Still a rough gem as several portions of the gameplay could have used some refinement. The on-foot FPS sections were particularly frustrating due to the inability to strafe and therefore to dodge grenades that were an instant kill.*

Anyway, praise goes to DID for the courageous effort and still managing to make the best RoboCop Amiga game from the all-out worst RoboCop movie.

(*) Instakill grenades were corrected in the Hit Squad reissue, aka RoboCop 3D. Play that one instead, as it also did away with the hardware dongle.
