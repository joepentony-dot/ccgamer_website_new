COMMENT: The voice belongs to the girlfriend of Bob Stevenson, the artist on Myth. "Bob had a lot of technical savvy for an artist and he proved it here by hooking up a really old games machine (an Atmos Oracle maybe?) with a mike and recording the sample data from his girlfriend. I heard later that he was a bit of a tyrant about it and made her do takes and retakes until she was really fed up. :-) I only ever got to hear the final few cuts, but I was delighted and we worked an extra weekend to get them into the game right on the deadline." (Comment by Peter Baron, programmer of Myth)
