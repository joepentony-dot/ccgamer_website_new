nicky99 2010-07-14 (10/10)
Its funny, but whilst many people have commented on how this has not dated particularly well, I totally disagree. For me, Uridium is one of the greatest shoot em ups ever made, and one that I happily play on emulation to this day. The beauty for me is that it is just one of those games that gives you immense satisfaction to beat the previous high score (like a lot of retro stuff) and so in a way it maintains an incredible timeless quality (rather like Defender). It has a raw-ness to it, and although it has 15 or so levels it some how managed to feel infinite and unbeatable. (which for me it is actually, as I have never clocked it properly in 25 years of playing it)
Visually its a real treat too,
Lets face it, even 2D power houses like the NeoGeo that came many years after the C64 never had anything that moved as quickly or as smoothly as this. It never slows up, it never stutters, and that helps the player feel in total control. It tests the reactions like very few games can. Love it!
