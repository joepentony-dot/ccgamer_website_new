Sternhammer 2021-10-08 (8/10)
I preferred the Street Hassle title so I'm going with that. Anyway STREET HASSLE has the trademark expanded pixelated sprites favoured by Beam Software and they're used to great comic effect. An hilarious (at least initially), well animated cast of adversaries and a great main character batter away over well drawn backgrounds. A very entertaining scrolling beat 'em up that's great for short bursts of cathartic humour laden aggression.
Not one for the woke snowflakes though.

Monk 2012-09-07
Mv64, you got it wrong. This is the ORIGINAL release. So your version with the childish name is the one where he has actually grown and messed up the hair that was actually neat and orderly here.

I think the best idea of the game is that there are different moves for every level. That gives you a big incentive to finish a level, just to see what the next moves will be like. And they are always so hilarious, you are never disappointed!

The only way this game could have been made more politically incorrect is if it had featured young women to beat up, especially feminists would have been nice.

This is one of those rare occasions where a game succeeds in being both humorous and playable. There are other games too, like Maniac Mansion, but usually it's one or the other. This is the closest the C64 ever came to Final Fight.
