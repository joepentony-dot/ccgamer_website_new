hazzard 2024-02-14 (9/10)
When it comes to simulation games on the C64, this is the only one I recall playing to simulate a steam train, and what an amazing simulation it is. You'll need to keep an eye on furnace temperature, water temperature, fuel level, water level, the condition of your brakes, the amount of coal remaining... It's just mind-boggling how immersive the whole experience is. You also need to manage your route and navigation (which entails having switches flipped at junctions) as well as ensuring enemy fighter jets don't inflict too much damage on you. The entire game takes place during twilight, which coupled with the constant "puff puff puff puff" from your steam engine in the background makes the entire thing feel so surreal to play. Highly recommended👍

Sigmoid 2016-07-25 (9/10)
This was one of the few games on the Commodore where it was perfectly clear what you need to do, the game was completable, and the production value was generally high.

It was, pretty much, almost like a *modern game* in the age of arcade coin munchers and off-the-wall WTFfests.

Bottom line, it's a very well-done resource management / strategy game with shooting sections. Very atmospheric and fun. Also as a little kid in the 80s, driving a train was so much cooler than flying a spaceship.
