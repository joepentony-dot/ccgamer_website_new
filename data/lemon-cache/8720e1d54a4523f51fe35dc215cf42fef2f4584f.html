Ola 2017-11-16 (6/10)
Though I like the "prepare to qualify" fanfare it is both monophonic (or perhaps has two voices in unison?) and VERY DIFFERENT to the arcade fanfare.

The arcade fanfare has two distinct voice parts, neither is on the C64 version. The main melody voice has only slight similarities to the single voice melody of the C64 version. The bass part is missing on the C64.

Weird, as all other versions from Atarisoft has the correct melody and the bassline, e.g. The very similar Atari 400 version. The Vic 20 version is the only exception as it has the bass line but the melody is simplified though clearly based on the arcade.

The arcade fanfare I FANTASTIC but the funny thing is I don't remember being disappointed when I first heard the C64 fanfare in 1984. On the contrary, I remember thinking: "Ah! Prepare to qualify! Just like being in the arcade only the voice sample is missing! "
