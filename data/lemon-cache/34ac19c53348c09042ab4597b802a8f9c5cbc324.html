Nayphee 2023-11-03 (7/10)
Like later Atari coin-ops, the original Pit-Fighter arcade game was simply a coin-eater. It's main attraction being the digitized characters and brutal violence to attract the player and stop them from noticing that there's actually no gameplay. You would mash buttons and hope for the best until you're bundled out at the two-minute mark before it demanded more credits. (Arcade Mortal Kombat would eventually fix that mistake and become a hit)
Without the flashy graphics, the C64 version of Pit-Fighter was never going to be a great game. Given what they had to work with the programmers actually made something that's sort of playable and fun for a while - if you can tolerate the juddery character movement and the weird joystick controls. There's a touch of skill involved in trying to stay alive long enough to at least beat the game, but the strategy isn't that deep.
A second player can be brought in so double the fun.
The music is good, if a little repetitive, and if you have the original disk version, you can have some more fun by making a backup and renaming all the character files so that the game loads them all in wrong. Then you can play as any of the enemy characters versus the good guys in a kind of bootleg "championship edition"!
