Notes:The tape version omits several features; the Little Computer Person is generated from scratch every time the game is started up (not only on the first boot), and thus does not go through the "moving in" sequence seen on other versions. Also, on cassette versions the Computer Person has no memory, and does not communicate meaningfully with the user, and the card games, such as poker, cannot be played.

tomarone 2013-01-29
This game was developed (all art, music, coding) in northern New Jersey for about a year before Activision bought it. David Crane needed another hit after his Pitfall series, they had money, so they bought all rights and credits from my employer. I was the musician, and coder with others at the company. It was a difficult project for us in the early mid '80's. But you can tell the artist's style by the other games we did.
