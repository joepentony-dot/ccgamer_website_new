mailman 2025-04-05 (5/10)
The game is very difficult to rate. On one hand there is beautiful graphics and super fluid animation peppered with a really nice music. On the other hand there is a gameplay. At the first glance there is nothing wrong with it but after a while all the "Golden Axe" fans will notice that the game is extremely easy and very dreadfully cut down. In one moment there can be no more than two figures on the screen so basically all the fights are one on one instead one on many (at least three in other versions). Such an approach very quickly becomes repetitive and mundane. All the correct timings in dealing the blows that exist in other versions do not exist in this one.

To sum up, the game probably could not meet C64 requirements and coders did what they could in order to give this game to C64 players. Graphics, music and animation are outstanding but do not go with the gameplay that is simply bland and not entertaining.
