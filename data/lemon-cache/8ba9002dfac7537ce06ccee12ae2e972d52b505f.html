Delta is one of the finest shmups out there. Amazing game for its time. I first saw it in Thomas Soft (RIP) headquarters near Stournari Str. In downtown Athens and it had blown my mind.

(+) single load (!)
(+) amazing design for the 1987 standards.
(+) the smoothest animation in the world. I don't know how did Stavros manage to code such a set of incredible sprite movements in 1987.
(+) Just look at the magnificent player sprite with the unique z-axis rotation, look at the space-worm's capricious movement and check closely the peculiar enemies' patterns.
(+) the Delta legacy of color selections, the sprites with the organic color and the metallic tint that influenced Armalyte and paved the aesthetic way for a lot of space shooters afterwards.
(+) Rob's epic SID masterpiece and sound FX


(-) The unexpanded potential. Imagine what could Stavros Fasoulas could produce with a multi-load system, with more time available and some people to help him.

COMMENT: "[...] The Delta music loader and ingame music was Gary Liddon's idea. [...] He was the producer at Thalamus at the time. He told Rob Hubbard to make the ingame music like the 2nd track from Dark Side of the Moon by Pink Floyd." (Info from Matt Furniss.) "The small jingles are all small clips from Sanxion and Romeo/Juliet music. They were all supposed to be for short stingers such as end of level, extra life etc..." "Delta was based on this minimalist composition technique inspired by Glass and a bit of Pink Floyd. It was quite hard to do and required some custom code to the driver to do it. The music was tedious to debug. The other Delta stuff was more conventional - I quite liked the other tunes. Delta was spread over a 2 week period....." (RH) According to Hubbard, Kentilla and Delta were the most complicated one to compose, they took the longest time to do and they both drove him insane.
