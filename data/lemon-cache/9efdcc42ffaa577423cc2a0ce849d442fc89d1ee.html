Nayphee 2023-11-22 (6/10)
For a budget game it was quite passable. First thing that grabs you is the amazing Rob Hubbard music with digitized electric guitar. The games themselves are nothing too special. "Space Invaders" is ripped off the PET version. "Astroids" (sic) is hard to play because of the size of the rocks on the small screen. "Snakes" and "Space Wars" are only fun with a second player.
Overall it's a nice package but it's no great shakes really. There's not much here for the solo player. Lazy Jones this is not.
