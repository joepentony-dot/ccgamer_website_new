qazi 2003-12-23
"Boring and senseless"
Excuse me? Iridis Alpha not only provides a treat for the senses with excellent scrolling, brilliant colours, and wonderfully appropriate sound effects, but it demands concentration if you are to master the game and it's rules.

You need to be completely switched on, and keep in mind energy, entropy, the current planet you are on and what half of the screen you are in if you wish to unlock the other planets. You also have to know when it is safe to land on the core (bear in mind that you are more vulnerable to attack when on the core), and understand that senseless blasting is rarely the best approach, especially when it comes to Licker Droids.

Real players can even attempt to get to the bonus stages (which I doubt the anti-IA brigade even knew about) so as to get even more points before returning to the main battle and hopefully unlocking another planet in one of the screen's halves; and the non-linearity gives the feeling of a whole universe, rather than a standard linear level progression system.

And "Foron" won't even justify his argument! My guess is that Iridis Alpha's complex ruleset is disliked by those who can't understand it.

At least give it a bit more time before mouthing off about how apparantley crap it is: I had no instructions for the game... Despite this I PERSEVERED and gradually began to understand how everything works: more than what the dissenters did.
