This just didn't do it for me. I guess I am not as big a fan as others who wrote a review here. To me the game is fun, don't get me wrong but really is lacking in many areas.

The games are repetitive and the annoying aspects are just that. Annoying. But the idea behind the game is there and it will capture the flavor of the stooges in a simple and innocent type of game. It has a few jokes, trivia and some mini games that will keep you amused for a short while.

The play control can be annoying and the repetitiveness of doing the same things over and over during your 30 day adventure can get tiresome too. But for a simple and easy game this is the way to go. Its a little below average though as it just isn't enough for my tastes. 5/10.
