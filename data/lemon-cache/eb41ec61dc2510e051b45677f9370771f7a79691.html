compnded 2024-10-30 (3/10)
The object of the game is to fend off waves of the Evil Dead until acheiving a score of 100,000 at which point the Book of the Dead appears which can be desposited into the fire to complete the game. Certain weapons protect you better than others and I think more health is preserved if you face enemies as they hit you. The claustrophobic nature of the game provides an interesting interpretation of the film's taut atmosphere and the idea probably looked good in theory. However, the combat is deeply unsatisfying and no new elements of gameplay are added at any point. It just boils down to a repetative points chaser.

Chris Neary 2003-01-08
I did the graphics in about 1983 for this game (Don't laugh!) I was working on Halloween (from the film) and had it half finished when it was cancelled. Mary Whitehouse was doing a thing about XXX rated films so the distributers didn't order many copies. I think it only sold around 5000 copies, so it might be a collectors item. Richard Lienfellner went on to greater things (He's VP of EA now), but I, eventually gave up programming in about 1988. I'm amazed that there are still copies of this game around. And yes I still have the Evil dead T-Shirt from the game.
Chris Neary
