There is a way to pass the "blocking car bug" that occurs in further levels.
If you move our Mountie to the extreme left side of the screen and keep walking, you can pass any object that is placed in your way. Just make sure to stop walking in a object free area, to avoid getting stuck.
Enjoy.
p. S. I always thought, people discovering those workarounds are either crazy or have to much time. Now I am one of them. ;-)
