nicky 2014-07-22 (10/10)
Absolutely love this piece of retro genius. Only discovered it recently but what a brilliant game. Gfx and sound fine, but where it really excels beyond any previous bowling on any other system is the subtle skill required, and the hint of random element it introduces in terms of how the lanes play a bit differently each time, meaning that you could never just point, click, and get a strike every time thus avoiding the failing of every single bowling game in existence. Very cleverly done indeed, and oddly compulsive. 10/10 if you enjoy sports games. No system since has depicted a better representation of its genre, and that is surely the hallmarks of a true retro gem.
