Nayphee 2023-10-30 (8/10)
It's quite rare when an computer port of an arcade game is more enjoyable that it's inspiration, but the Commodore 64 version of Spy Hunter achieved this. The physics as you bump into other cars is on-point, and the lack of a high gear that trashes your car easily in the arcade version is missing here. Your car is more flexible at handling threats from ahead as well as from behind. The end result is a highly enjoyable and playable game that remains a classic. Sure it's a little bit rough around the edges (especially on PAL systems), but this game keeps you coming back to it.
