One of the few 'menagerie of subgames' film licenses which actually succeeded. The first level is a Bionic Commando ripoff, and probably the most fun. Especially knocking out the thugs with your batrope - very satisfying. The batmobile level is a strange 'avoid 'em up' thing which would probably become monotonous as a full game, but as a three minute interlude is good fun here. Ditto for the mastermind level. The batwing stage is the weakest and probably should have been a lot shorter, but things pick up again with final cathedral stage, which is another Bionic Commando level, but suitably more intense than the first. The graphics and sound are well done throughout. It suffers slightly from being easy to finish, and is vastly overshadowed by the amazing Amiga version, but it's still well worth a download. 8/10.
