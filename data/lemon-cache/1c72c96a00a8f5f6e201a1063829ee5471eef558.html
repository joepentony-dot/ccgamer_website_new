Rekrul 2012-03-26
This could have been a decent conversion if not for the idiotic choice of controls. In order to turn, you have to hold down the fire button and move the stick left/right. This means that you can't strafe. It also means that you will end up turning at the absolute worst time because you meant to shoot and then quickly move to dodge enemy fire.

I realize that this method was used to accommodate a one-button joystick, but they should have offered players a choice of using a key on the keyboard for turning. At least then you could have hit the space bar with your foot. As it is, the game is pretty much unplayable.
