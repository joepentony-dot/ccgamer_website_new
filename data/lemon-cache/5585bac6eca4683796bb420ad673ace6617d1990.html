PopoCop 2017-08-24 (6/10)
It's a data disk that pretends to be a sequel. There are new levels with new graphics and few new sounds but the game engine is the same. Bugs from first CF are still there (shooting through walls or whole buildings). There are also new bugs - enemy soldier standing on the ground can throw a granade on a roof of high building (very bad pseudo 3D system). Storyline is weird, some ideas are stupid (medieval people using machine guns and bazookas). Many gfx and sound effects are out of place (the aliens scream like humans, UFO sounds like helicopter).
There is only ONE improvement - you can quit from game to main menu and restart current mission.
Cannon Fodder 2 looks like an unfinished game (probably rush job to publish it before Christmas 1994).
