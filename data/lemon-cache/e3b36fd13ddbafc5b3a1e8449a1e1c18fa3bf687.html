GSmith 2004-10-21 (10/10)
Find the combination to the safe by activating the flashing shields and then shooting at teachers with your catapult to make them reveal a number. This game is one of the best ever, captures perfectly the atmosphere of a school, is full of believable characters such as the 'trendy', who you can imagine wanting to be thought of as one of the lads at the sixth-former disco. There's also the cane-wielding headmaster, the terrifying old history master and the geeky-looking science master. Endless fun involves avoiding and biffing the school bully, writing naughty things on blackboards and avoiding the wrath of various teachers as you attempt to lay your hands on that school report card. A genuine classic. I can't understand why the sequel 'Bak to School' (which included putting mice in a girls' school and making flowers grow in order to scale the school gates) was never converted onto the C64.
