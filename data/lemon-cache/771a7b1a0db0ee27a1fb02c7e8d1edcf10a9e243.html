This was one of my "go to" shooters as a kid on the humble C64; I kept coming back to it over and over again and loved it. It is very faithful to the arcade with all the levels and bosses present and the gameplay gets quite frantic in the later levels. The lack of the "emergency tumble" move does make the game somewhat awkward though, since it's all too easy to get stuck on the edges of the screen with no way out. It always used to bother me slightly that the "victory dance" after clearing the level doesn't occur with this conversion - that part on the arcade always used to make me chuckle! Overall though, a fun shoot 'em up ✌️
