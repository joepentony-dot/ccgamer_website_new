Deist 2016-11-01 (3/10)
Let's sum it up: the graphics _are_ good (Pete Lyon was talented and renowned as such in those days), as are the music/SFX. The rest is bad. Not atrocious, but bad nonetheless. No wonder! Steve Bak coded this thing! Steve Bak was among the most despicable coders back then: kept claiming his love for the ST and how crap the Amiga was in every interview, and obviously would never've considered coding on an Amiga, so did all of his games code on the Atari ST and ported it as is.
In the case of this game (as with all others), it resulted in 0 original or fun ideas, boring game mechanics, dull and repetitive levels, badly managed and very uneven difficulty, for an ultimately pointless game.

As for the someone who stated below that the movie is as bad as the game, well, some people just can't tell great from dull, but that flick became a classic and rightfully so (look out for the documentary/Making Of coming soon!).
