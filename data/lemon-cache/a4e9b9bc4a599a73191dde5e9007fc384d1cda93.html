Colonel Kurtz 2018-06-15 (8/10)
For 20-25 years I never thought I'd rate this game less than a 9 or 10, but as I scroll through Team 17's history with the Amiga, I realise more and more how they had brilliant developers but not so great game designers. Technically and artistically, Project-X went to great heights and still blows me away to this very day, even with a first level that looked less spectacular than the following ones (I only got to level 4 twice).

We were in awe of its great difficulty, not realising that sometimes being unforgiving is a side-effect of flawed game/level design. The excessive power drain upon loss of a life, sometimes nearly impossible to recover from due to an uneven distribution/placement of power-ups (possibly the only flaw Battle Squadron had) and the scarcity of defensive measures were all factors that could - and should - have been tweaked better. These and other design choices create invisible dead ends, skillfully hidden by impeccable programming. Whatever good the Special Edition did in that department came at the price of content.

By the time they got wiser with game design (Worms, ATR...) it was too late, but still most of the earlier Team-17 games are little gems for one reason or another. Not being the best doesn't necessarily strip Project-X of its honorary position.

I'd say Disposable Hero probably still holds the torch in the H-scrolling genre on this platform, but I haven't spent enough time on Apidya yet.

Dan Locke 2010-05-18 (4/10)
This could have been (at best) a decent Gradius-style game, but there's just one small problem: powering up your ship makes it uncontrollable! You have to choose between the ability to kill enemies and the ability to dodge their fire - why not be able to do both?

Aside from that, it's consistently mediocre. The graphics are well-drawn but have a surprisingly low framerate, the sound effects are clear but feel inadequate in the absence of actual in-game music (a major flaw for a game released in 1992), and the enemy patterns are dull, artless, and cheap next to those in such superior games as Disposable Hero, Apidya, and Battle Squadron. It's slick, like every other Team 17 game, but that's about it.
