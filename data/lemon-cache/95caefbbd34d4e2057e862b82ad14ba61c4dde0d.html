Yet another person who had never heard of this game until it was stuck to the front of issue 42 of Zzap! 64.

I loaded it up not expecting much and found myself drawn into it. Probably one of the first sandbox games I had played, where you could just wander about trying things. I spent hours playing this game, the atmosphere felt unrushed and the music definitely added to this. This would have been sometime in October, so the start of the Autumn early evenings made it feel even more of a cosy experience When I listen to it now, it takes me straight back to playing this in my bedroom as a teenager.

A very harsh review by Zzap in my opinion.

Time Tunnel is a game with an amazing feel and atmosphere to it that it’s hard to put your finger on.
