yaztromo 2015-03-01 (5/10)
This is a poor effort that could've been much better.

Graphically Nemesis looks appalling. I can't remember another C64 game looking as blocky and pixelated as this. The backgrounds are non-existent, devoid of any life and the foreground extremely low res and lacking in colour.

The sprites are no better, looking terribly blocky, animated badly and move woodenly like they need the toilet.

Sound wise there's not much to speak of. The swish of your sword and the sound it makes on contact with an enemy is satisfying, but these are just about the only sounds you'll ever hear. Given that most of the time there is only silence, you'd think they'd include some ambience or a suitable 2 channel sound track to give some atmosphere.

The controls are awkward, moving around feels like wading through mud. Attacking enemies has been hobbled by the developers as much as possible presumably to increase the difficulty artificially. You can't attack while moving and while in the air. So stand still and press the fire button twice (yes twice) to swing your sword. It's a bad joke. In tight areas with lots of enemies you don't stand a chance. Also if you're too close you'll miss and while ever an enemy touches you your life drains. Even if it's only part of their foot as they walk above you on another platform. Given that you only have one life, it all makes this game too difficult.

The exits aren't marked, you have to try moving off the screen in one of the four directions.
