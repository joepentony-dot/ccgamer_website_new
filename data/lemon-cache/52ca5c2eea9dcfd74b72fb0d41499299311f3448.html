yaztromo 2014-01-19 (6/10)
A poor game that features one of Hubbard's most recognisable and catchiest tunes.

The gameplay itself is let down by pixel perfect jump puzzles that if you get wrong nearly always lead to getting stuck and having to restart the entire game.

If they'd put in a mechanism where you can restart a screen if you screw it up and thrown a couple of lives in it would've been a very nice game worthy of an 8 or a 9.

6/10
