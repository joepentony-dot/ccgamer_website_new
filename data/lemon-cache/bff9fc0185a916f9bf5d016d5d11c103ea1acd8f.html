From a glance, this conversion of SEGA's After Burner II is alright. While the European version by Software Studios looks to be more loosely based off the cabinets, Weebee Games's port, from the visuals and gameplay controls alone, is an approximation of what the Master System version would look like ported to Commodore computers. It's a sensible way to do things, since this 8-bit hunk of junk can't handle scaling nor floating point properly.

The solid music by Jeroen Tel makes the port worth it out of everything else, though.
