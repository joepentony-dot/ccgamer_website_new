MartinJSUK 2023-04-20 (7/10)
From playing this again nowadays it's a lot better than I remember it, though I still haven't progressed that far. It manages to create a real action-movie feel, with some lovely (though often unintuitive on first play) set-pieces like the boulder dash at the start, and clever use of the dynamite to blast gaps in walls or kill enemies without disturbing the bullet caches they guard. With practice you learn to anticipate the situations which feel at first like memory-tests, such as the motion-triggered bullets which can be avoided if you grab the token from the lower ledge, as seen near the start. The black and white mode is a nice novelty too. Turn your adult cynicism off, and learn to think like the designers, and its all quite good fun.

The slightly clunky push-scrolling does create issues at times, with spikes so often just out of reach, and the screens resetting when you exit them (which can be done accidentally) is a bad side-effect of this, if you run out of dynamite you can be completely stuck, and the overall difficulty is too high. There's a place for games designed this way alongside reflex-driven ones, and I think the good outweighs the bad. Nebulus' memory-test elements annoy me much more for some reason (maybe the sheer charm RD has, or the garish colours in Nebulus?), yet so many people who hated this seemed to love that.
