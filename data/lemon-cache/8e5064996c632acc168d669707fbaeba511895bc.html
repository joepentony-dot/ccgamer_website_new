yaztromo 2013-11-29 (9/10)
I never had this game back in the day and have only just discovered it now.

The graphics are nothing to look at but who cares when the gameplay is as solid as this.

The engine is really forgiving if you don't get your jumps pixel perfect or avoid that enemy with split second timing so frustration about cheap deaths will never creep in. Whenever you die (bar the odd enemy appearing right next to you) you'll know it was your own fault.

The sound makes the game even more addictive with perfect effects for collecting, jumping and dieing.

And about dieing. It's actually fun to die on this game, as your lifeless body bounces it way down the screen you can actually still collect items and finish the level!

You can also set the game speed according to your abilities. So if you're getting on a bit you can slow everything down

Perfect old skool C64 game 9/10 from me.
