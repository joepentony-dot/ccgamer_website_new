Nayphee 2023-10-27 (3/10)
There's miles of walking forever across boring backgrounds until something happens, and when it does, the fights have no interesting gameplay depth. You end up trading blows until one of you disappear into a wisp of smoke through attrition, as there's almost no way to dodge attacks. Then it's back to more boring walking again until the next fight, over and over and over again.
There are dragons in the sewers - that cheaply hurt you no matter what you do. Worst game design is that health and weapons show up randomly - critical to survival and quite rare - meaning you might win or lose depending on luck of the draw! Best of luck if you get shot up by a gun wielding assassin at the very start of the game because you were not lucky enough to be given a weapon to fight back! The end boss is also an unfair nuisance - requiring at least Jack or Shen to survive with a weapon.
It's just a war of attrition boredom, and luck. There's actually more of a game in micro-managing your three men and their abilities as you can switch between them. They will inevitably get hurt and close to death, so you have choices: "Do I use Jack until he finds a gun and then save him for later to shoot the more dangerous enemies? Who needs the health the most? Which character should get this strength potion?" etc. Nice gameplay concept if the rest of the game was any good - except it's not. There not even any music, just "bang bang zap" sound effects.
