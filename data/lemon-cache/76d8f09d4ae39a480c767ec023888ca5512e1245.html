Nayphee 2024-03-06 (7/10)
The first game I ever played on my own C64 back in 1987 when I got it as a pack-in game along with International soccer, Howzat, and America's Cup. It's a very playable clone of SEGA's early racer: Pro Monaco GP, with the addition of a twisting track section called "the Esses". Play is via paddles, as this allows you to swerve as fast as your wrist will let you. Let's hope the pots in your paddles haven't failed and gone shaky on you!
Just a whole lot of simple fun here as you go for a high score until you run out of time.
