yaztromo 2014-12-15 (6/10)
Presentation 8/10:
The presentation is fantastic. An attractive title screen, cycling against a very colourful high score table. In the background happy military style music plays.

The same can be said for the high score entry. Good music, good graphics, well designed name entry system. Definitely adds points to this game.

Graphics 6/10:
The in game sprites are well detailed. Enemy planes look and move great, especially the larger ones.

The backdrops are disappointing, there could have been much more detail. Check out that sea, it's just flat constant blue, no little ripples or glints. The land is a little better, but lacking in variation.

Sound 9/10:
No complaints. Solid believable SFX, if only a little tinny and great in game music that plays at the same time as effects. Excellent use of 3 channels. All the music is well composed.

Controls 6/10:
Fast and responsive movement. No auto-fire though and it is very sorely needed.

Gameplay 2/10:
The enemy planes and bullets just move too fast. There is no dodging in this game as it's close to impossible. Instead the whole thing is a memory test for each wave. Also planes come from behind and insta-death you unfairly. If an action game becomes a pure memory test then you know things are bad.

I like the few power-ups, but they don't do much to ease the pain.

Lastability: 3/10:
Once you realise how cheaply this game kills you, you won't come back much.

6/10
