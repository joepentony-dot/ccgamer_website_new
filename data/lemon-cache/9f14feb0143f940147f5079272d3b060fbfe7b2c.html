Neo-Rio 2021-07-06
Well this is the classic arcade RPG game on the C64. Wildly popular because no longer would you have to shovel coins into the arcade machine. Most of the fun of the arcade machine was the social interaction with other players, arguing with other players about who shot the food, who got greedy with the treasure, and who stunned the other player so you could stay in the game - while other players lined up to playing, waiting for you to run out of coins.
The C64 version only gives you two players and infinite credits, so some of the magic is gone. While it plays fast, early versions had a number of bugs and each move you make seems to be limited on a square grid - so there's something that feels missing with this conversion.
That said, at the time it was a lot of fun even for 2 players and we lapped this conversion up.
