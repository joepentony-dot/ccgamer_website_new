Pembo! 2003-08-25
Absolutely Stonkin'...
I almost ended up with a speccy, but after hearing the soundtrack on this waaaay back in 1983 I was instantly converted to the C64.
Most micro's back then where just bleeps, buzzes, pings, zaps & pops!
The Local WH-Smiths had the C64 running though a Hi-Fi amp & where demo'ing a few games.
THIS definately sold me the 64 as I'm a bigtime audio-junky!
I've had many mispent youthful hours trying to get "Rockin' Rodney" to the next level just to hear what the next track sounded like.
The GFX might not've been anything to right home about but the music which was way ahead of anything on a micro for it's time more that compensated.

A big Hi-5 to Tony ( Gibbo ) Gibson for keeping me boppin' in my youth.
