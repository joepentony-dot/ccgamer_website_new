Certainly nowhere near as much disk accessing as its prequel Darkmere, which was a very good thing indeed.
I played this,through to the end and rather enjoyed it overall, (although the long and unnecessary password system was indeed irritating).
Looked and sounded good too even though it was not a classic.
It was definitely worth buying and playing though back in 1995 when new Amiga games were becoming thin on the ground...
Amiga Power's low score was far too harsh in my opinion. Combat required skill and practice as it would've been no good being too easy.
The One or Amiga Action's score seems about right for me.
Core design were one of my favourite Amiga games publishers as I enjoyed playing nearly all of the games they ever released.
A lovely little, smooth scrolling, good looking, playable game.....
