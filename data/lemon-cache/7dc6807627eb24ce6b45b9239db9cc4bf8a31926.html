Demon 2016-03-16 (8/10)
This is a good conversion of the original arcade, mostly unchanged.

The controls are slightly clunky and they can be frustrating in levels where you have to navigate through narrow mazes. In addition, in some areas the dangerous obstacles (e. G. Cars and falling rocks) don't give you enough time to avoid them, and some semi-random deaths can occur.

But the style and humor are excellent, and really capture those of the cartoon. You'll really want to see what next devious engine the coyote will try to use against you, and in what even more devious way it will turn against him
