Killbot 2006-08-17 (6/10)
This garnered a lot of attention on it's release for the then jaw-dropping graphics, which still impress today. Even on a standard A500/600 it looked stunning but there was a catch - for all the bells and whistles it was really just Asteroids. And thanks to the PD scene, the Amiga had versions of Asteroids coming out of every orifice.

Admittedly it did introduce a lot of new elements including a fun 3D tunnel sequence, loads of enemy craft and the ability to select your level. In fact apart from a wildly fluctuating difficulty level there was really nothing wrong with it, yet for all the time and love that was clearly lavished on the game it was still just Asteroids. And when you could pick up a decent Asteroids clone for under a pound shelling out twenty on this just didn't seem worthwhile.

6/10
