Monk 2015-07-25
Then there are the C64 games that deserve to exist and have enough of their own to qualify as something to play even though an Amiga/Arcade version exists, like Paperboy, Commando, Bomb Jack, Spy vs. Spy, Alien Syndrome, Kung-Fu Master, and so on.

I am not sure where Zak McKracken and the Alien Mindbenders(tm) falls - it's a bit insignificant, as all the versions are really boring, have mazes, focus on money and so forth. First Samurai is a bit boring game anyway - the sequel is a bit better, but ends up also being frustrating in the long run. And although the C64 version is technically superlative, after seeing the glory of brilliant copper effects and the ghostlike, eerie sound and sound effects of the Amiga version, how can you go back to the C64 version?
