Colonel Kurtz 2010-10-31 (9/10)
It's probably harder to judge now than it was when I got it almost two decades ago. If it were all about the eye candy, gut feeling and originality you'd give it a 10/10 by all means.

Innovation came a bit at the expense of a few gameplay aspects, though. For one thing the combat system, albeit unique, was a bit awkward and did not allow for group battles. Then the inventory management was not exactly slick so to speak. Just a flat list where all items weigh just the same, and you get no warnings about how much room is left until it's too late.

Once I had to restart the whole game because I had run out of space right before I could take an essential item from a fallen enemy, and you cannot return later to loot a downed enemy. Given that this is one of those games that use up an entire disk for a single save position, you can tell how frustrating it was.

Still, these downsides fade almost completely when facing the epic intro, superb graphics (both technically and artistically), unique minigames and the other countless treats of this game. Definitely one of the games the Amiga community could be proudest of, too bad it was just *this* close to total perfection.
