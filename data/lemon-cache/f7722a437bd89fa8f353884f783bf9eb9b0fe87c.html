MIK UK 2011-08-24 (10/10)
If you could finish Dungeon Master with your eye's closed, beat Chaos Strikes Back to the wall, felt some what let down by Dungeon Master II then Crystal Dragon was here to see the Amiga out as the Jewel in the Crown of RPG - Dungeon Crawling.

The creators knew we were all Dungeon Masters so what they did with this was to turn the game upside down, inside out, stick a knife in where it hurts the most and kept twisting until there were no more wall's left to build a dungeon! Yes, this game is MASSIVE and very tricky even for Dungeon Pro's. Your worst nightmares that never made it into DM are here in floor design. Using a rope added an extra dimension knowing it may get you some where cool and with a teleport skull could you maybe help your self out from the 5 floors you just dropped down. Thank the makers they added a Magic Map!

This game will keep you on your toes for hours and you will be on the edge of your seat wondering what, when and where next every step of the way. As a Dungeon Master Clone this game is a fitting end of that era for the Amiga knowing that the game that started it all, Dungeon Master was originally called Crystal Dragon!

I feel this has been over looked by the majority of DM fans, for being too hard? Who knows... Still, Crystal Dragon in my collection will always shine as the Dungeon Master of them ALL.
