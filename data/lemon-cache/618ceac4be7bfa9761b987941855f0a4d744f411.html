Devin de Gruyl 2002-03-11
Of all the home conversions this arcade classic has garnered, the C-64 port is by far the best of the "classic" era. With graphics that are virtually indistinguishable from the arcade, responsive controls, and the maddeningly addictive Q*bert gameplay, you can't go wrong. All it's missing is an attract mode! There is only one thing missing, and it's minor - the original arcade machine used a random-noise generator to make Q*bert "talk" when he got nailed. Understandably, he's silent here. Back in the day, owning this cartridge was almost as good as having a real, live arcade game in your basement! It holds up extremely well today, too.
