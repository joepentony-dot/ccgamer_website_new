Jurbo 2006-11-03
Absolutely one of the best games ever on the C64. I spent a large part of summer 1988 with this game, and have to say that this is the single most rewarding gaming experience I have ever had.

Graphics - absolutely beautiful. People are asking for colour - well, which one do you prefer: 160 x 200 resolution with many colours or 320 x 200 with a single colour? I think the beauty in this game lies in the details of the hi-res graphics, in multicolor the graphics would lose all the charming details. Ok, maybe a hi-res sprite overlay could work for the characters, but then the backgrounds would still be blocky.

Sounds -gotta say I love the jingles.

Puzzles & game world - a huge and atmospheric world to explore. Some devious puzzles and a lot of extremely difficult jumping action.

The quite recent PC remake is very good, but I prefer the original C64 audio scheme.
