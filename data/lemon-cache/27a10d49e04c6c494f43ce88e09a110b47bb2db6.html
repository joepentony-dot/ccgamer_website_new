Scrawffler 2008-10-11 (8/10)
Very good game. Unique concept at the time, and addictive too. Bullfrog then went on to create other games similar to this, such as Theme Hospital and Dungeon Keeper - all with the same kind of gameplay, so this was the start of a new and interesting genre at the time.

It's a good thing you can save your progress. It crashes often, and is also a fairly slow-paced game, so you'll want to save your work and continue building your park over a number of days.

For some reason, I have many memories of playing this specifically over the Christmas holidays when I was a kid. How bizarre.
