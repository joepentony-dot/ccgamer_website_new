Need to comment on this one. Back then I tried the game but thought not too much about it. Recently I looked for a game to play with an emulator on a small mobile device, so needed a game that was not too fast and hat relatively big sprites. I rmembered Nebulus and man what a joy to play. You need to try it, BUT only with trainer. This is THE game you WILL need a trained version for (did I mention don't try it without?).
The game as others said is a mixture of reaction and memory test, and yes enemies fire at you. Interestingly the bullets (or touching enemies) doesn't kill you but makes you simply drop to the next level of stairs below. Nice touch BUT this feature is useless because for every level there is a time limit and you won't make it to the top anyway if you fell down more than once or so.
Do your self a favour and try the game, no time limit, endless lifes (doesn't matter anyway) and see every tower as an independent challenge.
That way the game is splendid, 8 points!
