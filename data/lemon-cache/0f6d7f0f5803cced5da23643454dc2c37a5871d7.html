Demon 2018-02-28 (8/10)
A TRULY original game concept.

This game definitely requires to read the instruction first. It's not particularly complicated, as it is based on a few key actions only (mainly: absorb, create, teleport), but without knowing what is your purpose in the game, it's just not possible to play. Once you do know, the first attempts may frustrate you a bit, but as soon as you beat a level you'll be seriously hooked to continue with the next... And your whole life won't be enough to see all 10000 of them!

The creepy atmosphere certainly adds to the game. There is something simply unnatural, insane, out-of-this-world in the perspective of being... What exactly? What kind of "thing" has an existence based on absorbing energy from trees, use it to create shells, transfer its consciousness into them, recycle energy by absorbing its former self, and ultimately taking over the sentinel on top of the mountain, before such sentinel sees you and slowly disintegrate you?
