BigM 2025-07-19 (10/10)
Comparing SNES and Genesis to original Amiga

The Good
I purchased the Amiga game years ago and was very engrossed in this cinematic marvel. One thing I noticed right away is that the music is expanded in the console versions. However, as movie directors know like Spielberg, some cinematic scenes work better without music. The Amiga version’s opening scene has great visuals but the sound effects are superb starting with the screech of tires. Music is added sparingly. The sound effects on the consoles are not as convincing and the music tends to get in the way.

The Bad
I noticed a lot of differences in the worms scene. The SNES version was very easy. The worms did not fall from above. On the other hand, Genesis had extra worms making the scene much more difficult.

The Bottom Line
I recommend the original Amiga over the consoles. If unable to test the Amiga version, try watching the opening scene on YouTube.
