TaoPhoenix 2007-11-17 (9/10)
I almost voted 7, then revised it back to 9.

I never made it past West City as a child because of the previously mentioned "delay tactics".

Twenty years later, I believe it is indeed possible to beat the game "hard way" without the codes. The concept is to "lock value" that none of the Threat Characters can take away.

South: Policeman actually increases your rest by a couple bars. Use this as a "transport" feature in conjunction with the soup kitchen. Stock up on Bones, Bottles, Ticket, and Dimes. In a pinch you can run back to South City, flip $2, and stay overnight. As a child, I never knew that with the haircut, the meat plant actually offers a job.

West: The IRS Man is not on West, so be cautious. Track what day the haircut is needed, prepay a weekly room, load up on tickets again. I preferred not to start Educating until these timed resources were fresh.

East: High School doesn't cost cash, so again, keep those intangible values stocked so you can run back to South City. The IRS can't take away education, so each class stays put.

North: As hinted elsewhere, the investments are already past the IRS's grasp. If you have a lot of patience, you'd only need about three days salary to start dumping it into the investment account. Brutal as the IRS is, he can't get you every time. If you burned a vacation, you could live off one investment by camping out back in South & West again.

This game is *possible* without the codes. But definely Not Fun.

Pyeman 2001-12-30
"Original" is the first word that comes to mind when desribing this game. While excrutiatingly difficult due to the ease in which robbers and IRS men can steal your hard-earned cash, it is still fun to play. The game is quite non-linear and you can make money and progress towards your fortunes in a variety of different ways: Steal bottles from the junkyard by distracting the nasty dog, get a shave and go to work at the local gas station, collect money off the street, etc. Regardless of its shortcomings, I love the game and highly reccomend it to anyone looking for something off the beaten horizontal shooter path.
