Monk 2013-07-07
This game is a nice nostalgy trip, nothing much more.

The graphics are crude, but back in the day, we didn't pay much attention to it, because there was all this exciting atmosphere, and it was one of the rare skateboarding games (together with 720° and Skate or Die), and it was fun to explore the scenery.

It was fun playing this, then going outside to practice actual skateboarding, and then come back to play some more.

However, playing it nowadays, it becomes clear that this is just a simple flag-collecting game.

Really - why do so many games feature collecting? Up'n'Down was similar to this, but from a different perspective. At least Paperboy was the opposite - instead of just mindlessly collecting and collecting stuff, like a capitalist drone-slave, you actually GAVE things to others! (Though of course you had to collect them sometimes, if you ran out)

I guess one could say that this is a simplistic mixture of Up'n'Down and Paperboy - both great classics, unlike Skate Rock.

Even the Daglish tune in the title screen isn't up to his normal standards. A playable, little game, but I doubt anyone would really play this for long if they didn't have a nostalgic feeling about the game.

There are so many so much better games in existence, especially for the Commodore 64.
