bronstein 2018-06-23 (7/10)
I had a go on this one and after 30 years I am willing to revise my first impression. There indeed is much more to this game than its infamous slowness would suggest.

The kick-o-meter is easy to understand but quite a challenge to control. Because it is very difficult to steal the ball from an opposite player, you have to strike your shots precisely to pass the ball to your own players. Once you handle that, you can organise nice combinations and eventually score some beautiful goals.

That said, the slowness does make the game a bit of a yawn, and some tackling and fouling would have added to depth; 7/10.
