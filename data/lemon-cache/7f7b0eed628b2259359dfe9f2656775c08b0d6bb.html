Monk 2013-09-27
For those people that seem to think the BBC-version is better, I can list some differences that I found.

In the BBC-version, almost everything flickers. Scrolling is also very jerky. The music is worse, and the sound effects are pretty awful, especially in comparison with the C64 version.

The graphics are too CGA-ish - not only the monsters are purple (which is appropriate), but also Frak himself (if that is his name) is the same shade of purple! Awful.

The delightful and intense, exciting and almost mysterious C64-Frak-atmosphere is completely missing.

The title screen is not very informative, but it does feature game graphics.

You have to use undefinable keys to play.

The BBC-version does have indeed a larger play area consantly visible, which helps, but all the other factors make it a worse game.

On the C64, the graphics move smoothly, nothing flickers, scrolling is nice and the menu lets you choose a lot of things. BBC-version feels unfinished and amateurish.

All in all, both versions feature the incredibly annoying 'if you do the wrong move, you die'-routine, which forces the player to learn the required pattern to complete a level, instead of exploring freely. Instead of jumping from THIS platform to the right, you have to go to the OTHER platform slightly on the left.. Groan!
