When the knives and guns come out, the game's difficulty curve turns into a cliff - and being based on an arcade game - this was the bit where you were supposed to reach "game over" so you could put more cash in. That part is disappointing but it's faithfully recreated.

Neo-Rio 2015-05-04
Why is everyone so harsh on this game? The Zzap review got it right. It's one of the better beat'em ups on the Commodore 64, and it plays close to the original arcade game. As long as you could pin your joystick down, using the keys was a godsend. You could chain moves together and with practice get really handy at dispatching enemies. This is fun, and what Double Dragon should have been. All the same, the game is no pushover and becomes ruthlessly unforgiving at the end. With a lot of practice, and a bit of skill, you can beat it - and the sense of being able to do that is great.

When you take into account just how awful the later conversion of Double Dragon was, This conversion of Renegade holds up quite well. All the levels of the arcade original are here, and the various bad guy's (and gal's) are all there too.
Sprites are OK, but the backdrops seem to get progressively worse after the beginning level on the subway.
What ruins the game is a cack handed control system involving the space key.

All that aside, im giving Renegade a 6/10
