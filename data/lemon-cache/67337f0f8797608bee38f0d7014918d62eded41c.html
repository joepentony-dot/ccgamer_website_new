Callisto 2003-02-08
When it's loaded, press the T key. You're now in the telemetry screen (you'll go here a lot). Press enter on the main words until all the red selections are green, and the No Go at the bottom says Go. Press fire twice. Now it's counting down. The small black gauge on the right marked error has a red line in the middle of it. When there is a massive red bar, press fire when it becomes nothing in the middle. You'll need to do this lots. The white bar just to the left of error box has a red line in the middle - you need to keep that centred, as well as doing the other thing. On the error box, the error accumulates - so if you have 4 goes and in total you're over, make sure it's under in total for the next section. There are three sections, one for each rocket stage. Do that correctly and voila, you're in orbit.
