RichardH 2015-06-26 (10/10)
A great classic! A small group of US soldiers crash in Vietnam with a Helicopter, they're injured, have almost no food and ammo, and you must lead them back to freedom, through a jungle full of Vietcong soldiers!

The game was inspired by the popular Cinemaware titles, which mostly offered great presentation but little gameplay. Apparently the developers tried to do better, but in that regard they didn't succeed. While the game offers fantastic graphics and sound, plus a great athmosphere, the gameplay is rather repetitive. You move around your group of soldiers, sometimes interrupted by mini games. Still, the game is absolutely worth playing and there is nothing that feels remotely similar to Lost Patrol!

WARNING spoilers ahead:
It's easy to beat the game if you know how, otherwise it is impossible. All you have to do is to never rest for more than 50 minutes, if you rest longer this costs food, and without food you're practically dead. Moreover, it seems pointless to visit the villages, as this gets you killed most of the time.
