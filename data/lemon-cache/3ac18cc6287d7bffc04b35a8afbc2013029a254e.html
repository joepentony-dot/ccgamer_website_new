rwx 2025-06-14 (7/10)
Ok, so in my teens I would have given this 9 or 10 as I thought it was a superb game. Today with more grown-up?/cynical? Eyes on it.. It's down to a 7 out of 10. Despite good graphics and a really good soundtrack, the gameplay is lacking and it simply boils down to you either running left or right to collect four pick-ups.. Just go where the eyes of hawks illuminate and that's all there is to it whilst avoiding/killing some enemies. It isn't much more than that. Plenty of levels, but a limited scrolling area make it feel cramped given the two sprite high main character. Yes there's some static parallax which looks nice, but it doesn't make up for what is on reflection average gameplay. The Zzap!64 review was inflated under duress from Newsfield/Thalamus stating if it didn't get a Gold Medal then Thalamus will pull out of the C64 market. Without the threat I'm sure it would have back then probably maybe got a Sizzler or a high 80s score. It's not that much different from games like Rimrunner which were average gameplay and a similar mechanic. The soundtrack from Jeroen is probably one of his best pieces of work and probaly helps the overall game. A bit more to do in the game would make it more enjoyable and less samey/repetitive.

*all flash and no substance* - The Loading screen looks great. The option to alter the music while loading (similar to Delta) works well, and keeps the user busy while loading progresses. Once the game has loaded, the in game title screen music rates as one of Jeroen Tel's best. There's a nice scrolling effect used on the in game title screen also. Nice run animation on the main player sprite (even if he does not bob up and down like a runner should). Then, for some reason, I found the game dull to play. The platform action is OK, nothing compared to say Mario. The concept of watching the Hawk's (located at the top left and right of the screen) eye flash, to prompt the player to run left or right, in the direction of the next Item to collect is rather thin. There's simply not enough to it.

Boçù 2002-11-30
How much did I love this game (and I still do)! Presentation is superb, with great mix-e-load (better than Delta's), attract mode, demo mode, a magnificent title tune, that cool talk-to-me story to set the scene and the clever multiload disguised as a recharge screen! There's even a secret level, and you can even practice the level you died on! Graphics: sprites good or OK, but the parallax scrolling is top-notch. The interlevel music is sooo catchy, the in-game "background" soundtracks are varied and really good, the sound FX are excellent. And the gameplay... Well OK it's nothing really original, but it's addictive and frenetic and the varied enemies' behaviour and the strategic weapons' use add extra depth to an already brilliant platform/sh'm'up. Also, the different level settings (temple, goldrush... ) add charm to it. I played it for months and even nowadays I enjoy it a lot. That much controversial Zzap! Gold Medal was really well deserved, in my opinion!
