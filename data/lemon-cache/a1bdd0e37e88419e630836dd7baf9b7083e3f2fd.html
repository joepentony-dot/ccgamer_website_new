Gurt 2008-08-09 (6/10)
This game is identical to Treasure Island, minus the port at the start, and the mini-game has been relocated to the bridges dotted around the map literally randomly (some can be bypassed or are pointless), and some graphics are different. Otherwise, the game is identical. Same map, the enemies are still pirates and throw cutlasses (!), the mini game is the same (different graphics), you still collect items, and after you get to the woman you have to rescue (a treasure chest in Treasure Island), you get chased by a ghost thing (which is identical to a sprite from the port in TI). To win, you go one screen north of where you go in TI. 95% identical game, probably not as good (the bridges are annoying, plus no port!)

Also, there's no way they'd get away with putting in slant-eyed, yellow-skinned giants on the bridge screens nowadays. (they're yellow in TI, too - but not slant-eyed)

Oh, and the game is frustrating. Pretty graphics, nice music, stupid gameplay.
