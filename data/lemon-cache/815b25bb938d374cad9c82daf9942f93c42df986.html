Nayphee 2025-11-06 (6/10)
This is an original game where you have to follow and learn patterns of the robot sheep so you can plan to corral them into a transporter. There's a real test of observation, and a puzzle element in which you have to learn where to dig holes, and when to block the sheep so that they go where you want.
Sadly the game is ruined by insanely high difficulty later on - particularly in the initial "droid chamber" sections - which become nigh impossible and make the game appear broken.
Amazing music though - as it suits the game perfectly.

Clive Brooker 2003-05-20
Oh dear what have I done to offend so many? Back in 1985 when I designed, programmed and single handed created the original Sinclair Spectrum OMAHD, I thought I had come up with something original. Reviews rated around the 75% mark and it was considered well worth the £2. 00 Mastertronic asking price. The C64 version (visually identical to my Spectrum original) had the added Rob Hubbard masterpiece of course. Incidentally, I have recently heard a current rap song which uses Rob’s theme. OMAHD is a maze “thinking game” which perhaps does not hold universal appeal. Today, we expect so much more from games, albeit that they have a large team and advertising budget and cost a small fortune. So perhaps the game is best judged by it contemporaries from its era (that’s my excuse anyway) and lets not forget that today, the game is offered for free. Still, for the odd few who enjoyed the game, that’s good. For those that describe it as excrement, it would be good to see their best efforts.
