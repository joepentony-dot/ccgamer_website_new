Monk 2013-01-26
This game oozes atmosphere. The graphics looked really good to me back then, and I was surprised at how well Asterix had been brought to 'life' - it was sort of miraculous to be able to play something that previously was only a comic book in my world.

That everyone was recognizable, and that the graphics looked better and more closely resembling the comic than I would have expected, added to the excitement.

The musics really create an appropriate atmosphere and feel to the location. Especially the hungarian dance in the prison cell really made an impression on my soul, and the funny 'village' tune was always uplifting to listen to.

Gameplay is where things fall apart, though. I became incredibly frustrated and disappointed when I realized it was not only a really difficult task to try to find the pieces of the cauldron, but it became impossible for me.

If only the fights could have been more sensible and easier, and the map more logical to navigate, things would have been great.

By the way, the STIL info says that the first music is "Asterix Movie Theme", but it doesn't specify WHICH movie. There were a lot of Asterix movies before this game was made, and I listened through them all (as far as I know), and couldn't find this song in any of them.

There are some slightly similar tunes in the 1967 movie, but nothing that matches completely. I wonder if Neil Brennan only borrowed a bit instead of being completely faithful to the melody and structure?
