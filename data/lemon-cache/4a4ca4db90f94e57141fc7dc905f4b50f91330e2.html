yaztromo 2015-03-01 (6/10)
A simple game. Go to talk to the girl, and then take her back to your spawn point.

The game is not much to look at, the Ants are nicely drawn and the 3D effect of the buildings is well done.

Sound is the games weakest point. Extremely simple scratchy SFX and little tuneless dittys, that's all you get.

Two things save this game and make it some what a classic:- The gameplay which is quite fun, trying to find a path to escape from the tireless Ants and the atmosphere, with it's odd geometric structures feels like some dystopian world after an advanced civilisation has long died and all that is left are insects grown massive from eons of unchecked evolution. Very surreal.

Sadly, once the Ants are really on you you have little hope of completing the level, the girl will often stop following you too so a tragic end is often inevitable.

6/10.
