LeC64win 2010-09-14 (10/10)
I play Omega Race pretty regular since this year, and yeah I think it's a very good shooter where tactical attack is very important. It's one of the first games I've seen played on the Commodore 64 in 1983 or so.

Being a Space Fury-fan, I immediately loved it. Also I found the linear graphics very well designed in those days and was impressed by the smooth, fast and furious gameplay. However, when I finally got a copy of the game around 1989, I hated the high difficult level. But now with the right practise I can truly enjoy this gem, that is magic on the C=64. I vote 10 for the action and graphics.

The version also resembles the arcade version much more. The VIC-20 version seems nice for some practise because it's easier. It's a nice touch that this vector-game has optional colors. I don't need 'Amega Race', haha. That version wouldn't load into my Commodore 64.
