hazzard 2024-05-27 (7/10)
The first time I played this game I was blown away, but after a few minutes the novelty quickly wore off. The fixed perspective is very limiting and the car oversteers chronically at the slightest joystick movement - it's almost as if you're driving on ice! It doesn't help that the other cars appear at identical intervals and always move with the same predetermined patterns, plus all the tracks look and feel the same! The fuel and tyre management add a nice strategy element to the game, but the drab gameplay and poor controls let it down somewhat. It's worth a play, especially with two players, but I never really understood what the fuss was about.

Creepy Neighbour 2016-06-09 (9/10)
An amazing racing game that was just as much fun in single player as it was in 2 player. Most of it you've seen before; single race, championship mode all on real world tracks. What it did have that other racers didn't was tyre and fuel management. Not only that when you had to make a pitstop you had to control the pit crew yourself. Not as easy as it sounds the best way to save valuable seconds was to have the fuel guy do his thing while the other guy changed the tyres. The problem was that if the fuel guy overfilled the car the fuel gauge would go back to 0! Then did you change all the tyres or just the ones that are badly worn? Get it wrong an you suffered a blow out and your race was over. Yes there are racing games that rely on strategy as well as out and out racing but none of them since have had you manage the pit crew as well. I have a huge love for racing games and it's all thanks top Pitstop 2 which started it all.
