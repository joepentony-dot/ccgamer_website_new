Weasel Fierce 2019-01-08 (8/10)
Outstanding game that is hard to place. Tactics? Strategy? Action? It has a little bit of each area.
The dark, unflinching cyberpunk/anti-capitalist atmosphere is thick and very well executed. The game never even questions the civilians you mow down, and it didn't make you do it either. That was up to you.
Human life is obsolete. Move along citizen.

The game is let down a little by having bigger ideas than it can execute. Equipment like access cards and time bombs are intriguing but never really useful, movement indoors is a pain and vehicles are seriously frustrating.

But an ambitious game that tried to be something that had never been seen before and one that few people forget playing.

The Amiga version is oddly yellow, compared to the PC version but it's perfectly playable. An expanded system does help. A straight 500 gets a bit pokey running this.
