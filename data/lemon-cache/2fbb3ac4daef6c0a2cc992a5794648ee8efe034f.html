MartinJSUK 2025-10-09 (9/10)
A pure arcade game in the Outrun mould, though with a largely realistic driving feel with stunning handling. It eschews the strategic elements of its bigger more home-computer style predecessor, but with the thrill of a descending timer, varied locations to learn with a new challenge past every checkpoint, and stunningly fast and smooth effects-laden full-screen graphics. A high quality viscerally-thrilling title that clearly received love and care, though not perfect. I'm not sure if it's actually the Amiga's best Outrun variant - Lotus 1 and Crazy Cars III each have their own take on the genre - but the first to really push towards the Amiga's full technical potential, and I can see why it's the most iconic.

Maff Rignall 2005-09-30 (10/10)
Brilliant! A really quite clever sequel, which builds upon the first without cloning it entirely. It switches the standings-based gameplay of Lotus 1 for a more arcade style 'against the clock' system, which completely changes the whole thrust of the game, even though the actual racing sections are almost identical. The 'get sucked into roadblocks' road handling of the original was also reworked into a friendlier, bouncier, 'rubber tree' physics model.

Many diehard fans were aghast at the changes, and indeed Lotus 2 loses the competitive feel of the first, especially when playing solo. But it works as something else entirely. The graphics engine was significantly improved, and though there's only eight levels here they're all fantastic. From the tumbleweed-strewn roads of the desert level. To the head-on traffic on the freeway, right up to the final level which takes place in the middle of a heavy storm. All great.

And the null-modem linkup more than made up for the absence of league scoring. With two Amigas and three mates, this is all you need for an all-night LAN party... Leave the PCs at home :-). But even playing solo, Lotus 2 delivered the goods.

This has got to be one of the best sequels ever - It's not a limp rehash of the first game, and it's not a cynical cash-in, either. It complements the original perfectly while still being a brilliant game in its own right. And for my money, it's the best sprite-based racer on any platform, *ever*. Top stuff. 10/10.

When Lotus 2 came on the market I was sceptic at first. My hopes were so high and I just didn't think it was possible to better the first. When I played it, I realized that for me in some areas it was better then the prequel, but it some parts I was a little disappointed. First of all the better things: 1. In one-played mode you now had fullscreen view instead of half-screen with workmen tuning a Lotus as if their lives depended on it. 2. The courses where long and continuous, so you didn't drive the same lap over and over again. Also some nice features driving under trucks or other traffic in the opposite direction. 3. The different driving-conditions in the different tracks, 4. You could play the game via a null-modem cable. TONS of fun with friends! Things I found a disappointment: 1. Driving against the clock instead of against other cars, 2. The lack or realism like hitting something without loosing speed, nitro's and timeballs on the circuit and jumping over rivers by hitting trees. 3. The two cars, which didn't have much difference drivingwise and you couldn't choose between the two. For me Lotus 1 and 2 are pretty evenly matched, but I just prefer the first one slightly. But don't be mistaken, Lotus 2 is one hell of a racer!
