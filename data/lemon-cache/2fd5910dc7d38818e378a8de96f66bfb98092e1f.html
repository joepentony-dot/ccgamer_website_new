A case of a nice idea, badly implemented. Trundle your tank around the level setting up deflectors so when you fire a laser it blows up the door to the next level. The tank is a slippery bugger to control and the game lacks any sort of urgency.
View all comments (745)
Richard of TND 2023-06-24 (5/10)
I first saw this game on a cover tape, and found this game to be really tough. The idea is to take existing reflectors and re-position those onto a different holder, then press F5 to trigger a laser to hit the exit. If a laser misses and hits a different obstacle, or you get caught by enemies a life is lost. You have to start again and then the reflectors are moved at a random place. An unfair fate for failing the puzzle. It isn't a bad game, but personally I prefer Anarchy as a tank puzzle game.
