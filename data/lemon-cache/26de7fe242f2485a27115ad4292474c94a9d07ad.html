Tim 2002-06-05
An utterly absorbing fantasy adventure. Sending your four heroes out to recruit warriors and armies to face the massive onslaught of Doomdarks forces. Sometimes you had to hide, others fight, but throughout there was that wonderful Midnight Landscape of towers, lakes, downs, mountains, armies in the distance and outland keeps and castles. You could complete the game with either a quest or a miltary campaign. After weeks and weeks I had saved a position that allowed me to Assault Doomdarks castle meanwhile one of my heroes (Fandrake the dragon I think) had snuck in Frodo style to Doomdarks domain to steal and destroy the Ice Crown. The Assault failed, Doomdark was one tough bastard, but the quest succeeded and Midnight was freed. It is one of the most satisfying epic victories I have ever known. If you are a fantasy nut you will love this game.
Jeremy 2002-05-13
And I agree with both of you. Utterly gripping, I was hooked from the moment Doomdark's armies first crushed my feeble attempts to stop him. The LOTR-inspired background is great (I even loved the story in the manual!) and in my opinion does more justice to Tolkien than any of the 'real' LOTR games on the C64. Later followed up by Doomdark's Revenge, but I never got into that game as much as into this one.
