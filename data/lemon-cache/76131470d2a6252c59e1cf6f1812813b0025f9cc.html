It's a lot like the arcade "Bank Panic", but it's not quite the same. We're missing some game elements -- like bomb on the door, tied up citizens, and the "wanted poster" hints that the citizens give as to what door a bandit waits at.
Instead we gain a few new features... Such as the much larger cast characters, the sneaky hat guy who sometimes has a bomb, and the end of round "The good, the bad, and the ugly" shootout for bonus points and lives.
It makes it a more interesting game!
