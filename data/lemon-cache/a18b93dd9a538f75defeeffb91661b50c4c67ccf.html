compnded 2024-12-13 (5/10)
Probably the best Tiertex conversion ever to grace the C64, which I know is faint praise. It provides a decent approximation of the coin-op and the gameplay is reasonably well implemented although the controls are far from tight. Graphics, particularly the backgrounds, are crude and the sound including the tunelets is awful. The one solace is that it trumps the Amiga conversion.

Ugly graphics mixed with slow action, unbearable music and pitiful sound effects.
What's worse is that the shoddy controls added to the lag in response leaves no time to react in many situations, making it totally unfair and ruining the gameplay of the original where you were always able to react in a split second, so if you failed you knew it was your own fault and not the sloppy programming. The arcade game was brilliant and had me totally hooked whereas this is a sad mangling of a classic. The Amiga version is even worse. Oh it's Tiertex... Well that explains it.
