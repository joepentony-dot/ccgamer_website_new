MP83 2025-04-30 (9/10)
Very enjoyable early platformer with very smooth, almost modern, game design.

I love how you can climb on and off ladders by holding diagonal directions, meaning that you never have to stop first and then press a direction.

I love how you can crab ladders from mid-air and jump off them at any point you want.

I love how the player character bounces off the opposite direction when jumping against a wall/platform.

I love how holding the jump button makes you character jump constantly.

I especially love the 2nd loop of the game, which is really unique. Mother duck, which was previously just standing there on the top-left corner, starts to suddenly chase you.

Overall, a great fun!

Play the BBC version, and you won't want to even touch any other version. The C64 version is decent (it's kinda hard to make a bad version of this), but ruined by a few things; the sound effects, the WAY TOO WIDE main sprite and the dull color of the eggs.

I mean, what's going on? C64 can do anything the BBC can do and more, and yet, it used this bloated, super big sprite and very dim egg color.

How did they mess up this simple, SIMPLE game this bad?

Not a total disaster, as Chuckie Egg is 'playable' in pretty much any platfom, but for heaven's sake, at least give the BBC version a go before making your final decision about this one.
