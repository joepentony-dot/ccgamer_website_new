The original release for Apple II was in 1982, but looks like the Commodore 64 version was released in US 1983, and the year after in UK,

Also the Ariolasoft release had a Win a Commodore SX64 competition in the case. This machine was first released in 1984.
(Ref: advertisement and editorial in Compute's Gazette issue 2, vol 1. 1983 archive.org/details/1983-08-computegazette/page/n103/mode/2up?q=choplifte)

Formats for C64 were Cartridge, Disk and Tape. Haven't found shots of disk versions, but the other two.

Cartridge, US, $39,95 (unsure if it was released in UK)
Tape: £9,95
Disk: £12,95
