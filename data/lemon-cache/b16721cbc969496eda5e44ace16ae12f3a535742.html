Thelead 2025-04-20 (7/10)
Some odd comments on this one. Certainly nothing like a Diablo/Rogue style game, this is very much an arcade adventure with slightly iffy combat and lots of items to find and manipulate. Lovely, detailed sprites and some great animation let down by a leaden chiptune that tries faux medieval but misses the mark. Save system is awful with a code at the end of each of the first seven levels which can take an age to complete and lots of trial and error leads to typing that damn code in over and over. Despite the flaws, its a very well made game with lots of puzzles and traps to best wrapped in a familiar but well done plot.

In the long line of dungeon crawlers, this rogue-lite is a bit too much on rails and humourless for my taste. That being said, it has some beautiful touches like the inventory art, which is stunning.
Concerning the puzzles, they are good enough without being too frustrating. The isometric view may be pretty but it is also highly annoying when you can't see what is close to a masking wall. Otherwise, it's a fine effort with decent animation which is let down by feeble sound effects. Not bad but not great either.
