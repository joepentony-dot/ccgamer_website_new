JohnD 2024-03-04
How Dragon's Lair C64 came about....

Alan Maton brought a Coleco Adam into the Software Project's office, with a copy of Dragon's Lair on it. Steve Birtles and I had been working a a tape loader for the C64. As we played Dragon's Lair it I realised that the Adam was loading the next level from tape whilst playing the game. With what I had learned about C64 tape loaders I said "We could do that for the C64!" Alan responded "Go for it! He raced to his office and immediately started work acquiring the rights from Coleco. Dragon's Lair C64 was simply a copy of the Coleco Adam version.

Dragon's Lair II was based on the arcade version and I chose levels I felt we could program. It was also a much better game, with much better music (thanks Rob Hubbard), in my opinion.

John Darnell
