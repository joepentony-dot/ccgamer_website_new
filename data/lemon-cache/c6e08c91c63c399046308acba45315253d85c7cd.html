ScartDeluxe 2016-03-19 (1/10)
Now this is more like it. A really high profile game that does everything the Megadrive version does and then it does some more. The presentation of Desert strike on the Amiga totally wipes the bottom floor with the Megadrive version. Whoa! Hey hit me! The music is brilliant, and the presentation is super slick, like the start of an action movie. Then you get to the game, and the graphics are improved with transparent shadow effects. It's so subtle, and achieved by using the halfbrite bitplane, which is a nice cheap effect often used to increase the usual ocs colour palette from 32 to 64, by offering an extra 32 colours that are half as bright as the first 32. With all that out of the way, this game is a mission based helicopter game. There are three weapons of varying power and speed, and you have to be careful not to run out of ammunition and fuel. A mad man that I suppose is supposed to emulate the western portrayal of how Saddam Hussein is trying to take over the world by obtaining the secrets to producing the best weapons in the world at the time, and it's your job to stop him. It's a sickening plot to be fair, but if you're decently desensitised by such things then you'll be able to ignore that and have a great time with this game, just as I did in my naive childhood days. These days, having matured and knowing a bit more about the world, this game makes me feel quite ill. I don't think I shall ever play it again.
