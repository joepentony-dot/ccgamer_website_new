Sebatosh 2025-10-30 (10/10)
After remembering being mesmorized by this game in my youth (without knowing where to go), I've finally found the time - 30+ years later - to complete the game.

Although it is not perfect (the combat slows down with multiple enemies on 1 screen for instance), the atmosphere is brilliant. A walk in the forest, orcs and bandits everywhere, suddenly you hear the waves crashing, knowing you're approaching the end of the forest and can see the tides roll in. The mysteries that are rumoured about, the arch mage in his tower... The day and night cycle!

It's easy to die in Times of Lore, but fortunately the game becomes a lot easier when you find the magic axe. A much needed weapon necessary to manage your way through the frustrating dungeon levels near the end...

Despite its flaws, I love this game; Times of Lore can be seen as an action game with minor RPG elements, as a proto-Diablo even, which becomes better when you engulf yourself in the world Origin has created.

It's a shame the wonderful music from the intro screens is not returning during the game. It's an even bigger shame that the follow-up Bad Blood doesn't improve on the gameplay or graphics at all (despite its promising post-apocalyptic setting).

I would love to see a remastered version of Times of Lore with all flaws removed, paired together with an improved Bad Blood... But yes, this classic version is also in its current state definitely worth your time!

Thelead 2024-02-20 (7/10)
It's an ambitious take on an arcade RPG but it has a few too many problems to fully recommend. Presentation wise its excellent with excellent graphics and an easy to understand icon system accessed by a tap of the space bar. Forgetting to tap the space bar can end your game as you brutally murder the poor sod you are talking to though so take care. I have a few issues. Firstly the play screen is just too zoomed in. In a game with a huge map, only seeing a very small section of the map is a real pain. This is mitigated slightly by the inclusion of a map in the box but its still a problem. This may also have something to do with the second problem, its glacial pace. There is regular slowdown and you move at a very sluggish pace. Again, this can be mitigated by the purchase of a magical item but if you miss it the game is a real slog to get anywhere.
If you can see past the problems there is a big world to explore and quests to complete.
