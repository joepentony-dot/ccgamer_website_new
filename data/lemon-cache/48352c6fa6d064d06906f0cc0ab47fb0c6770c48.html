Takumaji 2011-08-22 (9/10)
Great Giana Sisters may have been a Mario rip-off but taken for itself, it still was one of the best scrolling platformers on the C64. Gameplay is smooth as silk, graphics are good, just like Chris Hülsbeck's music and the in-game sound effects. It has a well-balanced difficulty level that caters to beginners and pros alike and is extremly addictive, just like the original SMB. The makers got sued by Big N over it, the similarities to Mario are very obvious, down to the the "inverted" name of the game - Great Giana Sisters vs. Super Mario Brothers... Still, I think Nintendo should have countered Time Warp Production's game with a SMB conversion for C64 of their own instead of taking legal action. Sadly, this was their standard in the late 80s and throughout the 90s, back then, driving competitors out of the market via lawsuits and legally gagging dev'er or distributor contracts was Big N's fave pastime. Ah well... Rip-off or not, Giana Sisters is an excellent game that will always be part of the C64 hall of fame - Quality Made in Germany! . 9/10.
