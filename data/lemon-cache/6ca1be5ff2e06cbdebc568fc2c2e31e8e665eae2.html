MartinJSUK 2024-03-06 (7/10)
I like what's here.

The combined keyboard/mouse control system is original and works really well, allowing you to target one enemy while moving to avoid another. The main sprite is impressive, and the huge number of enemy sprites and associated gore means there's rarely a dull moment. Having to moderate your fire to prevent the gun from overheating is a nice idea. There are some interesting enemies, ranging from paratroopers landing from above to vehicles which guys get out of (if you don't blow it up first). The sound is hugely impressive, especially if you have an A1200 for the speech. It's a good aggression release.

Sadly though, that's all that's here.

Levels only vary in setting and time period, scrolling remains largely automated with no ledges or hiding places to find, and there are no power-ups to collect or buy - things like limited smart-bombs or a selectable shield would have added some basic strategy. I can't help but thinking that the original plan to alternate with platform levels would have made for a more interesting game, even if those weren't of as high a quality as what's here. It's just too limited to fully justify the price at the time.

mike_tyson 2012-05-03
This is a gallery-type shooter with a similar concept to the arcade classic SDI- Strategic Defense Initiative (1987) by SEGA (*): you control your crosshairs independently from your "ship" - actually, a walker or what nowadays is commonly called mecha.

Unusually, the scrolling (multi-layered, I count three scrollfields) moves to the left, unlike in 99% of side scrolling shooters.

The walker can only move left/right (with keyboard or joystick), while the crosshair can move all over the screen and is controlled with mouse. You have unlimited ammo, but the gun can overheat so it's better to shoot in controlled bursts and use the lock-on feature where appropriate. Very accessible control scheme.

The graphics are very good, with many tiny sprites and hordes of enemies on screen, and excellent animation. The backgrounds convey a post-apocalyptic, moody atmosphere that fits the game. The sound is also superb.

The story of Walker is about time pirates and some sort of changewar I guess. You will walk and blast through a bombed out Berlin in the last moments of WWII, and through devastated future cityscapes. Wide variety of enemies.

Gameplay, graphics, sound are all top notch! Walker is one of the best shooters on the Amiga, do yourself a favour and play it!

(*) Not the Cinemaware title from the same year
