trebor 2017-04-21 (7/10)
There's definitely some persuasion for better or worse when ranking this unique and standout title under the C64. Being a horror movie fan, especially a fan of the involved franchise will hold some sway and draw. If you hate horror movies or/and the franchise, this title will likely be held in less favorable light.

However, it is a good game on its own merit. The various melodies and environments to explore in along with good graphics and an occasional screen and "surprise" horror scene screen keeps the pace of the game flowing nicely.

Setting personal goals such as no kids dying helps to up the challenge, but disappointing that outside of an introductory backstory there is no real distinction between the various characters a player becomes throughout the game. They all move at the same pace and stamina. They handle weapons all the same. Shame a missed opportunity there, but still a fun game to play.

Some in-game glitches hold this title back some too, including Jason occasionally warping in and out of disguise at times he should not. The thrill though in protecting the kids, hunting down Jason, and taking him out is what keeps you coming back for more.
