Mountain Man 2004-05-29
In a nutshell, this game had great graphics and great music but terrible, terrible game-play. Even though it looks like there are a variety of games to choose from, there were basically only two: a simplistic fighting game and a more interesting shooting game. The fighting game consisted of simply swinging your weapon as fast as you could to bludgeon your opponent into submission. Whether the weapon was a mace, an axe, or a sword, the game-play was identical. You'd hold the mouse button down and move the joystick in various directions to execute different moves, and while each one looked different, they all had basically the same effect, so there was no strategy involved. The shooting game is the better of the two. You had to move a wobbly cross-hair over a target and fire, taking into account the parabolic arc of the arrow or bolt. The crossbow was the easier shooting game simply because your projectile moved faster making it easier to hit targets, but each variation--bow and crossbow--provided an interesting challenge. Not the best of games, but at least it was a budget title.
