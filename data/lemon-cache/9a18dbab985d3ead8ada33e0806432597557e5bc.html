Nayphee 2023-10-24 (5/10)
As far as fighting games go, this one is very average. I got it on the 6-Pak compilation and it feels a bit like filler. Unlike Karateka - which has the gameplay this game most closely resembles - there's a real lack of strategy here. You basically go toe-to-toe with your enemy and trade blows until one of you falls over. You can't even block or dodge in a timely fashion. If you get the distance just right between you and your enemy you *might* be able to whack them in the head without getting hit back, but most likely you'll miss. It all looks and animates very well at least, but it controls sluggishly and is utterly tiresome, repetitive, and boring - with little else to do. Once you knock your enemy out after what seems like an eternity of waiting, they drop an urn that you can hit - but the effect of that is totally random. It may help, it may harm, it may just ruin your chance of finishing the game. Good luck with that I guess. Nice music, at least, until you get sick of hearing it.
