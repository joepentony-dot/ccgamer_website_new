Nayphee 2025-11-05 (7/10)
One of Epyx's early RPG games, "Gateway Drug to Apshai" is a more appropriate title as this game is quite addictive and have you wanting more. It's very much a primitive ancestor to "Diablo" and all action RPGs, and it does this in a very satisfying way. You can shoot arrows and swing your sword around and then gain experience to go deeper into the dungeons to fight harder monsters and explore further - ad infinitum - to rack up a high score. The game is extremely playable despite it's dated presentation. Give it a chance and it will have you wanting to play more RPGs.
