Far, far too easy, but still one of the best C64 games ever, due to its playability, excellent graphics and pumping soundtrack on the shoot-'em-up stages. I actually still prefer this to the 'sequels' on Amiga and SNES...
Paul Brooks 2002-01-16
I agree with alex that this game, along with the Turrican are without doubt the best games on the C64, and may I be bold as to say their is only a couple of rivals on any platform, including the PC. It astounded me with it's graphics, explosions, exploration, hidden secrets and the weapons where awesome. I don't finish a game unless it has something special to offer and the turricans games, (not the 3rd installment on the amiga) are the benchmarks game wise that programmers even now should aiming to better.
Alex Aslanidis 2001-12-31
This, according to my opinion, is the best ever C64 game. It combines massive playability, with technical perfection. The graphics are AWESOME and the levels are huge. Platform shoot-em-ups were never this good. With 5 different worlds to conquer, amazing parallax scrolling, ultra-big baddies, underwater sections, a blindingly fast left-to-right shoot-em-up section, and many many other features, this is as good as it gets...
