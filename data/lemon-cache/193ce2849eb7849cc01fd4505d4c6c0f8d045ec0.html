Borough Medley 2015-07-04
Elite got the license for Thundercats back in the day. They started working on it but to ensure the game would be ready to ship for the Christmas season they developed not just one Thundercats game but two. As December got closer and closer they decided to instead see if they already had a near complete game to fix up and release as Thundercats. They found a game by F. T. L. Called Samurai Dawn. That got released as Thundercats - The lost eye of Thundera. What of the other Thundercats games? One of them was meant to be released as a sequel to Ghost 'n' Goblins, called Beyond the Ice Palace, but Capcom had other plans. It kept the title though and is essentially a Thundercats game. The second, which to this very day still has the Thundercats theme playing.. Yes, it's what we all know as Bombjack 2.
