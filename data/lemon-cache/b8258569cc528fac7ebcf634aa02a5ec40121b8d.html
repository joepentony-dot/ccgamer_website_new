yaztromo 2015-03-22 (6/10)
The 3D effect is well done, very believable. The atmosphere is cleverly crafted with the use of strange background noises.

The let down in this game are the between level stages where you must shoot or avoid enemy fighters. Gauging your height relative to them is hard and so it's easy but dull to just avoid them.

The main levels are a lot of fun though. Flying down each brick 'tunnel' blasting ground targets which explode into flames is great.

6/10. Would've been 7/10 if they'd left out the mid level stages.
