virtualsky 2023-11-19 (6/10)
I'll start by saying, I've played worse. For me, the jumping mechanics is probably the worse thing about this game. It's difficult to control exactly where you're going to land. The graphics are pretty good and well detailed and I'm actually glad that the sound is subdued. This kind of game requires some concentration, so an annoying background tune playing over and over again would probably send me running. Once I read the manual, I had a much better idea of what the game expected of me and how to battle the different baddies. There's actually a lot more to this game than I first thought there would be. So, I'm actually intrigued enough to spend some more time with it.
