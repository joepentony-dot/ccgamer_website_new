Monk 2012-10-03
Richard: You didn't try the function keys, didja?

This game is actually FAST, and many ways realistic. To call it slow can only come from RTFM/Eye-Dee-Ten-Tee error.

I managed to land the bike very well every now and then, but never managed to learn how I did it.

EKJC is a neat little game, but it does get somewhat boring in the long run. The big main character and bike were very impressive at the time, and they still look very good.

Twr: Do you think Atari 2600 games are ugly, too? Are all big pixel graphics ugly to you? What's with this big pixel hate among OLD COMPUTER USERS, I don't get it. This game is beautiful, and the 'liveliness' of the big character makes it all even better. So even if you hate enlarged sprites, the animation that this game displays makes more than up for it - it makes it all look very good and immersive.

The hospital thing was interesting and humorous at the time, though I didn't read english that well yet.

All in all, a nice little game for awhile, in small doses every once in awhile - but don't expect to be entertained in the long run, it does get tedious and boring.

As a nostalgy fix, it's as good as any.
