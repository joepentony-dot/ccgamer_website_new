It's an interesting challenge, but just not a particularly appealing one, or one that you would be expecting from a TV show about a state-of-the-art military helicopter. It copies Fort Apocalypse to a degree, and has much smoother scrolling. Rather than play as a simple arcade challenge, there are traps and switches that need to be understood in order to rescue the people. In addition to this, you have to search the caves to locate where the scientists are once you rescue the last one - and they could be anywhere! A fair bit of trial and error is involved to learn how the whole cave system works.
It's a well coded and polished game, just not the most exciting thing to play, or something that has wide appeal because of it's brutal difficulty in places. The music is nice initially, but gets repetitive real quickly

TITLE: Airwolf Theme [from the TV series]
ARTIST: Sylvester Levay
COMMENT: "When I was interviewed by Brian Wilcox at Elite for a job they sent me home with a little task to prove that I had some computer and progamming skills. The task was to make the Oric Atmos computer I had at home play the theme tune to Airwolf. Over the weekend I managed to get the tune playing using a very simple assembler that I had written in basic. As a result I got the job at Elite and the first thing they had me doing was programming the Airwolf tune on the C64 as they were doing a game based on the TV show. After this I found myself doing music all the time and that is how I got started." (MC)
