jokoe 2019-07-01 (9/10)
Flashback is Excellent!

In particular I appreciate the way combat works---it emphasizes careful monitoring and thoughtful planning under time pressure without requiring pixel perfect aim or lightning fast reflexes. I think this is brought about by the duration it takes to carry out your various moves (stand up, crouch, turn around, roll) and the duration of your enemies' moves: long enough that you have to plan reasonably well, yet not so long that you can get away without improvising in the heat of the moment. Just exactly the right pace for me.

The puzzles are fairly easy, but somehow just difficult enough that you feel at least a little bit clever when you solve them. This include some combat sequences---they should really be thought of as real-time puzzles.

The sound effects are great, as is the sporadic use of music for emphasis. The graphics are attractive.

Graphics is the one area where 25 years of technological progress really makes a big difference for the better; by attractive, I mean "attractive, if you don't mind the screen resolution and the constraints it imposes". I do not mind :-)

The story---if you're uncharitable you call it derivative, if you're charitable you call it a tribute. In my view it's perfectly competent and workable but nothing spectacular. And while I enjoy the watching the story unfold in the cut scenes, story is probably the game's weakest part. Still: competent, workable and richer than many other games.

RobFarley 2004-12-05 (10/10)
Conrad... OK... He thought he should have been in Miami Vice with this brown leather jacket, but after you got past that you could really start to enjoy the game.

This was a wonderfully absorbing game, with cut scenes enhancing the play rather than slowing it down. The animation was amazing due to the 2D polygon engine, used to great effect in both the game play and the cut scenes, because only points were stored rather than bitmaps this meant that you could squeeze many more animation frames in the limited memory.

The one problem with this game were a couple of puzzles where you were against the clock and you had to be pixel perfect with activating items which meant replaying a mission over and over until you got it right (anyone remember trying to get the disk in the computer?)

From a personal point of view I was playing this game when the Chemical Brothers 'Exit Planet Dust' album came out and the two fitted together perfectly.

After I left the Amiga to the PC I bought the follow up 'Fades to Black' that took the Another Life / Flashback series into glorious 3D. This game kept the feel of the previous Amiga titles and featured FMV cut scenes.

Anyway, this game is and was stunning and truly epic.
