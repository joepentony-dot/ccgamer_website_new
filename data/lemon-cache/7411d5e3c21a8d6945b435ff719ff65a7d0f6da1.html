You must navigate through multiple levels of an asteroid, fighting aliens and rescuing your buddies from the clutches of the Mekon. You have a total of thirty minutes to complete the game. Exploration and trial-and-error puzzle solving are the orders of the day. I have only one real complaint, and it's a minor one: The game control, especially when fighting Treens, could be a LOT better.
