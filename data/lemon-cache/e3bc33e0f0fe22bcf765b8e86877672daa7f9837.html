Scrawffler 2008-10-03 (6/10)
A fun game with nice graphics and very amusing animation, with great crazy voices to match. I love the fact that, in this whole series, you can't actually understand their language (except for the fact that there are subtitles, or course).

The puzzles were extremely difficult, mostly because they were so illogical and most of the time you could only come to a successful conclusion by trial and error. In this particular game, this was no easy task because you had a health meter, and if you made a mistake it could be fatal!

Luckily, the levels had passwords, and these passwords were different depending on how much health you had at the time. So if you had the patience, you could keep trying until you completed the level with full health. Then all you had to do was write down the password and you wouldn't have to worry about doing the previous level again. It was a very difficult game, but the comedy kept you going and made it very enjoyable.
