C64 Mat 2025-11-19 (9/10)
What a great game! Such a simple title, but full of personality. The music was bouncy and fun, the gameplay was fast and snappy, and the animations were really funny and polished.

Your little pilot would run out and wave at you before getting into the plane to commit suicide. When you shot infantry, they'd scream and back flip. When people fell from the sky, they'd bounce from their tummies to their backs. Just really funny and well presented.

Exactly what a budget game should be!
