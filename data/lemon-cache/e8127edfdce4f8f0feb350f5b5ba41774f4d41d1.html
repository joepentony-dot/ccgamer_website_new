My favourite game on the 64 and still beats most things on more powerful formats. Many a long night has been spent trying to progress further into the game. I never finished it but it didn't matter as I'd had hours of gameplay out of it. Full of atmosphere and neat real world physics make it one of the best designed computer games ever. They tarted up the graphics for the Amiga but I thought it lost some of its charm then even though it played identically. Once you learned the joys of teleporting it made the puzzles a lot easier to solve. It also has a very elegant ram save function to save a bit of frustration at tricky stages within the game.
