What would make anyone play this? I guess any time the theme is WAR, it gets people awfully excited. Which tells more about the people than the game, but that's the way it goes. If you want GOOD War-themed games, try the sequel, Raid Over Moscow, War Play, Blue Max, or even Paratroopers.

I know there's probably some militaristic explanation why this is called a "Beach Head", but when you really think about it, it makes no sense. As kids, we were always wondering where the head is, and what does it even have to do with a beach - like, is there supposed to be a head made entirely out of a beach? A beach that is head-shaped (though that would logically be 'head beach').

Why would anyone play this besides some weird, militaristic nostalgy? The sequel was OK, but even that had some really boring parts that were dragged on too long. And although the knife fight was fun, the graphics of that stage were again gloomy and depressing (they didn't have to be).

There's really not much content to this 'game'. Just five minigames, that barely even qualify as games. Any of those minigames alone would be absolutely hated by any gameplaying community. But if you put them together, and give it a nonsensical name (I saw some beach, but not any heads in this one), it suddenly gets praises all over the place. Go figure.
