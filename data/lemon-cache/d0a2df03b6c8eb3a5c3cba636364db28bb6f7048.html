hazzard 2024-02-18 (7/10)
Stunning visuals and some of the best SID tunes you'll ever hear - hats off to Jeroen Tel. The game feels like more of a tech demo than a serious racer though, since it is all style with minimal substance.
To start with, your car is too wide for the road and the controls feel clunky and a bit unnatural. The weather effects look wobbly and the way the traffic moves towards you with a jerky motion takes some of the polish off the gameplay.
I was one of the poor suckers who had this game on the tape, and the multiload was horrendous and prone to freezing. There's just no escaping the fact that a multiload does not work for a racing game, so they shouldn't have even tried.
Worth a playthrough for the music alone, although preferably a disk or emulated version.

It was a lot of fun, but I recomposed every tune about three times to get it perfect. I still think it is one of the best audio tracks I ever did on the C64." "One nice, very nice note: 'Turbo Outrun' (the music) has won the: 'Best music on 8bit computer 1989' award at the PCW show (now ECTS) in London. :D Too bad I had to find out later. US Gold received the actual award. Bastards! I wasn't even at the scene when it was presented. :( " (JT)
