zaphod77 2014-12-22
There is a lot of misinformation about how to win this game. It is NOT easy.

There is a rumor that you have to modify the game disk to disable either the auto fail from coming too close to the test site, or the time limit. Neither needs to be disabled to win.

The time limit is actually a step counter. Your route must be perfect with no missteps.

There are three variations on who accepts what. Two of those need trial and error to work out. The solution is different depending on your copy of the game.

c64wiki has the truth.

WANDUS 2003-12-29
Well, don't fret people! I really and truly fell in love with this game in 1985. It was drop dead gorgeous. The fact that it required note-taking and map-drawing to complete it did not actually make it all that different from most of the games I played during the 1990ies. Indeed, while the presentation surely cannot keep up with today's lofty standard, it was extremely topical and entertaining at its time and not at all hard to get into. In comparison to other games of the era, such as the epical MASK or SERPENT'S STAR-adventure games, HACKER was immediately accesible. And, for the record, one of the very few games to make your girlfriend believe your were actually hacking a US government computer system. Well done. A classic. Skill, well, back then, games were simply as demanding as they could technically be made to be. As far as originality is concerned, well, there's always HACKER.

Devin de Gruyl 2002-03-11
As originally shipped, the game came with no instructions - not even a Quick Reference Card. When you loaded the disk, you were met with a simple "LOGIN PLEASE:" prompt. That's it. Hacker throws you in at the deep end without bothering to teach you how to swim first. This is very much a period piece; the film WarGames was still fresh in everyone's mind. The "hacker" motif is used simply as a narrative device to hide what is a clever little strategy/diplomacy game. You won't feel like you're "where you're not supposed to be, " as the ads claimed, but it's still an intriguing little game...
