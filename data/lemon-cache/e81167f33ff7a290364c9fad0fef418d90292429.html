It is good that C64 received its "SWIV" conversion but unfortunately this is not a good conversion. The hardware limitations are clearly visible and the game does not give proper amount of entertainment. Fewer number of enemies on one screen were replaced by their annoying type of circling bullets. The "goose ship" became some sort of a sturdy "bastard ship" and the loading sections made the game to lose its flow.

Authors of the conversion tried their best to recreate the original making the graphics looking good and sprite animation fluid, however it does not give even half of the fun of the original.

There are games that conversion from 8 to 16 bit as well as the other way around is futile. The problem lays either in hardware limitations or simply be the design. SWIV fits to the former.

Pyramidenkopf 2015-08-19
The C64 has a ton of great horizontal scrolling shmups. When it comes to modern vertically scrolling ones the field of competitors is much thinner. Imho SWIV is the best of the few. I actually prefer it on the 64 over the 16bit versions because the smaller sprites and missing shadows make the game look and feel much less overcrowded. Of course it's still intense at times. But it certainly is very beatable (with the heli). The difficulty curve is extremely flat. The game starts out difficult, but it doesn't get MORE difficult. There is a lot going on on screen. Everything moving is done with sprites, the multiplexing is great. The graphics are good overall. I would have liked some in-game music, at least during boss battles, but the spot fx are very fitting. The intro/hi score musics are not among my favourites but are still atmospheric pieces, and I prefer them over the Amiga music. Sadly, there are almost no extra weapons. The upside of this is that losing a life and the collected extra doesn't put you in a big disadvantage like in a lot of other shooters. The level design could have used a little more variety and with only 4 levels the game is not very long, and it's also missing a proper ending. And my usual gripe with Storm releases: the slooow disk loader that luckily at least works very well with parallel port speeders. But nitpicking aside, shmup fans should play this.
