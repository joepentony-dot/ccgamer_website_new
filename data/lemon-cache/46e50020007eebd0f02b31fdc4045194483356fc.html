Monk 2017-11-18
"Rampart King" (urgh); You are mistaken. How could an ORIGINAL version come out two years later than another version? No, please stop worshipping the inferior Nintendoo console, and
give credit, where credit is due. This is a C64 forum anyway, not a place to worship or praise the competitors, at least irrationally, like you did.

Just check the Mobygames entry: mobygames.com/search/quick?q=rampart

That clearly indicates that the NES version came much later. Arcade version is usually the original, and so it is also in this case.

Heck, even Genesis version came before the NES-version. I bet even the DOS version came before the NES version.

In case, these games that are ported to various generations are sometimes very interesting. Rampart is an excellent game, when played with two other players (three players altogether) simultaneously, but quite difficult and tends to become repetitive and boring, and sometimes somewhat unfair as well.

It's a novel idea, which is rare (even at the time), and it's interesting how well it was ported in most of the platforms. I had a lot of fun with this one, but just don't expect your action game freak friends to get enthusiastic about it.
