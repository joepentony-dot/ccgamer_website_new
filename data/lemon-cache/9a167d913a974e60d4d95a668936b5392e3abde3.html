Mike 2002-09-24
The first in the long series of enjoyable platform/adventure games from Codemaster's own Oliver twins (i wander what they are doing now?) You have to collect all the items necessary to make a potion to defeat the evil Wizard, Zaks. To find these objects you must navigate a hostile and dangerous landscape. A magnificent atmosphere and some enjoyable puzzles help make Dizzy a classic 8-bit game. On the down side, this first incarnation of Dizzy only allows you to carry one object at once (very annoying). The Spectrum port graphics, although well drawn do not make good use of the full potential of the C64.
