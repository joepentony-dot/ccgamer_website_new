No piracy game has yet captured the scope and variety of this one. A main screen to navigate, where wind factored highly, a battle screen for ship to ship combat which gave a decent feel for the broadside tactics of cannon warfare, a beautiful merging of your actions and your mens actions when boarding the enemy ships - the better you did in the sword fight against the enemy captain, the betteryour men did. It had options for controlling your crew, it had all of the carribbean to recruit from, it had epic quests to find your lost family in the carribbean, treasure maps, and the ability to raid towns. I still remember my ill fated attempt to cross panama like Sir Henry Morgan, desperately trying to keep troop morale up.

This really was a fantastic piece of gaming, merging strategy, trading, twitch and roleplaying genres practically seemlessly.
