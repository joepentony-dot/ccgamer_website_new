midnight 2005-10-27
Great game. Using Splix strategically with the MAT will stop the controllers pretty much anywhere. To finish the game, get the spanner, dynamite, pick, go to the upper left levels and get the mat and platform (anti grav). Get the chemicals and contam the bath. Go to the upper right level and get 1 x detonator. Get the TIRU capsule (extreme upper right level). Get the pass card by using the anti grav platfor and cross into the room. You will need to regenerate to get out (this is the short cut). Go to the lift shaft and get out at extreme top. Choose left, use pass card to cross the barrier. Use a detonator to destroy controller. Proceed to get "O" and "P" circuits. Go to upper left level but towrd the bottom and find "START" circuits. Remove and place circuits to spell "STOP". Go up and use Pass card to get the crystal. Go to lower right levels. Use dynamite at bottom of reactor (between the two bottom pillars). Once blown up, you have a limited time to get to the tardis which is upper left level but toward the bottom just right of the Master's tardis. Don't carry the crystal for he will come out and take it from you as you pass his tardis. Press F5 making sure you have the capsule. Done.

This game CAN be finished. Damned if I remember exactly how to do it now, but I distinctly remember finishing it twice when I was 10 or 11 years old, so I know it can be done. From memory... Eventually you'll find a shovel (head left), dig your way out of the blue caves (head right), and make it up to the orangy factory place, where some more collecting and fancy jumping will see you reach the lift to the surface.
