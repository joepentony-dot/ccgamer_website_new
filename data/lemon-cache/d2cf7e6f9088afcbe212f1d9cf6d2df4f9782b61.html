C64 Mat 2025-11-04 (8/10)
The six retail Freescape games on the C64 (Driller, Darkside, Castle Master, The Crypt, Total Eclipse and Sphinx Jinx) all hold special memories for me. Ostensibly puzzle games from a first person perspective, they're truly immersive for those with the patience. For those without the patience, note that you can run them all on an emulator with a Super CPU emulated as well - it will speed up the 3D, but won't affect anything else, including the time limit! MAKE SURE you download the instructions, as the games had loads of keyboard commands for things like changing step size, turn angle, crouching and standing, and in the case of Driller and Darkside, even tilting and elevating your vehicle! I recommend using a tool like XPadder to put all the controls onto a modern controller.
