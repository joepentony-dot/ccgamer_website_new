Peter Eriksson 2001-12-29
"Nexus" is in many aspects a mile stone marker in the history of C64 game development. Not only does it feature an excellent soundtrack, digitalized graphics and a real interesting plot, but the gameplay itself is quite sophisticated. Think of "Nexus" as a mix between "Kung Fu Master" and "Mission Impossible" and you'll have "The Matrix" of the 1980s. There's suspense in this game; watch how the mood swings from friendly to hostile and feel yourself caught in the middle. Nexus is highly recommended!
