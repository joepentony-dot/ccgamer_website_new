Coops 2024-08-24 (9/10)
I was a big fan of Wonder Boy at the arcade when I was a kid. So when I first saw this C64 home port of the game at a friend's house, I was amazed. I'm not sure what wizardry was performed to fit this into a single load game on a machine with 64K of memory.

There's no doubt the Sega Master System version is the best home port of Wonder Boy. But the C64 beats it in some areas. These are:
- Mushroom actually changes the fruit to junk food.
- You can see the score and other information while the game is running, not just between lives and levels.
- The snakes only pop up when you get close to them, just like in the arcade.
- The beginning of each round shows the area and round in the same cartoon font as the arcade.
- The splash when you fall in the water is large and detailed.

Unlike the arcade version though, the skateboard in the C64 version is useless and only makes the game harder / impossible. Also the bad fairy's egg isn't speckled so you just need to memorise which eggs to avoid - a bit unfair.

All in all, a very commendable effort for a mid 80s C64 game. And one I still enjoy playing to this day.

One of the best arcade conversions for the C64. This game had it all - the music, the graphics, the levels. Usually you lose something in the translation, but the programmers did an outstanding job here. You get all the insane arcade power-ups like the skateboard and the fairy. The tile-set for each level even changes, just like the arcade. Loved it in the arcades, love it even more on the C64. It's right up there with Ghosts'n'Goblins and Bubble Bobble as far as arcade ports.
