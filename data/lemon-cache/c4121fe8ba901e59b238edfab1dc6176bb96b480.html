Ian Paul Andrews 2004-05-17
Hit the opposing player with any one weak move. Follow that up with as many varied moves as you want. Pin them at your leisure. Played for wins this game is no fun. Played for fun this game is a knock down drag 'em out comedy extravaganza. Remember to make full use of moves that don't exist! Marvel at the reverse clothes line (throw opponent to ropes, face away, stick arm out, wait for them to run into the back of your arm). Cringle at the evil flying hair pull (throw opponent towards ropes but instantly perform the high flying tackle from behind). Laugh at the cancan kick (repeatedly change between the knee lift and the kick). What more can you ask for...
