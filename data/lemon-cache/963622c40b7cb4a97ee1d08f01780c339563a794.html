Well, this game came out during my Amiga-to-PC transition. I ended up playing the demo of the Amiga version endlessly, then shamefully I bought the PC version (and it's pretty decent Windows sequel). A really really entertaining concept. Often the game scared me quite a bit as I'm waiting for something to jump out. I quite liked the PC version. I believe the Amiga one was just as good. The Alien movies were definitely the inspiration and they nailed it pretty good.

If you're frusted, keep trying...I was frustrated a bit too on the demo but soon got the hang of it and when I got the full game I had no problems at all (well, except for getting the stupid PC sound to work). I don't know if this game has aged or not...I'll have to find out. I'm sure there is no way I can get either PC game going but this should emulate just fine and I can (shamelessly again) finally play the Amiga full version.
