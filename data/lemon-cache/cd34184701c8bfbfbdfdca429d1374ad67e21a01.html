Frightmare 2023-11-16 (6/10)
Decent conversion that sits in between the Amiga and NES ones; colors are a bit dull, graphics are a little blocky and controls aren't the best, but it can be fairly enjoyable as a two-players game (not so much against the computer). The penalty-shot and the brawl sub-games are well-done, so is the digitized speech on the titlescreen. Skateball/Skate Wars was probably better, still it can be worth a try.
