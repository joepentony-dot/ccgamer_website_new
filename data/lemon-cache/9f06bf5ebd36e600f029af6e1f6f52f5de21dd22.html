hazzard 2016-04-22 (9/10)
A surprisingly well-made and very playable beat 'em up, especially after the disaster of the original Double Dragon game on C64.
The in-game music by Dave Lowe is simply a joy to listen to, and the graphics are very nicely done with colourful character sprites which animate smoothly.
The overall level design and enemy positions are mostly replicated fairly well from the arcade version.
It is a shame that some of the weapons are missing (like the shovel) and likewise you can't kick the logs or crates along the ground towards your enemies like the arcade version. There also seems to be a bug where you can only pick up weapons from a certain direction.
As mentioned in other reviews, the way your enemies almost always "duck" your attacks feels like a cheap move by the developers to artificially ramp up the difficulty and drag the game out.

Anthony Burns 2010-04-05 (7/10)
A lack of playtesting unfortunately mars this game, suggesting it was rushed out before it had been properly fine-tuned. A shame, as if the collision detection had been fixed, the time limits increased, the combat balanced less in favour of the enemy (whom it is only safe to attack with jump-kicks, which they seem to duck with alarming regularity), and the weapons been made easier to use (some can only be picked up from one side, and some are best avoided altogether), it might have been quite a classic. Even so, the graphics are very good, with some clever touches (cartwheeling enemies, comical attacks, enemies breaking through walls, neat level transitions); the music is even better than the arcade's, and the digitised sound effects, though not always clear, are an atmospheric addition. The final battle is also quite a climax ...
