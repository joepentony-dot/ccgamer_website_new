Monk 2015-06-12
A good game, but the Atari version is just so much better. In all regards.

And this game exemplifies nicely how Atari's red looks better than the C64's red on real CRT televisions.

The wizard looks so stupid on the C64, and is such a disappointment in the gameplay strategy - on the Atari version, you at least get to feel there's something magical happening, with spells being conjured and the skulls rising from the ground, and the 'Crackpot'-like gameplay is great.

The C64 version has just some goth teen throwing some rocks at you while posing really idiotically.

And don't get me started on how wrong the flame looks, sounds and feels, urgh.

I seriously recommend the Atari version over this (I played them side by side on real machines, just by the way).
