hazzard 2024-02-09 (5/10)
Predator is a good-looking game with a nifty introduction sequence and catchy title tune, plus the in-game sprites are highly detailed and animate fluidly. The tension of the movie with the jungle setting and predator stalking you with its crosshairs has been ported over very well.
Where this game comes up short is in the gameplay department - to put it simply, it stinks! Trying to aim your weapon properly feels fiddly at best and the difficulty and overall gameplay feels unbalanced and rushed.
It was pretty cool being able to cover yourself in mud and pick up the radio later in the game, but these events ultimately have zero influence on the final battle with the Predator. To make matters worse, I happened to own the version of the game which was bugged, meaning you couldn't crush the Predator with the log, rendering the game unwinnable. How the testers back then didn't notice this is a mystery for the ages 🤔
The developers should've spent a bit more time refining the gameplay, adding in-game music, and actually making the game beatable! Stick with watching the movie and ignore this game.
