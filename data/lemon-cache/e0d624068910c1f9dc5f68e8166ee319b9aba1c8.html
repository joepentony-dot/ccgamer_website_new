Nayphee 2024-12-03 (7/10)
An extremely well presented Zaxxon-inspired game where you have to "win races" - a term which is used very loosely as there's no opponents to race, but a whole lot of blasting of aliens and scenery.
A nice concept in theory. In practice, the gameplay suffers from repetition, and you can easily cheese it. Just hack out a channel with your ship and then go full speed until the end. There are bonus points for not doing this on some stages, but that's not much incentive. Is it fun though? Well you can get quite far - until the stages where one hit on the scenery can brutally and frustratingly end your game.
