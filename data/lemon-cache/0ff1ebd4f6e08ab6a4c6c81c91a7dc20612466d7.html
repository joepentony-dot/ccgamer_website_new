There was once a time when a simple chess game was considered a triple A title! Nowadays you have difficulties even *finding* any chess game for a contemporary console, let alone a half-decent one (the last good chess game I came across was Konami's Chess Kingdoms on the PSP).

Battle Chess is just chess with animations. When you take another figure, you can see a funny (actually: silly) animation especially made for the pairing of figures. Well, it's funny for a while until you turn off the animations, change the figures to normal Stanton-like figurines, start playing seriously and suddenly discover that Battle Chess has a damn good chess engine behind it! And this is were the true fun begins.

PantheraLeo 2008-02-10 (7/10)
It's chess like you have never seen before.

Its a standard chess game with a fun twist. The chess pieces actually battle it out in amusing and fun little sequences that occur every time you or the other player takes a piece. So you will have the Queen use a magic spell on another piece or the Rook turning into a rock monster smashing another one as they claim the spot. It adds a nice twist to the old game of chess.

But push come to shove, its still just a chess game in every way. There is nothing else to the game besides chess with some fun little moments before a spot is taken. Yeah it adds something extra to the mix but just seems like there could have been more here then there is for some reason.

But if you like chess this is the way to play it. The moments that come between each capture of a spot is great and seems even greater when you are playing against another human and you both get to watch the piece get destroyed or humiliated somehow before they claim the spot. Its a nice twist on chess.. But its still just a chess game any way you slice it. 7/10
