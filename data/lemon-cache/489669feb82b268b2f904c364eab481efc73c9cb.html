Technical;
Arcade has a horiz. Play area of 208 pixels and sprites are 16 pixels wide so you need 13 sprites to fill the screen horizontally. On the C64 you have a playarea 232 pixels (116 real) and sprites are 24 pix. Wide (12 real) so you need only 10 to fill the play area that's why it doesn't have correct proportions and this breaks the game too.

COMMENT: Does not cover the arcade version. "Bombjack 1 was based on the old coin op machine. The title tune is a Jean-Michel Jarre cover. We used to copy tunes like this all the time in the early days, I think the legislation at the time didn't cover computer based music, that situation didn't last very long though and we then needed to do original compositions (supposedly). Only tune 2 is from the original arcade machine. I seem to remember that in the original version of the music I used Lady Madonna by the Beatles." (MC)
