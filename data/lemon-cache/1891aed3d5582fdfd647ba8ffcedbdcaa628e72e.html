Gurce 2019-07-29
I spent some time trying to decipher the Thai text in the background of the box art.

I think it says:

Phithi khao phu yort mongkol 25 maseng

Which I could loosely translate to "opening ceremony for people of great auspiciousness - 25 snakes".

The Thai-"Y" character (Yor Yak) was omitted from the "great auspiciousness" term. Initially, I thought the artist just lazily omitted it.

But then I noticed that the Thai "Y" character exists on the boxing glove, alongside the English Y character.

Perhaps the artist was giving the impression that the boxer had punched the "Y" out of the background banner.

Oddly (or perhaps intentionally), this modifies the meaning to be something more like "unauspicious".
