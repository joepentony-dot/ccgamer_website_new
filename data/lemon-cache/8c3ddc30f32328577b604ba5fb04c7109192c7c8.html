Monk 2014-12-01
As far as the game itself,

Ninja is a great classic. There was a version called "Ninja Warriors", but that might have been an error by someone creating those turbo tape collections.

The only things about this game that deserve some spanking is that it's too short, too easy to complete, and the graphics could be improved a bit (even the Torii in the lake is fake - THAT design does not exist in any real Torii).

The playability is great, even exciting, when you take on five thugs and an evil ninja, and win! The strategic approach with the shuriken and kunai added to the gameplay.

The kick-animation used to inspire and amaze me back in the day, and the brutal swordhit is very ninja-ish.

The music of course deserves lots of praise, it's an atmospheric masterpiece, though not authentically oriental-sounding.

A fun, immersive saturday morning game to play first thing after waking up.

Paul: I found the same bug accidentally! It was a surreal moment to see some 'new scenery' in a game this old.. Then things got too weird and the game crashed, I think.
