yaztromo 2014-12-15 (8/10)
Roam around, find the items scattered about the map to improve your bike for the race. Some are in hard to reach places like on the high beams of the construction site, and require careful manoeuvring of your bike to reach. When you've got enough pieces then go to the race and drive up the finishing straight!

Great faux 3-d graphics for the time, and the catchy Rob Hubbard tune is instantly recognisable and timeless.

This game is an example that a lack of depth doesn't make a bad game as long as it's fun. Driving around collecting items enjoying the speed, graphics and music is a blast.

The controls are superbly done and deaths mainly feel like your own fault.

It only loses points for sometimes trying to spot the next object can become a chore. As you find them, it's best to note down their locations for next time.

8/10
