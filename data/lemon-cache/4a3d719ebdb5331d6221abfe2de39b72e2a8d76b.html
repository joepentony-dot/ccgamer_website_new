R.S.S. 2003-05-12
Be warned, this is a long review...
This game is unbelieveable. The 16 levels didn't come in any specific order, which was a great idea. The only 2 levels that were always in order was the first, Hide the Gribblets in the Cave, and the last, if you were one of the very lucky few to get that far, Back to the Nursery.
The game was rock hard. In fact, it took me 13 years! But seeing the end message, All Gribblets safe and sound, made it all worthwhile. When I did beat it, I did it in one shot without saving, which made me feel good inside! The Flying Creatures, psi grubs, web, mutating creatures, the energy, known as psi system and of course, the black crab Seon were all brill. Another classic from Hewson and Andrew Braybrook. 10/10
Jase Jennings 2002-07-10
My favourite C64 game, and one I will always cherish. Oh to bring this screaming and kicking into the 21st century using the quake engine ... The thought of it has me palpitating.

It's a game I actually managed to complete, although I don't think you could possibly do so from start to finish in a single sitting, it does get particularly difficult with creatures morphing ever more quickly, and Seon getting ever more agressive. I would freeze the game after each level and save it to tape, and even then it was no walkover.

Breathtakingly original, beautiful conception, a masterpiece of fun gameplay.

Play it today and pretend that your 13 again
