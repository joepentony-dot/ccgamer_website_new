Tskreli 2004-06-04
Very True. A sparkling sequel (?) to DemonStalkers, This time with Landscapes, Towers, Dungeons, Rivers to Float by in, hidden traps, more items to use, and the infamous slow death curse. First time you play, you're sure to get lost unless you stick to the main trails, but other puzzles will have you finding new screens every time you play. Make certain you have your spinny-puzzle-disc for this one, too. Hours of top-down rpg-ish action fun. Watch for Black slime!
neuromancer 2002-12-23
Same people who made the excellent demonstalkers. This game is a two-player dream. It's a really involving adventure, with some puzzlesolving, and gauntlet style arcade battles. You really have to cooperate with a friend and be prepared to invest a LOT of time into it. My friend and I still get together every summer and play (but we always have to start at the beginning, bc we both forget what we were doing). Try to get your hands on a boxed copy bc of the map and nice artwork. Amazing game.
