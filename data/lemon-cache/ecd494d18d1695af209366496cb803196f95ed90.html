DigitalQuirk 2007-10-16 (3/10)
I had a love-hate relationship with this game. I loved Transformers, and I hated this game.

This game is a platformer where you run around and collect puzzle pieces as one of five Autobots: Hound, Jazz, Bumblebee, Mirage, and Optimus Prime. They exist in silos that roll up when you select them. You can place them in these silos when you wish to recharge them or select another one. You can also make them transform, fly, and shoot their weapon. The SID version of the theme song isn't bad.

That's the good part. Now for the bad. Fly up and just touch the bottom of a platform or ledge, and you explode and die. Land on anything but a perfectly level surface, and you explode and die. Doesn't matter if you're the all-powerful Optimus Prime, or the pipsqueak Bumblebee. You will explode and die if you run out of energy. You can also make your man explode and die by simply selecting another Autobot! At the same time, your enemies; whether it's Megatron or Starscream, can do suicide runs at you; they explode, doing little damage to you! You can also take a lot of shots from them before dying.

You do get to transform, but you won't get very far. Odds are good that you'll fall off the edge of a platform and crash and die. That's pretty much the theme of the game; your enemies, otherwise deft, will crash and die into you.

The only virtue of this game is the Transformers. Replace these characters with something unknown, and this game would have crashed and died a long time ago.
