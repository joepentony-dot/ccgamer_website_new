Nayphee 2023-10-26 (9/10)
A solid conversion of the arcade game. Two players can play, and while there's no rotary joystick on the C64, you could use keys to lock or unlock the direction of your player if you wanted. The game is actually very fair - you can get through it without losing a life if you have the skill - as strategic picking off of the enemies is what it's all about. You can, of course, rush in and "go commando" too - so the trigger-happy and impatient are also catered for. However, knowing when to push forward and when to hang back was part of the skillset required to win.
Half the fun is advancing through the map to see what's up next, and with the addition of functional tanks and powerups, it's one of the best games of it's type on the C64.

alucardthe69th 2023-06-16 (4/10)
Ikari Warriors is one of the very best arcade games of the 1980s. The C64 version seemed to take forever to come out and when it eventually did it was certainly not worth the wait. And it pains me to think that the Amstrad version (I wasn't a fan of the Amstrad!!!) was such a great conversion, to get this instead, well let's just say (deep breath) at least they tried. It's kind of playable, but considering this came out two years after the arcade and around a year after the Amstrad version it was a massive let down. I didn't know at the time that there was a US version released in 1986, but having now seen it I am so glad I missed it - it makes Elite's effort seem positively spectacular in comparison. Basically if you want to play Ikari Warriors stick with the original arcade game!
