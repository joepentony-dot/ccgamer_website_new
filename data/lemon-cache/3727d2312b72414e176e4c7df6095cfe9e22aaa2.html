Davey Davey 2024-01-31 (5/10)
This one is a bit of a shame. I remember buying Kixx games of the ZX Spectrum and some would have a catalogue listing of their releases and one of them was Championship Wrestling and I so wanted to buy it but could only find the C64 version, and there was a reason for that, because it was never released for the Spectrum.

Now here I am finally playing it years later, and well, I am quite disappointed. I suppose it's something I missed out on and would have enjoyed much more in my youth but I was underwhelmed to say the least. The setup is there, clone Hulk Hogan, clone Ultimate Warrior, clone Ivan Koloff, clone Hillbilly Jim, big muscled dudes doing battle in a tiny ring, what's not to like? Then the bell rang and six seconds later Koloff pinned Hogan and I lost. That was it. That was the match, it happened and if you blinked you missed it.

Now I didn't get the long loading times stated here but if I did, well, all that loading for six seconds? Now I did get a little better over time and did win a couple of matches along the way and it was nice doing some moves for a change, a body slam, an aeroplane spin, even throwing my opponent out of the ring at one point. But honestly, I can't sing my praises on this. It's certainly better than Rock 'n Wrestle on the Spectrum, another one everyone raves about and yet I find it to be quite appalling. This isn't that bad but I can't help but shrug my shoulders and wonder what the hype was about...much like wrestling today in fact.
