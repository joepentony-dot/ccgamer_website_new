Hordriss 2014-09-04
I've recently played this for the first time, and have to say I can't understand the love for it.

They've done a decent job with the visuals, but the gameplay just doesn't hold up. The original coin-up was fast, frantic and fun. This is not. The one button joystick does a game like this no favours, the controls are not that responsive and the collision detection is appalling. And the game commits the ultimate beat-em-up sin of having enemies that will knock you down continuously and because of the timing of their attacks you don't have a chance of getting back up again.

The music is also so generic and dull.

Yet another poor arcade conversion on the C64.
