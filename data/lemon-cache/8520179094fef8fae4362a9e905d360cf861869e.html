dougmcse 2021-05-10 (10/10)
Almost 40 years later and this game still stands the test of time. It's a perfect combination of puzzler and arcade runner. You need lightning reflexes and the ability to strategize, all while counting vertically and horizontally to make sure you don't dig yourself into a hole you can't escape.

The game is very responsive and the single character-sized Lode Runner meant the levels were much larger by comparison. Add 150 levels and the included level editor and you have endless playability. I've played many other ports and sequels of this game and this is still the best.
