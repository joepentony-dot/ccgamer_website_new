mike_tyson 2011-11-25
Moonstone is a singular masterpiece. We played this during my highschool years countless times, it's endless fun in multiplayer. Combining boardgame elements with a vs fighting game against human and computer controlled opponents, with plenty of gore as the icing on the cake. Looking back, the main thing missing from this game was that unlike in typical vs fighters like Street Fighter II, here each player basically controls the same character (only in different color). If they had done it like SF II and its myriad clones, so that there would have been four or even more different knights with different abilities and movesets to choose from, then Moonstone would certainly had had more lasting impact. Also, the battles especially against human opponents were over really quick. As it is, there are so many fantastic ideas in Moonstone, which none of them have caught on in other games. A fantastic Amiga game, which could have changed the way vs fighters are designed today. Play this with three friends!

Upgrading your weapons, using spells, gambling for more (or less) money, Stonehenge and the tower, which can be used for extra help in the game, the other knights (especially when playing with more players, this is much fun!); it all adds up to the enormous fun of this game. Graphics are good, sound is alright and the playability is after some go's easy to master, but sometimes a little easy, when you know the weaknesses of the monsters. And... Yeah, this game is bloody! That adds up to the fun too! One of the best slashers out there! Play it!!!

Graphics: 7/10

Playability: 9/10

Overall: 9/10

Madjock 2004-12-03 (10/10)
Moonstone was an instant classic if there was ever one, relatively unknown in the mass market the game was immensely playable, especially with four players (two maximum at one time). After the atmospheric intro, you play a lone knight - sent to a corner of the map to find a piece of the moonstone. Collect all four and make your way to Stonehenge for the final showdown.

Loading was a killer with this title, but it was indisputably worth it. I'd love to see a modern remake.
