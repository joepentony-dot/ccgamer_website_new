Nayphee 2023-10-11 (8/10)
This game really captured the feeling of tension and horror of the movie in a way that no other game had up until that point. The terror of seeing that alien approach and turn your player's avatar into that of an alien was scary stuff. The game becomes even more nightmarish as aliens cut the power to the base so that everything is in the dark and can barely see anything. The real horror of this game, however, is the micromanagement of your team of marines, the conservation of extremely limited ammo, and navigating your way through a base that can only be described as a maze (and a kind of in-game copy protection! - you can't hope to win without that map). That's also perhaps the most frustrating part about playing it, but perhaps simultaneously the most accurate to the source material.

As you young boy I was both fascinated and scared of Alien after seeing photos in a magazine and reading a bit about it. Aliens definitely falls into survival horror category and should be treated as such, there may be things that don
t work but games like Alien Isolation have taken all that on board and are great examples of the evolution of horror games.
This is a slow burn play with jolts of action with some quick menu interface.
I score Aliens 7/10
