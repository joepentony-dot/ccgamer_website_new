MartinJSUK 2025-03-01 (6/10)
I never played this much and was never keen, and in truth I'm still not. That said, I can see the appeal of playing it as a two-player game back in the day - I guess you had to be there to 'get' it. The overemphasis on combat is to the detriment of the racing gameplay, in my (seemingly unique) opinion.

Controls are poor, and the removal of the handling improvement upgrades from the original just exacerbates this issue. No longer being able to select the courses in order also removes the strategic element of removing the difficult ones first before the computer cars improve in quality. Graphics are a little too murky and drab (though the window is bigger than in the first one), scrolling doesn't always keep up, and the limited view at the edge of the track is also irritating. Whether you clear the jumps seems random. Further frustration sets in when you get regenerated on top of a mine, or hit a mine inside a tunnel.

Maybe the questions to gain or lose money are hilarious when playing it at a young age, but without having played the game back then they just feel forced, unfunny and a needless random / memory test element - lose almost all your money just before the last race, as I just did, and you're not happy.

Putting time into it now, it offers little compared to contemporaries like Super Off Road Racer, Nitro and perhaps even the first Super Cars (though a lot of this game's issues apply there too).

Theseus 2016-07-29 (9/10)
This is indeed a very fine game which even today remains highly playable. While the graphics are at best just above average, the gameplay is excellent and remains so even after one has completed the three difficulties several times. The difficulty "hard" is indeed quite hard, which allows for many hours of challenging gameplay.

An interesting feature is that the armory-shop, which appears in-between the races, allows for speculation. You simply buy certain weapons when the price is low, refrain from using them and sell them again after the next race if the price is sufficiently high. This can potentially make your driver unashamedly wealthy which in turn allows for an excessive amount of weapons in the last races. I wonder if this was intentionally planned as a part of the game.

I have only come across one annoying mistake: If you buy three motors your car will not be able to make more than a few jumps in a row. (You will be jumping too long and your car will blow up). This is a problem in the hard races, which features several levels with three or more jumps in a row, and it thus makes the hard level even harder.

Overall an excellent game.
