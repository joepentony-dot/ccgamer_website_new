In the 80s I had two obsessions- Skating and playing my C64 so of course I geeked out over Skate or Die.
Having played 720* and Skate Rock every week I was curious to check out the latest game on the market.
Right off the bat the presentation is great with some synthesised guitar riffs and detailed artwork that also conveyed the attitude of skating. My fave events were Half-pipe freestyle, Downhill Jam and the Jousting (two player)
Skate or Die is a fun game that has some variety and can be quite competitive when playing with a friend. For sure one of the best sports games on the commodore.
