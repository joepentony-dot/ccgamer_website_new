Monk 2011-08-21 (10/10)
This comes about 5-7 years late, but better late than never. A few corrections to other's wrong assumptions.

Midgard:
1) It's not a lawnmover, it's a plough.
2) You do NOT have to 'start over again' - you lose only SOME distance you have gained each time you die (it only seems that way if you are such a bad player that you are able to gain only a very tiny distance - in that case you lose it all of course)
3) Once you practice it a little bit, and get 'into the zone', the game is surprisingly easy - you can usually complete the first level two times out of three, and the third was a failure because you just messed up too much with stupid mistakes.

It IS a difficult game, but from another perspective, it's an excellent "Zen"-game. When you empty your mind and focus, you will notice completion of the first level happens almost automatically. Too many thoughts in your mind and you will fumble.

Koivu: the 'bug' is more like a 'feature' - it's there to make you realize you can't achieve anything worthwhile by cheating and choosing the easy way. A very good teaching. It's a temptation that a good player avoids.

TITLE: Uuno Turhapuro [from the movie]
ARTIST: Jaakko Salo
COMMENT: Title theme of many Finnish "Uuno Turhapuro" movies.
2
TITLE: Autumn
ARTIST: Jori Olkkonen (Yip)
COMMENT: Another version of Yip's tune from Scroll Machine, played using a simpler music routine, because the original music routine interfered with the game's code: "I had to ask Jori to make the music with slightly less sparse synching, but when I did that the sound quality dropped a bit. The result was a bearable compromise. The screen glitches were almost gone and the music still sounds fairly good." (From an interview with Pasi Hytönen, the programmer of the game, in MikroBITTI 4/87)
