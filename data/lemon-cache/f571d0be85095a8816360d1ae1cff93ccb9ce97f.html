MarkTheMorose 2010-05-24
Quite simply one of the best, most addictive and immersive games I've ever played. Despite the 'zero graphics, rubbish sound' appearances, this will draw you in and make you think. I dusted it off recently and played it under emulation, and got a bit further than I did 'back then'.

@ MrGunn: are you sure you're using the correct key? On the C64, I think it was the mathematical symbol 'up arrow'. Now, under emulation, that might be converted to an asterisk, '*' but there are two on the PC keyboard, one is shifted 8, the other is on the numeric keypad. Try both.

indi 2007-10-25 (9/10)
This game has to be the best immersive game I've ever played. Because its not trying to be anything other than a BBS screen, it feels like your actually taking part, not just trying to imagine you are. Theres no fancy progressive system, you just have to discover things that are all openly stored there. I got the same thrill playing it now as I did back in the 80's. Amazing game, and great fun for anyone that loved the old BBS scene.
