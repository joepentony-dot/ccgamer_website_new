Sparky-D 2022-06-02 (9/10)
I was always a bit ambivalent about this game, but I played it more often back then, especially together with one of my best buddies. We even drew ourselves a map of the game, which included all rooms of this huge house, that we had discovered so far.
The extremely high difficulty-level in some rooms, in which the player really need to jump pixel-precise, directly from out of the movement more than one time, sometimes took away the fun a little bit, of continuing the game. But sooner or later, we always found the motivation again, to go on playing "Jet Set Willy", because we wanted to see more rooms of this building.

We never managed, to play this game completely through and even today, I still haven't been able, to finish it, although I'm actually a good player. I also read in the internet, that the game has a bug and can't be finished, but as far as I know, there exists a crack-version in the meantime, that had this problem fixed.
The difficulty-level in "Jet Set Willy" is really hard and there are also some unfair spots, in a few rooms, where you also need a little bit luck, to make it. But the game really has a great atmosphere overall, combined with good classical music and a lot different enemy-sprites, therefore I can only rate it well and give it 9 points. "Jet Set Willy 2" never could give me the atmosphere, that this first part here gives me, that's why I prefer this game here.

With a slightly more balanced difficulty, I would even give this game 10 points.

Johan Grandin 2002-02-05
I prefer the C64 version to the Spectrum version (original by legend Matthew Smith), but I started off with the C64 version (by Shahid Ahmad) and learned to master its stairs and ropes.

"Rich from his past mining exploits, Willy has bought a huge mansion with over 60 rooms, most of which he has never seen. There's been a mammoth party and the guests have left the place in a dreadful mess. Willy just want to go to bed, but his housekeeper, the nightmarish Maria, won't let him until every bit and piece has been picked up and tidied away. "

This, along with Manic Miner, is the most fun and addictive platform game I've ever played. The rooms in the house all have a distinctive feel and left a permanent impression on me. It really felt like being in 'The attic' or 'On the roof' when one managed to reach those rooms! The game was huge (for its time, 60+ rooms), hard and challenging - yet the atmosphere and pure fun kept you going!
