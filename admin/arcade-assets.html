Lost Sizzler Playlist Workflow

  • Batch import: choose Auto-categorise Music Batch and select all prepared files together. Names containing exploration, combat, sanctuary, named, loadula/lodula or stalker are assigned automatically.
  • Multiple tracks: uploading to a Lost Sizzler category adds tracks rather than replacing earlier music.
  • Random rotation: the game avoids immediately repeating the previous song when alternatives are available.
  • Crossfading: category changes and consecutive tracks blend over 2.5 seconds.
  • Exploration remains dominant: General Exploration plays during ordinary rooms and resumes after combat, sanctuary, named-enemy or Count Loadula states end.
  • Individual control: every uploaded song can be previewed, disabled, re-enabled or deleted separately.
  • Gameplay art is protected: production character animation, enemies, bosses, hazards, collectibles and power-ups remain versioned with the games so an audio upload cannot alter collision or animation behaviour.
  • Other assets: backgrounds, normal sound effects and Commodore Quest scene music retain their existing single-replacement behaviour.
Recommended audio: MP3, 256 kbps, 48 kHz stereo, with no long silence at either end. Each file must be below 25 MB.
