Quietly keeping CCG going

Support CCG

The archive, reviews and videos remain available without payment. Anyone who chooses to help can do so through one of the understated options below. There are no compulsory prompts and no public display of contribution amounts.

Watch, Comment and Share

Watching a video, leaving a useful comment, sharing a playlist or subscribing also helps the channel reach more Commodore fans.

Open YouTube

What voluntary support helps with

  • website hosting, domains, databases and archive maintenance;
  • research and preparation for longer videos and retrospectives;
  • capture equipment, microphones, storage and production software;
  • retro hardware and games used in channel coverage;
  • livestreams, community tools and continued website development.

No obligation

The public archive remains open regardless of whether somebody contributes. Support should only be given when affordable, and choosing not to support never reduces access to the normal website.

Businesses seeking a sponsorship or collaboration should use the separate Work with CCG page.
