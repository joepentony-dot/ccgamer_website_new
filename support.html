What support helps fund

  • website hosting, domains, databases and archive maintenance;
  • research, magazine checking and preparation for long-form videos;
  • capture hardware, microphones, storage and production software;
  • retro hardware, games and preservation materials used in coverage;
  • community tools, livestreams and continued development of site features.

Passive support routes

Purchasing from the official shop, using disclosed affiliate links, watching monetised videos and maintaining a membership are designed to support the project without requiring direct administration for every transaction.

Businesses seeking sponsorships, advertising or collaborations should use the separate Work with CCG page.

No obligation

The public archive remains available without a contribution. Support is voluntary and should only be given when affordable. Membership, donation, shop and payment processing are handled by the relevant provider under its own terms.
