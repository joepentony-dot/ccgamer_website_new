Original firmware and user-supplied media
-----------------------------------------
Original proprietary Commodore/MOS KERNAL, BASIC, CHARGEN and 1541 DOS ROM
images are NOT distributed by CCG. Users can choose legally obtained original
firmware through the local ROM vault for more complete game compatibility.
Locally selected user media is not uploaded to CCG.
The separate CCG Online Library carries only owner-authorised hosted media.

Not bundled by this CCG route
-----------------------------
Optional C64 READY 3D Retro Vibes models, upstream PWA shell and upstream
product branding/assets are not required for the CCG emulator route.
