ROM images and user media
-------------------------
Commodore/MOS KERNAL, BASIC, CHARGEN and 1541 ROM images are not distributed by
CCG. Games, disks, tapes and cartridges selected by a user remain user-supplied
media and are not bundled with the emulator.

Not bundled by this CCG route
-----------------------------
The CCG route does not bundle Commodore ROM images or copyrighted game media.
The optional C64 READY 3D Retro Vibes models, upstream PWA shell and upstream
product branding/assets are not required for the CCG emulator route.
