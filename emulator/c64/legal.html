Open-source licences

The browser emulator includes third-party software distributed under open-source licences. Its copyright acknowledgements, relevant licences and source information are available in the third-party notices and source repository.

SID audio components

The SID voice, transistor-level filter/mixer, nonlinear volume stage and external RC output model include GPL-derived material translated or derived from reSID as distributed with VICE 3.10. This route now uses the upstream SID AudioWorklet and its embedded WebAssembly engine, with JavaScript reSID fallback. Technical provenance and corresponding-source references for those parts are recorded in the bundled third-party notices.

View bundled third-party/source notices.

ROM images

Commodore KERNAL, BASIC, character and drive ROM images are not distributed by CCG. Users must supply their own legally obtained copies. The CCG ROM setup stores validated ROM bytes locally in the user's browser.

Online Library media

The CCG Online Library is a separate media manifest. Only files that Cheeky Commodore Gamer is permitted to redistribute may be listed there. The library does not alter the separate requirement for C64 system firmware.
