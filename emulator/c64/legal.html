System firmware and no-setup mode

CCG does not distribute the original Commodore/Microsoft KERNAL, BASIC, character or 1541 drive ROMs. The emulator instead includes the generic BASIC and KERNAL pair and matching open character ROM from the independent MEGA65 Open ROMs project. These firmware binaries are unmodified and released under the GNU Lesser General Public License version 3 or later. They are replacements, not original Commodore firmware.

The built-in Open ROMs boot the real emulated CPU, but do not reproduce the exact copyrighted Commodore startup firmware and do not support every game, command, loader or feature. Visitors needing maximum compatibility may supply their own legally obtained Commodore system ROMs through SYSTEM ROMS; those files stay in that browser's local storage.

Open ROMs provenance: upstream commit ad178dbe4d48cd6a317737a8e0e7e662f7e33d32, unmodified files bin/basic_generic.rom, bin/kernal_generic.rom and bin/chargen_openroms.rom. See the upstream copyright and licence notice, LGPL-3.0, GPL-3.0 and the corresponding upstream source.

Online Library media

The public Online Library is restricted to games with stated redistribution permission. Visitors can also load their own C64 disk, tape, cartridge and program files directly from their device. The optional original Commodore system firmware remains user-supplied; open-source Open ROM firmware is bundled for basic no-setup use.
