Where does the Speedball 2 “Ice Cream!” sound come from?

The famous “Ice cream! Ice cream!” stadium announcement comes from Speedball 2: Brutal Deluxe, released for the Commodore Amiga in 1990. Developed by the Bitmap Brothers and published in the UK by Image Works, the game puts armoured teams into a fast, physical futuristic sport. That unexpected cry from the crowd became a favourite bit of Amiga nostalgia.

Who created the sound effects in Speedball 2?

The original Amiga credits list Richard Joseph for music code and sound effects, with Nation 12 credited for the music. These are the game's overall audio credits; they do not establish who voiced the ice-cream announcement. You can cross-check the Amiga credits on MobyGames.
