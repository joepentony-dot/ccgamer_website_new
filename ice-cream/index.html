Where does the Speedball 2 “Ice Cream!” sound come from?

The famous “Ice cream! Ice cream!” stadium announcement comes from Speedball 2: Brutal Deluxe, released for the Commodore Amiga in 1990. Developed by the Bitmap Brothers and published in the UK by Image Works, the game puts armoured teams into a fast, physical futuristic sport. That unexpected cry from the crowd became a favourite bit of Amiga nostalgia.

Why was Speedball 2: Brutal Deluxe so highly regarded?

Speedball 2: Brutal Deluxe became one of the Bitmap Brothers' best-known Amiga games, earning 96% in Zzap! (March 1991) and 95% in CU Amiga (February 1991). Amiga Power later placed it at number three in its all-time Amiga games chart, a position it also held in subsequent rankings.

Who composed the Speedball 2 soundtrack?

Simon Rogers wrote the music for Nation 12, while Richard Joseph handled the Amiga music code and sound effects. The soundtrack won Best 16-bit Soundtrack at the 1991 Golden Joystick Awards, and the in-game samples became just as memorable as the music.

Who voiced the Speedball 2 Ice Cream seller?

The famous “Ice cream! Ice cream!” cry is commonly attributed to Michael Burdett, reportedly credited under the name Jams O'Donnell. The surviving game credits thank “Jams O'Donnel” for the voice; the identification of Burdett comes from later accounts rather than those credits alone.

Historical references: original Zzap! and CU Amiga reviews, Golden Joystick award and Amiga Power rankings, voice credit, and later voice attribution.
