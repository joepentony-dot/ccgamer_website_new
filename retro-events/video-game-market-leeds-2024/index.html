Video Game Market Leeds 2024 – Royal Armouries Retro Gaming Finds

On-location coverage from the Video Game Market Leeds 2024 event at the Royal Armouries.

This Retro Events episode follows a visit to the Leeds video game market and captures the experience of browsing retro stalls in person. It highlights event atmosphere, collector interest and the social side of retro gaming meetups.
