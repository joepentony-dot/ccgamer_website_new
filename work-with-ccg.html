Products and services

Reviews and demonstrations

Retro hardware, licensed products, books, events, controllers, preservation tools and suitable software may be considered for editorial coverage. Supplying a product does not guarantee publication or a favourable conclusion.

People and stories

Interviews and features

Developers, musicians, artists, publishers, collectors, event organisers and preservation projects can propose interviews, archive contributions or historical features.

Suitable partners

  • licensed retro hardware, mini systems, controllers and accessories;
  • retro events, exhibitions, museums and preservation organisations;
  • books, magazines, documentaries and officially licensed merchandise;
  • independent games with a credible Commodore, Amiga or retro connection;
  • capture, streaming, storage or production tools relevant to the audience; and
  • retailers and services with a strong fit for retro-gaming enthusiasts.

Editorial and disclosure standards

  • Paid relationships are labelled using direct wording such as Advertisement, Sponsored or Paid promotion.
  • Gifted or loaned products are disclosed where relevant.
  • Factual claims supplied by a partner may be checked and edited for accuracy and audience relevance.
  • Final opinions remain with Cheeky Commodore Gamer.
  • Campaigns involving unlawful downloads, counterfeit goods, deceptive claims or unsuitable products will not be accepted.

Affiliate relationships are covered by the Affiliate Disclosure.

Request a proposal
