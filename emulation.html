How to Play Commodore 64 Games Today

The Commodore 64 remains one of the most iconic home computers ever created. Released in 1982, it went on to sell millions of units and introduced an entire generation to computer gaming.

Today there are several ways to enjoy Commodore 64 games — from modern software emulators to dedicated hardware recreations and modern retro consoles. This guide explains the most popular ways to experience classic C64 games today.

Quick Start – The Fastest Way to Play C64 Games

  • PC or Laptop: Install a Commodore 64 emulator such as VICE.
  • Plug-and-Play Hardware: Use modern recreations like THEC64 Mini or THEC64 Maxi.
  • Retro Console: Evercade systems offer licensed retro collections.

Commodore 64 Emulators

Emulators recreate the Commodore 64 hardware on modern computers.

  • VICE – the most widely used Commodore emulator and available on multiple operating systems.
  • Hoxs64 – highly accurate Windows emulator with excellent compatibility.
  • CCS64 – long-running emulator popular with C64 enthusiasts.

Modern Commodore Hardware

For players who prefer a plug-and-play experience, modern recreations of the Commodore 64 provide an easy way to enjoy classic games on modern televisions.

THEC64 Maxi – full-size Commodore 64 recreation with working keyboard

THEC64 Mini Black Edition – compact plug-and-play C64 console

Common Emulator Problems

  • Game will not load – ensure the disk image is attached to Drive 8.
  • No joystick detected – configure joystick port settings in the emulator.
  • Game runs too fast – disable warp mode.
