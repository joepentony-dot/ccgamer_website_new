Emulation Guide

This page documents recommended emulators and setup approaches used across the Cheeky Commodore Gamer library. It’s designed as a practical reference rather than a technical deep dive.

Everything here focuses on stability, authenticity and ease of use — the same setups used when capturing footage for the channel.

Commodore 64 Emulation

The Commodore 64 remains one of the best-supported retro platforms for emulation. With the right setup, accuracy and compatibility are extremely high.

Recommended Emulator

VICE is the primary emulator used for C64 coverage. It offers excellent hardware accuracy, wide format support and consistent behaviour across platforms.

  • Accurate CPU, VIC-II and SID emulation
  • Supports disk, tape and cartridge formats
  • Available on Windows, macOS and Linux
  • Ideal for both casual play and recording

Common Formats

  • .D64 – Standard floppy disk images
  • .T64 – Tape container format
  • .PRG – Single-file programs
  • .CRT – Cartridge images

General Setup Notes

  • Use NTSC or PAL timing that matches the original release
  • Enable true drive emulation for disk-based titles
  • Map joystick to keyboard or controller early
  • Save states are useful but not always authentic
