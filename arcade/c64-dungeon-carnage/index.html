Objectives & Rulebook

One route, three clear steps. Optional rooms make you stronger; they never block the exit.

◎

1 · COMPLETE THE HUD GOAL

Keys, generators, rescue, exploration or guardian. The five-minute reminder always shows what comes next.

◇

2 · CLEAR THE SIGIL GREAT HALL

Enter the opened reinforced gate. It locks behind you while the Sigil Warden, its Praetorian and their minions defend the great hall. Defeat every defender, then collect the Exit Sigil.

⇥

3 · REACH THE EXIT

Follow the radar marker back to the floor exit. The Sigil opens the route to the next floor.

✦

DEATH STALKER

Exactly one can appear on each floor. It can open ordinary room doors, and normal weapons only knock it back. A lit carried torch frightens it away and stops its pursuit until the flame expires or is extinguished. Collect Banishment Essence and distil a Banishment Flask at an Alchemist, then press B nearby. Permanent banishment awards 15,000 score and 250 XP, and drops a Banishment Artefact with its existing reward choice.

▣

DOORS, BRONZE KEYS & SECRETS

Bronze keys open optional bronze doors or standalone locked chests. If a chest is inside a room you already unlocked with a bronze key, that same key covers the room and the chest costs no second key. Enemies stay clear of unopened room entrances and cannot shoot into the waiting corridor. Cracked walls hide secrets; 5% of secret rooms conceal another cracked pocket.

★

LOOT, AMMO & XP

You begin with the Archive Sword and no firearm. Pickups now name the thing you actually collected: weapon caches evolve your current firearm instead of advertising random gun names, armour and XP show the amount gained, health is identified as a potion, and ammunition shows the rounds added. Firearms are found or bought, and each floor receives ammunition based on the enemies actually generated. At zero ammo ATTACK automatically returns to melee.

£

THE GAMBLER

A rare one-use Gambler can appear during an ordinary dungeon run. Stand beside him and press G to stake 1,000 score on his animated reel. One position pays 2,000 score, two positions reduce your health to 2 HP, and three positions simply take the stake.

⚠

DEATH & ZERO XP

Normal death moves half your score, the XP penalty and carried loot into one recoverable cache. Recover it before dying again; a second death permanently erases the old cache's non-essential contents, while essential keys or an Exit Sigil are returned safely to the floor and marked on the maps. You can lose at most one level and your most recent upgrade. The first death that leaves your total XP at zero gives a final warning. If any later death leaves total XP at zero a second time, the run ends—even if you recovered the earlier cache.

?

ENEMY TACTICS & OPTIONAL CHALLENGES

Enemies can reposition, flank and hide behind solid furniture. Tougher enemies assess cover more often and re-plan faster, but smarter tactics do not grant extra speed or damage. Player shots can smash ordinary furniture to open a route; enemy fire cannot destroy it. Clues, memory tiles, torch sequences, arenas, boulders, bridges and secret rooms offer extra score and stronger loot.

♨

WIND, SPIDERS & FIRELIGHT

About one corridor in twenty becomes a haunted windy passage. Its whistle warns of an adjoining webbed chamber crowded with fragile one-HP spiders. The wind snuffs an active torch with a pssst and visible gust, while fireplaces illuminate two rooms on every floor.

☠

HORDES & ARMOURED KNIGHTS

One low-health skeleton horde can rise on each floor and bursts into flying bones when defeated. Archive Knights carry heavy armour and high health: they cannot shoot, but their sword strike is dangerous at close range.

⚔

TREACHEROUS HAZARD ROOMS

Floors 1–2 contain one dedicated hazard room; floors 3–5 contain two. Timed blade galleries, ember floors and arrow halls signal their cycle before striking for 1 HP. Watch the pattern, move between safe tiles and do not rush the warning phase.

♥

LAST-RESORT RECOVERY

If every health pickup on the current floor has been consumed, an injured player regenerates 1 HP every two minutes. During a failed Sigil Lockdown, its gate reopens, surviving defenders reset and the death cache is placed outside the chamber so the challenge can be attempted again.
