## Architecture findings

- `v10-41-r59-live-regression-fixes.js` is the current final RAF owner. Before the stabilization clock candidate, visible active play called `update()` exactly once per accepted RAF with `dt=Math.min(45,gap)`. This discards all active wall time above 45 ms per rendered frame and is now a direct numerical cause of slow simulation under loaded/low-FPS Solo.
- The current Phase 3 candidate keeps R59 as the singleton RAF owner but gives only `dungeon-solo` bounded wall-time substeps: maximum 45 ms per substep, four substeps / 180 ms per rendered frame. It has no persistent debt accumulator, so pause/hidden time remains discarded at the accepted-RAF timestamp boundary. Horde, Spy and other controllers retain their established single-step path.
- `v10-41-mode-runtime.js` maintains mode lifecycle and owned-system gates from a separate 40 ms interval while also supplying the shared frame boundary.
- `v10-41-r30-owner-seal.js` checks normal movement ownership every 16 ms and can restore the golden `movePlayer` owner plus clear movement cooldowns.
- `v10-41-r30-global-movement-guard.js` maintains update/movement/damage ownership and movement-stall recovery on another 40 ms monitor.
- R60's Solo cadence wrapper calls R30 `adoptReleaseMoveOwner()`. Once accepted, R30 stores that exact function as `goldenMove`; `v10-41-r30-owner-seal.js` then protects/reasserts that function through both a property assignment gate and a 16 ms seal. The latest instrumented R60 Solo run showed this stack stable at four movement functions with one R60 cadence layer and no R30/seal repairs during the measured retry.
- `v10-41-r32-spy-loader.js` runs an always-on 20 ms monitor and calls `ensureR59()` globally; R59 then maintains its own 40 ms ownership interval.
- `v10-41-r57-desktop-prep-stability.js` retires R56's original periodic timer, then bridges `R56.installOwners()`, pending chest work and combat work from its own 80 ms tick.
- `v10-41-r56-playtest-completion.js` identifies its environmental damage owner only by the top-level `__ccgV141R56EnvironmentDamage` marker.
- `v10-41-r60-horde-combat-integrity.js` includes a Solo live-play addendum whose 40 ms installer currently identifies update, movement, start and environmental owners by top-level markers. `wrapEnvironmentalDamage()` does not inspect wrapper ancestry before installing another R60 layer.
- R60's environmental wrapper deliberately carries the R56 and R29 compatibility markers as well as its own R60 marker. That helps while R60 remains top-level, but does not make the ownership scheme bounded once another marker-neutral wrapper sits above it.
- The accelerated Solo soak is now executing in Canonical Load Safety. On `9ab948374...` it completed its baseline and 16 real pause/resume cycles, then failed the post-cycle active sample at a simulation/wall ratio of 0.3262. That is direct numerical reproduction and replaces the earlier state where upstream R60 tests prevented the soak from running.
- Ownership/watchdog monitors are not being broadly removed yet. Consolidation starts only after the bounded Solo clock candidate proves stable and its timing metrics identify what residual churn remains.

## Verification policy

A short CI smoke test is insufficient to close LS-SOLO-001 or LS-SOLO-003. Long-session timing defects require soak evidence. The target is sustained Solo simulation time approximately equal to active visible wall time, with pause/hidden time excluded and no later catch-up burst.
